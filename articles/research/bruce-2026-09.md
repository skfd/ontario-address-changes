# Research notes — County of Bruce, 2026-09

Raw findings behind `articles/researched/bruce-2026-09.md`. Notes, not prose.
Window researched: snapshots 2026-08-21 → 2026-09-30 (8 snapshots inside the month:
09-09, 09-11, 09-14, 09-17, 09-24, 09-25, 09-29, 09-30; 30 pull days, 0 failed).
Research done 2026-10-22.

Conventions: **FACT** = the linked page says it. **INFERENCE** = a reading, not the
source's. **SNIPPET** = seen only in a search-engine summary; the page itself could not
be fetched — do not print. **EMPTY** = searched, nothing found. Every claim carries a URL.

Source caveat: bruce publishes no stable row id. `ARN` is the MPAC assessment roll
number, one-to-many and re-keyed on the assessor's schedule, so `datasets/bruce.toml`
keeps it in props but synthesizes identity from number + street + unit + rounded
lon/lat. A point the County moves by more than ~1 m therefore gets a new key and the
tracker records one removal and one addition for the same address. **44 of this month's
124 additions and 44 of its 66 removals are that** (list in §0). The article says so and
attributes it to the tracker. Municipality names below are the file's `Municipality`
field; town names (Teeswater, Neyaashiinigmiing) appear only where a source gives them.
