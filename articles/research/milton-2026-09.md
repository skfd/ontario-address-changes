## 1. Tremaine Road → Gaetan Road and Carton Lane

- **FACT** Town notice of intention to rename "Tremaine Road to Gaetan Road and Carton
  Lane": objections in writing by **Mon 15 June 2026**, by-law to be considered by Council
  **Mon 22 June 2026, 7 p.m.**
  https://pub-milton.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=14967
  (also once at https://www.milton.ca/en/news/public-notice-renaming-of-tremaine-road-to-gaetan-road-and-carton-lane.aspx, which now 404s).
  Confidence: medium (search extract; fetch 403).
- **FACT** Why: Halton Region proposed that the newly built road be designated Tremaine
  Road, so the existing Tremaine Road north and south of Campbellville Road needed new
  names. April 13 2026 staff report proposed Troop Point (north of 401) and Gaetan Road
  (south of 401).
  https://calendar.milton.ca/meetings/Detail/2026-04-13-1900-Council-Meeting/990f9f81-4d4c-4b58-a15d-b43900de5527
  Confidence: medium (search extract).
- **FACT** May 11 2026 report: a written delegation (Robert Muller) at the April 13 meeting
  said a poll of affected owners preferred "Carton" over "Troop"; report proposed Carton
  north of the 401, Gaetan south. Report says Carton *Road*; the notice says Carton *Lane*.
  https://calendar.milton.ca/Meetings/Detail/2026-05-11-1900-Council-Meeting/cd31c88c-f3e0-4785-be37-b466010c876e
- **FACT** Milton Today: Council deferred in April after objections to Troop Point, then
  approved an amended motion: Carton Lane north of Highway 401, Gaetan Road for the
  southern portion. A councillor questioned "Road" since the northern section is a dead
  end. Carton honours M.J. Carton, who sat on Nassagaweya and Esquesing councils in the
  early 1900s.
  https://www.miltontoday.ca/local-news/milton-finalizes-tremaine-road-renaming-plan-12299759
  (also https://www.miltontoday.ca/local-news/milton-pauses-tremaine-road-renaming-plan-12157675)
  Confidence: medium (search extract; 403 to fetch).
- **FACT** Gaetan: chosen for local significance; "Gaetan land was donated to Conservation
  Halton by the founding members of the Italian Canadian Club" (April report, per extract).
  Confidence: medium-low; extract only.
- By-law number: an eSCRIBE listing of the 22 June meeting shows "055-2026 By-law renaming
  Tremaine Road to Gaetan Road and Carton Lane" (search extract only). **EMPTY** on the
  vote or minutes: nothing found confirms passage. The article says it was on the agenda,
  not that it passed.
- **EMPTY** on the 09-09 walk-back: nothing public on why 66 southern addresses were
  briefly Gaetan. The owner's flag note reads it as an over-applied rename narrowed to the
  by-law segment (INFERENCE, consistent with the report's north/south-of-Campbellville
  scope, but the record does not say so).
- Context **FACT**: Milton Now (8 Sep 2023): around 2000 Tremaine was extended to the 401
  and re-routed, and the bypassed section became Old Tremaine Road.
  https://www.miltonnow.ca/?p=113860 — matches 7586 Old Tremaine Road near 7580 Tremaine.
- Context: Milton Now on Halton's new four-lane Tremaine bridge over the 401 and the
  interchange "expected to open in December 2026": https://www.miltonnow.ca/?p=130219.
  Year of the bridge opening unclear in the extract. Not used.
