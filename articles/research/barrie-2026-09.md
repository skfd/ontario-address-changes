Raw findings behind `articles/researched/barrie-2026-09.md`. Notes, not prose.

Conventions: **FACT** = the linked page says it. **INFERENCE** = a reading, not the
source's. **SNIPPET** = seen only in a search-engine summary; the page itself could not
be fetched — do not print. **EMPTY** = searched, nothing found. Every claim carries a URL.

Source caveat: the September snapshots were pulled from the ArcGIS Online layer
COB_ADDRESS_POINT; the dataset was re-pointed to the licensed Open_Data/AddressVW view
on 2026-10-12 (datasets/barrie.toml, flags.toml). The attribution line names AddressVW.
Rows that read "present" in `lookup.py` are relative to the post-switch store; the
AddressVW view does not publish most *Pending* rows, so Pending addresses added in
September (203–209 Dock Rd) end on 2026-10-05 in the store. That is October's story.
