# Research notes — Regional Municipality of York, 2026-09

Raw findings behind `articles/researched/york-2026-09.md`. Notes, not prose.
Window researched: net 2026-08-26 → 2026-09-29; ten snapshots kept inside the month
(09-01, 03, 05, 09, 10, 12, 13, 24, 26, 29); 30 pull days, 0 failed. Research done 2026-10-23.

Conventions: **FACT** = the linked page says it (fetched directly). **SNIPPET** = seen only
in a search-result excerpt; the page was not opened (403/404), so not for print as fact.
**INFERENCE** = a reading, not the source's. **EMPTY** = searched, nothing found.

Source caveat: the layer is region-wide (Aurora, East Gwillimbury, Georgina, King, Markham,
Newmarket, Richmond Hill, Vaughan, Whitchurch-Stouffville). Identity is PNT_GIS_ID. A
re-addressing onto another street is issued by the Region as a *new* PNT_GIS_ID, so it shows
in the file as one retired + one added, not as a rename — that is the Region's record, not a
tracker re-key. PRO_DATE and PARID are in `ignore_fields`. No `location_min_move_m` floor is
set in `datasets/york.toml`, so 1–4 m nudges are counted in the location figure.

Flag queue: `python run.py flags` on 2026-10-23 shows 4 open flags (guelph, simcoe), none for
york. Brief has no HELD section. First York article; July and August are `[complete]`
(August net +7: 8 added, 1 removed, 8 renumbered).
