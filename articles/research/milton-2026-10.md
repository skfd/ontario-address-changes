## 1. ADU rules

- **FACT** Milton zoning allows up to three additional residential units on an urban
  residential lot with a detached, semi-detached, semi-link or townhouse, four units in
  all; Council amended the Official Plan and Zoning By-law; Ontario's Planning Act
  allows up to three units on an urban residential lot.
  https://milton.ca/en/business-and-development/understanding-additional-residential-units-arus-in-milton.aspx
  https://milton.ca/en/business-and-development/additional-residential-units.aspx
  Confidence: medium (search extract). A commenter puts the approval at June 2024 — not
  used, not from the Town.
- **FACT** (from September notes) ADU needs a building permit; applications online only.
  https://www.milton.ca/en/business-and-development/resources/Building/ARU/2025-ADU-Guide-and-sample-drawings---Jan.2026-Final.pdf
