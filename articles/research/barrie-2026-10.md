**Unit 1 additions — what the City says about additional units.**
FACT: Barrie By-law 2020-010, "The Additional Residential Units Registration By-law"
(office consolidation), https://www.barrie.ca/Additional-Residential-Units-Registration-Bylaw.pdf
— s.2.1 "No person shall operate a additional residential unit or permit the occupancy
of a additional residential unit unless such additional residential units has first been
registered within the City"; s.4.5 the Registrar "shall record the municipal address in
the Registry"; s.4.6 written notice goes to the owner, emergency service providers,
Building Services, Waste Management, utilities.
Definition 1.1(i): a self-contained unit "ancillary to, an existing dwelling … within the
principal building or within an accessory building".
INFERENCE ONLY: the Unit 1 records may be registered additional units. The file does not
say so. Do not print as fact.
barrie.ca/node/14898 ("Additional Residential Units – What you need to know"): 403 to
automated fetch. SNIPPET only for "registration required" pages node/9551, node/9460.
