# Twenty-one new Kawartha Lakes addresses are flagged as approved extra units

**191 Front Street West** in Bobcaygeon now has a 191B, and 195 next door has a 195B. They are
two of 21 new addresses in October that the City of Kawartha Lakes's file marks as approved
additional units, more than a third of the 57 it really gained. It was the file's first
change since 1 August.

## By the numbers

|---|---|

Fifty-six on a file of forty-four thousand is about one in 790, after a September in which
nothing changed at all.

## The B at 191 Front Street

The file has a field called `ARUApproved`, and 21 of the new addresses carry it. The City's
[guide to additional residential units](https://www.kawarthalakes.ca/media/u3qdmu5c/aru-guide-2023.pdf)
explains how they get there: every such unit must be registered under By-law 2020-162, and
"upon completion of registration, a municipal address will be assigned to the ARU." A lot can
hold up to two, one in the house and one in an accessory building, or just the one inside the
house on the Oak Ridges Moraine.

They are spread across the city: five in Lindsay, including 74B Melbourne Street West and
34B Maryknoll Avenue, two in Bobcaygeon, and the rest in villages and townships from 5B
Homestead Road in Pontypool to 88B Childs Road in Dalton. Most of the lettered points sit 7 to
44 metres from the plain number. Ten end in B, seven in D and one in C, and three have no
letter at all, such as 34 Dunbar Drive. Neither the file nor the guide says what the
letters mean, or whether a unit is in a basement or over a garage.

The flag is on 123 records now, up from 90 on 1 August. Twelve of the 33 extra are addresses
that were already there, such as 191 and 195 Front Street West themselves.

## Seven numbers on Sanderson Drive

The busiest street was Sanderson Drive in Lindsay, whose 62 sits 116 metres from 53 Debois
Street. The file gained 60, 62, 64, 66, 68, 112 and 116, filling a gap between 58 and 70 and the gaps around 110
and 114. The old 58 record became 62, and a new record took the 58.

The file lags the street here. **66 Sanderson Drive** was
[listed for rent on 8 January](https://property.ca/listings/x12677578) as a newly built Tribute
Communities townhome in "Lindsay Heights", and leased on the 28th. The tracker has read the
file since June, and no 66 was on it before the 3 October snapshot. Nothing public that could
be found says why the numbers came when they did.

## George Street crosses King

Nineteen addresses changed street name, and all three changes are about a direction, not a
new name.

In Omemee, 14, 15 and 17 went from George Street North to George Street South. All three sit
south of King Street East. That also ended a double: the file had held two 15 George Street
Norths, and the one north of King remains. It still holds two 13s, about 220 metres apart, and
the southern one is 34 metres from the new 15 George Street South. Also in Omemee, 49, 51 and
53 Church Street became Church Street East, as the street's other five already were.

In Lindsay, 13 addresses from 437 to 460 William Street gained a *North*, joining the 386
records that had it. **459 William Street** did not, and it is now the only one in Lindsay
without a direction, 88 metres from 450. No by-law or notice for any of this turned up.

## What the file did to itself

Six of the seven "retired" addresses never left. The City's record IDs moved instead: 22 Maple
Avenue, for one, gave up its ID to 1676 Peniel Road and took a new one, which on paper looks
like a 20-kilometre jump. Ten addresses were involved, and each stayed on its own point.

The one that truly went is **313 Fox Road** in Emily, whose point was identical to 312 Fox
Road's. 312 is still there. A retired record is not a demolition, and the public record is
silent on this one.

**20 Byrne Court** in Bexley became 4 Byrne Court at the same point, on a street whose other
numbers are 5, 8 and 12. The file does not say why.

Six points moved more than 100 metres, five of them in Emily: 124 Meadowview Road by 896 metres,
372 Hayes Line by 687, 884 St Luke's Road by 406, 801 Peace Road by 369 and 836 Hayes Line by
195. The sixth is 694 Highway 7A in Manvers, by 102. The file gives no reason for any of them.

counted here, and the dates above are when the file first showed a change, not when it was made.
