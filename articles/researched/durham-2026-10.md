# 3355 Elsa Storry Avenue had two records, and now it has one

Durham Region's address file ended October one record shorter, and no address left it. On
23 October one of two records for 3355 Elsa Storry Avenue in Pickering came off, and the
other stayed where it was. The same update nudged thirteen points nearby by three to five
metres. Nothing else changed.

One record out of a quarter of a million, and all of it on a single day.

## Two 3355s

Since the tracker's first read on 9 June, the file had carried 3355 Elsa Storry Avenue twice,
under two different asset ids, on two points 62 metres apart. One sat in sequence, six metres
from 3353 and six from 3357. The other sat up the street among the 3370s, nine metres from
3373 and ten from 3375, numbers that are all on file in their own right.

The one that came off was the second. Its id, 314293, also belongs to an older block: of the
111 records on Elsa Storry, only 3440 shares that block with it, and the rest run from
350568 up. The 3355 between 3353 and 3357 is untouched, and the Region's layer still shows
it with an edit date of July 2025.

Why the file held two, and why one left on 23 October, is not on record. Neither is who Elsa
Storry was: a search found no naming record for the street. Only two asset ids have left the
file since that first read, and this is the first with no replacement; the other, 89 Quiet
Waters Way in Port Perry, was reissued under a new id about two metres away on 18 June.

## Thirteen points, one edit

The other change sits 140 to 175 metres away: 3530 to 3542 Brickyard Drive, seven even
numbers, and 1728 to 1738 Mineral Springs Way, six more, two runs about 35 metres apart. Every
point moved north or a little west of north, by three to five metres, less than the six or
seven metres between neighbouring points on these rows. No number, name or other field changed. The Region's own edit date on
all thirteen reads 23 October, and no other record on the layer carries an edit date since
September's update.

Further along the same street, [condos.ca](https://condos.ca/pickering/1761-mineral-springs-way-pickering)
lists 1761 Mineral Springs Way as an attached townhouse in what it calls Rural Pickering.
[Mattamy's Seaton Whitevale page](https://mattamyhomes.com/ontario/gta/pickering/seaton-whitevale)
lists homes on Charcoal Crescent and Sugarcane Court, within about 300 metres, though not on
these two streets. Nothing public says why these points were moved.

## What to watch

September's two questions have answers. 3561 Sideline 34 is still 3561, and the undefined
state field did not read 1 on any record this month.

3355 is not the only double. Thirty-five other addresses, down to the unit and the town,
appear on more than one record, among them 3323 Salt Springs Drive, one street over, on
points seven metres apart. Some may be two real places: the two records for 1401 Brock Road
are 1.6 kilometres apart. The file does not say which, so next month's question is whether any
of them follows 3355 off it.
