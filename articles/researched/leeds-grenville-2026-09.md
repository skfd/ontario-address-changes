# A Prescott duplex becomes five apartments, and Merrickville Grove loses eight numbers

On 1 September, 235 James Street West in Prescott gained Units 1 through 5 in the United
Counties of Leeds and Grenville's address file. The building is on the market as a
"[turnkey legal 5-unit apartment building](https://www.rew.ca/properties/235-james-street-w-prescott-on)",
and the listing says it was "originally a duplex". The plain 235 is still on file beside
its five units.

## By the numbers

| | |
|---|---|
| New addresses | 44 |
| Retired | 9 |
| Renumbered | 0 in the tracker's count (several renumbered in place, see below) |
| Street renames | 0 |
| Address splits | 1 |

The file grew by 35 addresses, less than a tenth of one percent of the 54,142 it held
in early August. The counties republish their file about once a month, and all of this
arrived in a single update on 1 September. The other twenty-nine days of the month
brought the same file back each day.

## 235 James Street West

The five new units share the base address's roll number, so they are the same
property. The listing describes one three-bedroom suite, two two-bedrooms and two
one-bedrooms, and new kitchens and bathrooms throughout. It does not say when the
conversion was done, and no Town of Prescott permit or planning item for the address
turned up. The file records the five units arriving in September. When the building
was actually divided, the public record does not say.

Prescott had two more small clusters the same day. **130 Henry Street East** gained
four records labelled 130-A to 130-D, with the original 130 still on file. Four new
numbers, **240, 242, 244 and 246 Water Street West**, each arrived with a roll number of
its own but blank number and full-address fields. The numbers appeared only on their
labels and in the 911 address.
The counties filled the field in at the next update in October. Nothing public explains
either cluster.

## Eight numbers off St John Street

All nine retirements were records leaving the file, and eight of them came from one
place. **826, 828, 856, 858, 928, 932, 968 and 972 St John Street** in Merrickville were
retired in four pairs. All eight had been on file since the tracker began in June. None
had a roll number yet, and nothing was added in their place.

They sat in a long run of new numbers along St John Street and Wallace Street, and
the other 75 of those are still on file. That run is the land of Merrickville Grove,
Park View Homes' subdivision west of St. Lawrence Street. When the plan
[was first presented in 2019](https://ngtimes.ca/?p=22641), it would open an unbuilt
stretch of St John Street and extend Wallace Street to meet it. It proposed 67 two-storey
townhomes, 16 bungalow townhomes and a 15-unit apartment building. A year later
[council approved a revised draft plan](https://ngtimes.ca/?p=28196) with 99 units and
fewer bungalows. A townhome at 752 St John Street, which is still on file,
[is listed as sold](https://www.wahi.com/ca/en/real-estate/on/leeds-and-grenville/merrickville-wolford/752-st-john-st-merrickville-wolford-k0g1n0-ontario-canada)
as part of Merrickville Grove.

Why these eight numbers came off, the public record does not say. A retired address
is a record leaving the file, not a house coming down. The same day, a dozen of their neighbours on
St John and Wallace had their points nudged a few metres.

## Old Whaley Drive fills in, and the file untangles itself

Old Whaley Drive in North Grenville first appeared in the file in August, with 20
numbers close to County Road 20. In September it gained seven more: 100, 101, 103, 105,
107, 109 and 111. The street now has 27 numbers. A search turned up no street-naming
by-law, plan of subdivision or listing for it. The public record is silent on what is
being built there.

The seven numbers did not arrive in the usual way. In August, the seven record IDs they
now use carried copies of addresses that already existed elsewhere: 215, 217 and 219
Reuben Crescent in Kemptville, 855 Coyote Lane, 79 and 115 Benson Park Road, and 457
Lyndhurst Road. Meanwhile, the original records for most of those addresses were
missing. On 1 September each ID was given its Old Whaley Drive address, and the
originals came back. A record at 660 Veenstra Grove in Gananoque had spent August
reading 650 Pine Street, and it went back too. That is why 8 of September's 44 "new"
addresses were already on the August file. Five are records that had gone missing and
came back on their own IDs, and three were re-issued under new IDs. One came back with a
different number: 459A Lyndhurst Road returned as 457.

The same update also corrected a typo on all twenty of Old Whaley Drive's August
records, where the township had been spelled "Oxford on Rideau Two".

## The rest of the paperwork

Most of September's other changes were the record being tidied, much of it in Rideau
Lakes:

- Seven range addresses became single numbers, among them 476-528 Summers Road (now
  476) and 2416-2430 R24 (now 2430). In two cases the range broke into separate addresses:
  1158-1172 Davis Lock Road became 1158 and a new 1172, and 255-259 UB2 became 255, 257
  and 259.
- On Jones Court in Augusta, 20 became 23, 22 became 25 and 22A became 22, each keeping
  its roll number. A new 20 arrived without one. 5975 County Road 15 is now 5575.
- 13A Whitefish Lake Road became 13-A, and a handful of other suffixes gained hyphens.

The rest of the 44 are single new numbers, mostly on rural roads in Rideau Lakes and
North Grenville: Salem Road, Acton Corners Road, Chaffey's Lock Road and others. One of the
two records for 1 Snake Island was also retired. The other is still on file, about
16 km away.

*How this is measured*: The tracker reads the United Counties of Leeds and Grenville's
published civic-address layer every day and keeps a snapshot whenever the contents
differ from the last one kept. The counties update the layer about once a month, and
September 2026 produced one snapshot, on 1 September. The month's figures compare that
snapshot with the last one before the month (4 August), so changes made between 4
August and 1 September are counted here. The next update, on 6 October, belongs to
October. The source gives every record a stable ID, so an address renumbered in place
counts as a modification and not as a retirement plus an addition. The daily sum of
changes equals the month's net because there was only one change day. The tracker made
thirty daily reads and none failed. Each day's report is on the site linked below.
