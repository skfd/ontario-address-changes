# Twenty-nine Milton houses gain a second address

In October, 29 houses in the Town of Milton gained an extra address for an additional
dwelling unit, from "1303 Stirling Todd Terrace - ADU 1" on the 1st to six more on the
31st. One of them, 159 Martin Street, already had a first unit. Its "ADU 2" made it three
addresses on one lot. Two plain new numbers make up the rest, and nothing came off the file.

The file grew by 31 addresses, about one in fifteen hundred. Every change was an addition
that stayed. The day-by-day count and the month's outcome are the same 31.

## Second units, one house at a time

An additional dwelling unit, or ADU, is a separate home on the same lot as a house: a
basement apartment, a unit over a garage, a suite out back. In Milton's address file it
gets the house's number with a suffix. So "845 Hepburn Road - ADU 1" sits beside the plain
845 Hepburn Road, which has been on the file since the tracker began in June. That holds
for all 29. Each house was already there, and each unit has its own record number.

The 29 are spread across town rather than clustered. Two streets got two each, but on
different houses and different days: 845 Hepburn Road on 4 October and 619 Hepburn on the
9th, and 781 Whitlock Avenue on the 4th and 1439 Whitlock on the 31st. By ward, 14 were in
Ward 3, 10 in Ward 4, 3 in Ward 2 and 2 in Ward 1. The busiest day was 4 October, with
seven, from 401 Baylis Court to 812 Apple Terrace.

The file held 2,188 ADU addresses at the end of the month, up from 2,159. That is one in
every 21 addresses in Milton.

## Three addresses at 159 Martin Street

159 Martin Street and "159 Martin Street - ADU 1" have both been on the file since June.
On 21 October an "ADU 2" joined them. Milton's zoning
[allows up to three additional units](https://milton.ca/en/business-and-development/understanding-additional-residential-units-arus-in-milton.aspx)
on an urban lot with a detached, semi-detached or townhouse, for four homes in all, and
each unit
[needs a building permit](https://www.milton.ca/en/business-and-development/resources/Building/ARU/2025-ADU-Guide-and-sample-drawings---Jan.2026-Final.pdf).
The file does not say at which step of that permit the address is issued.

## Two plain numbers

6554 Tremaine Road arrived on 6 October, about 200 metres from 458 McJannett Avenue and
545 Dymott Avenue. Its address record number, 19883, is an old one; the month's other
new records are all numbered above 49,000. It had not appeared on the file before
under any street, and the public record is silent on why it appears now.

13335 Britannia Road arrived on the 30th as a bare point: a number, a street and nothing
else. It has no full address line, ward or property record. Its nearest numbered
neighbour is 13520 Britannia Road, 256 metres away. Nothing public mentions the address.

After September's Tremaine Road renaming, October was quiet. The file is a record of
paperwork completing, and this month the paperwork was almost entirely second units.
