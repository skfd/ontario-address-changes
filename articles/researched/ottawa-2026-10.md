# Farebox Way, Monorail Road and three more Stittsville streets arrive in one day

On 22 October 2026 Ottawa's address file gained five streets it had never held, all
in one block just south of Hazeldean Road in Stittsville: **Farebox Way** (75
addresses), **Cable Car Crescent** (42), **Whistle Post Way** (35), **Monorail Road** (11) and
**Stationhouse Walk** (1). Backbend Terrace and Cranesbill Road got longer the same day.
Those 199 addresses are close to two in five of everything the City added in October.

About one address in 780 on the file at month's end was new in October, fewer than half
September's 1,161. Three days account for most of them: 160 on the 2nd, 59 on the 9th and
201 on the 22nd.

## A train's name, and a monorail

100 Monorail Road is 109 metres from 5618 Hazeldean Road, the address on the plan of
subdivision for Minto's Abbott's Run community. The
[Ottawa Business Journal](https://obj.ca/new-minto-community-in-stittsville-selling-september-9/)
placed it "north of Abbott Street, south of Hazeldean Road, and east of Iber Road" when it
went on sale in 2023, and explained the name: "an ode to the first steam train to pass
through Stittsville, the H.H. Abbott in 1870." That line closed in 1990 and its bed is now
the [Trans Canada Trail along Abbott Street East](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stittsville).
That plan,
[D07-16-16-0020](https://ottwatch.ca/devapp/D07-16-16-0020), covers 89 hectares.

So a community named for a steam train now has a Whistle Post Way and a Stationhouse Walk.
It also has a Farebox Way, a Cable Car Crescent and a Monorail Road, which are words from
transit rather than from 1870. Whether all five belong to one theme, and who picked them,
the public record does not say: no naming report turned up for any of them, and nothing
public says which phase of Abbott's Run these streets are in.

## 160 addresses in Richmond Meadows

Every address added on 2 October was in one patch of Richmond, off Meynell Road: 64 on
**Homecraft Way**, 42 on **Coltsfoot Crescent**, a street new to the file, 28 more on Showman
Street and 26 on Royal York Street West. 501 Homecraft Way is 63 metres from 384 Meynell
Road. A listing places Homecraft in
[Mattamy's Richmond Meadows](https://allthingshome.ca/new-homes/developments/richmond-meadows-richmond),
about 1,100 homes planned at Perth Street and Meynell Road, and the same neighbourhood
where September's Patchwork Circle became Patchwork Place.

One small mismatch: a
[Mattamy listing in Richmond Meadows](https://condos.ca/listings/x13626882) sells a lot on
"Homecraft Street", with possession in April 2027. The City's file says Homecraft *Way*.
Where the names Coltsfoot, Homecraft and Showman came from, nothing public says.

## Greely: 39 numbers on a subdivision that is for sale

On 9 October **West Beach Way** in Greely gained 39 addresses, 1300 to 1438, on a street
that had held seven. The same day the file moved the point for 1240 Old Prescott Road about
280 metres west-southwest, keeping its number; it now sits 67 metres from 1324 West Beach
Way.

The two belong together. A
[2021 committee report](https://pub-ottawa.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?documentid=80267)
describes a subdivision at 1240 Old Prescott, "on the west side of Old Prescott between
Mitch Owens and Parkway Roads": "37 residential lots, the continuation of West Beach Way to
Old Prescott Road, a park, a path block, an open space block and a stormwater management
pond." The file, [D07-16-13-0032](https://ottwatch.ca/devapp/D07-16-13-0032), was opened
in 2013 for 45 lots, and the
[planning rationale](https://webcast.ottawa.ca/plan/All_Image%20Referencing_Zoning%20Bylaw%20Amendment%20Application_Image%20Reference_2021-07-21%20D02-02-13-0034%20Planning%20Rationale.PDF)
records it cut to 37 and draft-approved on 7 May 2021.

The land is on the market. A
[commercial listing](https://www.commercialcafe.com/commercial-property/us/on/greely/1240-old-prescott-road-greely/)
updated on 6 November offers "a draft plan-approved 37-lot subdivision on 47 acres". So the
numbers arrived while the land is still being marketed as a draft-approved subdivision. The file has 39 new numbers
where the plan has 37 lots, and it does not say what the other two are for.

## Eight townhouses on Innes Road, three numbers each

On 29 October, 23 addresses arrived at 2651 to 2665 Innes Road in the east end, 81 metres
from 101 Bearbrook Road: seven plain odd numbers, each with a **B** and a **C**, plus a B
and a C beside 2663, which has been on file all along. In May 2025 the Committee of
Adjustment
[considered a request](https://ottwatch.ca/meeting/6fb3ebba-06cd-4a46-a3c5-cb4eb045419b)
at 2663 Innes "to subdivide the property into five separate parcels of land for the
construction of eight townhouse units", and the page shows it granted. Eight townhouses,
eight odd numbers. An
[earlier plan](https://ottwatch.ca/devapp/D07-12-22-0182) for a four-storey building with
18 apartments on the site went inactive in 2023. What the B's and C's are, no permit or
application found says.

## A semi on Westhaven, and a plaza on Merivale

259, 261 and 261 B Westhaven Crescent, 36 metres from 200 Clare Street, left the file on 24 October, five days
after *259 2 A* and *259 2 B* arrived. In May the City
[issued a permit](https://open.ottawa.ca/datasets/construction-demolition-and-pool-permits-2026)
(CON-2025-015695) to "construct a 2 storey semi-detached dwelling with 1 additional
dwelling unit in each (basement)", listing 259 A, 259 B, 261 B and 261. The permit's four
labels and the file's do not yet line up.

On 10 October, 1653, 1657, 1663 and 1665 Merivale Road came off the file. 1649, 1651 and
1669 stay, and all seven numbers share a single map point:
[RioCan Merivale Place](https://riocan.propertycapsule.com/p/retail-real-estate/Ottawa-ON-K2G+3K2/riocanmerivaleplace),
the plaza at Meadowland Drive. Why four of its numbers went, the public record is silent.
The other retirements were pairs and singles: 3054 and 3080 Navan Road, 5494 and 5510
Boundary Road, 2883 and 2885 Riverside Drive.

## What the file did to itself

433 addresses switched between the layer's two kinds, *MAIN* and *SUBORD*: 407 from
subordinate to main and 9 the other way, plus 17 that changed kind and moved at the same
time. The biggest group, on 2 October, was six streets side by side in the west end:
58 on Weecay Circle, 52 on Beet Root Crescent, 41 on Alembic Crescent, then Linseed Road,
Calendula Place and Leone Farrell Street. All six had been on file since June. Meanwhile 505
of October's 517 new addresses came in as subordinate. The
[layer](https://maps.ottawa.ca/arcgis/rest/services/Municipal_Address/MapServer/0) still
does not define the two values.

On the 29th, thirteen points downtown, 443 to 461 Sussex Drive and 6, 18 and
22 Murray Street, each moved 30 to 39 metres, keeping their numbers. They are on the
National Capital Commission's heritage block between Murray and Clarence,
[restored after 2015](https://heritageottawa.org/en/news/ncc-unveils-construction-plans-7-clarence-street-and-heritage-properties-sussex).
Nothing public from 2026 says why the points moved.

## What to watch

September's watch list stays open: Spumante Street's 86 addresses in Riverside South and
Hyacinth Way's 34 in Cardinal Creek are still all subordinate, and Patchwork is still a
Place. Add
two more: whether Homecraft becomes a Street, and whether West Beach Way's 39 numbers stay
on the file while 1240 Old Prescott is for sale.
