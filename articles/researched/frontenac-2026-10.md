# Two Frontenac addresses took a letter, and only one address really left

On 8 October, 1038 Pranger Court in Central Frontenac became 1038A, and a new 1038B
appeared 89 metres away. Eleven days later the same thing happened in South Frontenac:
184 Burridge Lake Road became 184B, with a new 184A 24 metres from it.

The file grew by 11 addresses, about one in every two thousand, after growing by 16 in
September.

## One number, two letters

In both cases the original point stayed exactly where it was and only its label changed.
The letters went opposite ways. On Pranger Court the old address became the A and the
new point the B; on Burridge Lake Road the old address became the B and the new point the
A.

Pranger Court already had 1026A through 1026D, so every number on the court now carries a
letter. The nearest address on another street to 1038B is 1038C Pleasant Hill Lane, 42
metres off.

The file does not say what stands at any of these points, and a search found no listing,
notice or news item for either address. South Frontenac's
[public notices](https://www.southfrontenac.net/news/) from 21 August to 2 November
include about 20 planning items, none of them on Burridge Lake Road.

## The one that left

57 Oak Shores Crescent in South Frontenac was last seen on 13 October, with no replacement
in the file. 55 and 59 are still there on either side. A retired address is a record
leaving the file, which is not the same as a building coming down, and the public record
is silent on which this was.

The other three retirements were not addresses leaving:

- **1033 Primrose Lane** is the correction September's piece was waiting for. Its full
  address has said Primrose Lane since 28 September while its street field said Price
  Road; by 19 October the street field agreed. The point did not move, so what looks
  like a new street here is one row being fixed.
- **8078 Road 509** in North Frontenac moved 90 metres by 7 October.
- **1585 Leggat Lake Road** in Central Frontenac shifted about two metres on 8 October,
  the day a new 1575 arrived 57 metres away.

## The twelve that are new

Besides 1038B, 184A and 1575 Leggat Lake Road, nine single numbers joined established
roads. Central Frontenac took seven of the twelve, including 949 Crow Lake Road and 1146
Steiger Road, the last arrival on 31 October. South Frontenac took three, among them 2171
Keeley Road and 1127 Beach Lane. North Frontenac took two, 1060 Turkey Lane and 1170
Hamilton Hills Lane. Frontenac Islands had none.

## What the file itself did

On 8 October, 18 labels closed the gap between number and letter, so 1050 A Penyck Lake
Lane became 1050A and 5112 A Battersea Road became 5112A. No address of that form remains.
Nothing else about those rows changed.
