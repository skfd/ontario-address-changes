# Patrick Drive in Frankford gets ten blocks of four numbers in one day

On 25 September 2026 the City of Quinte West's address file gained twelve points on
Patrick Drive in Frankford. Most are not single numbers: they are ranges like 47-53 and
98-104, each covering four numbers on one side of the street. One of those blocks, 79-85,
is listed for sale as a serviced parcel zoned for townhouses, with a concept of four
townhomes on it. Elsewhere in the month, 17294 Telephone Road became units A and B.

## Patrick Drive

Before September, Patrick Drive ran from 6 to 43 in single numbers, all on file since
the tracker's first read in June. On the 25th the file added 45, then six ranges on the
odd side (47-53, 55-61, 63-69, 71-77, 79-85, 87-93) and four on the even side (74-80,
82-88, 90-96, 98-104). It also added one point carrying the street name and no number at
all, near the north end, 55 metres from 26 Diamond Street. The new points sit a few
hundred metres from South Trent Street, with March Street and Brittany Lane the nearest
other streets.

The file records a range as a single point and does not say what it stands for. A
listing for 79-85 Patrick Drive does: "Block 2, a fully serviced residential development
parcel" of about 0.43 acres, zoned Residential Type 3 Exception 23 (R3-23), "permitting
townhouse dwellings", with a preliminary concept of four townhomes and two adjacent
blocks on offer for a concept of twelve. It calls the land "ready for building permit
applications" ([MLS X13863234](https://www.greatertorontohomepros.com/property-search/detail/211/X13863234/79-85-patrick-dr-quinte-west-on-k0k-2c0/)).
That is a realtor's description rather than a City record, and it covers three blocks
where the file issued ten. No City notice, by-law or agenda item for the rezoning could
be found, so the public record is silent on when it was approved and on what the other
ranges are for. If the listing is right that the block is still waiting on building
permits, the numbers have arrived ahead of the houses.

## 17294 Telephone Road becomes A and B

On 10 September the plain 17294 Telephone Road, in the Murray ward, came off the file
and 17294 Telephone Road units A and B arrived the same day, one of them about ten
metres from where the old point stood. Nothing public was found on the property, so the
file's record is all there is: one address became two.

## The rest of the month

Four single numbers arrived on rural roads: 1648 Frankford-Stirling Road (10 September),
151 Rose Road (16 September), and 329 Preston Hill Road and 44 Will Johnson Road
(19 September). Searches for each found nothing.

Two of the retirements are not addresses leaving. 205 Sagers Corners Road was retired
and re-added on 10 September with its point moved about 40 metres. 396 Deer Run Road
had carried 396 on its label but 406 in its street-number field; on the 19th that row
left, and on the 25th a row with 396 in both came back at exactly the same spot. The
tracker identifies Quinte West's rows by number, street, unit and position, because the
file has no permanent record number, so either kind of correction shows up as one
address out and one in.
