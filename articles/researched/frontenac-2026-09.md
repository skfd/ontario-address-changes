# A new lane off Road 38, and three addresses that kept their spot but changed their name

On 23 September the address 5758 Road 38 in South Frontenac left the County of
Frontenac's address file, and on the same day 125 Inspiration Lane appeared at exactly
the same point. Five days later four more numbers joined it — 107, 109, 111 and 126
Inspiration Lane — a street the file had never carried before.

The file grew by 16 addresses, or less than a tenth of one percent of the 22,381 it held
in mid-August. Every one of the four retirements has a matching addition, which is
why nothing shows as renumbered even though three points took a new address without
moving.

## Inspiration Lane

The five Inspiration Lane points sit in a line about 200 metres long: the nearest
addresses on other streets are 5768 Road 38, 66 metres from 107 Inspiration Lane, and
4789 Holleford Road, 127 metres away. One of the five, 125, is the old 5758 Road
38, given a new street name without moving an inch.

The file shows the change was made in two steps. When 125 Inspiration Lane first
appeared on the 23rd, its full address read Inspiration Lane but its street field still
said Road 38; on the 28th the street field was corrected and the four neighbours
arrived with it.

Why the lane exists, and who named it, the public record does not say. A search for
Inspiration Lane in South Frontenac turns up no township notice, by-law or news item.

## Two more addresses that stayed put

The same thing happened twice elsewhere in the county, with no new street involved:

- **1148 Nine Mile Point Road** in Frontenac Islands left the file after 4 September,
  and on the 22nd **63 Lucas Point Lane** appeared at the identical coordinates. Lucas
  Point Lane is not new; it has carried seven other addresses since the tracker began in
  June.
- **1213 Cronk Road** in Central Frontenac was last seen on 18 August, and on the first
  September snapshot, 2 September, **1179 Cronk Road** stood on the same spot.

A third address, **122 Fuller Road West** in Frontenac Islands, kept its number and
street but its point moved 63 metres. Because the file publishes no permanent ID for its
records, the tracker sees every one of these as one address leaving and another
arriving. The file says nothing about why any of them changed, and nothing public turned
up.

## The sixteen that are new

That leaves 16 points with no predecessor. Ten are in South Frontenac: the four on
Inspiration Lane, and single numbers on Old Thirteen Island Lake Road, Ellerbeck Road,
Twelfth Concession Road, Stage Coach Road, Hall Lane and a Primrose Lane. Three are
in Central Frontenac, among them 2493 and 2503 Crow Lake Road, which arrived together.
Three are in Frontenac Islands, on 16th Line Road, Sunrise Lane and Reed's Bay Road.
North Frontenac had none.

1033 Primrose Lane arrived with the same mismatch as 125 Inspiration Lane: the full
address said Primrose Lane, the street field said Price Road. That one was not
corrected until 19 October, so it belongs to next month's file.

## What the file itself did

On 24 and 25 September six addresses had a stray double space taken out of their full
address — 1141 Nine Mile Point Road and 1334 Howe Island Drive among them. Nothing
else about those addresses changed; someone was tidying the record.
