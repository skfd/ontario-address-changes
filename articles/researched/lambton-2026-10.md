# Twenty-two new numbers on one corner of Brigden, and a former Sarnia church reaches the county's file

On 1 October 2026 the County of Lambton's address file gained 22 numbers on one small
block in the Township of St. Clair: fifteen on **Boswell Street** and seven on **Melwood
Drive**, side by side. That is most of the month. The rest is a scattering of single
numbers, among them 220 George Street in Sarnia, the former Central United Church.

The tracker's raw count is 37 in and 6 out, because a point that moves is counted as one
of each; the five moves are explained below. The 31 is about 0.05% of the file, well
under September's 87.

## Boswell Street meets Melwood Drive

The new numbers are 2465 to 2485 Boswell Street, on both sides of the street from 2472 to
2478, and 1571 to 1576 and 1578 Melwood Drive. They carry on from 2464 and 2468 Boswell and
1570 Melwood, all already on file, and the two runs end together: 1578 Melwood is 21
metres from 2485 Boswell. The nearest other streets are Courtright Line, 53 metres from
2465 Boswell, and Jane Street, 101 metres.

All 22 show 30 September in the field the file labels *approved*, within about a quarter
of an hour.

The file does not say who is building here, or whether anyone is yet. A 2025 listing for
1552 Melwood, further up the street, [describes](https://www.zoocasa.com/saint-clair-on-real-estate/1552-melwood-dr)
a "newly built" home "on a quiet dead-end street in the heart of Brigden". Beyond that the
public record is silent: no builder, subdivision name or lot count turned up, and St.
Clair's council highlights for June, August and October name neither street.

## 220 and 373 George Street

The month's last two rows came on 29 October, both in Sarnia. Neither is new to Sarnia.
The City's own address file has listed 220 and 373 George Street since the tracker first
read it in June. The county's file simply had not carried them.

220 George is the former Central United Church. In January, [Sarnia News Today reported](https://sarnianewstoday.ca/sarnia/news/2026/01/20/denied-heritage-request-paves-way-for-new-ownershipof-former-central-united-church)
that council had taken no further action on a heritage designation, clearing a sale to
Heikal Group Inc., which proposed a daycare in part of the building and "residential
intensification" on the rest. The sale was expected to close in March; nothing found
confirms that it did, and nothing ties the county's new point to those plans. On 373 George
the record is silent.

## A lot in Southcott Pines

**10296 Grand Oaks Drive** arrived on 15 October, 56 metres from 10157 Sumac Court. The
number has a paper trail of a sort: it was [listed in April as vacant land](https://houseindex.ca/mls/listing/lambton-shores/10296-grand-oaks-drive/X12996956)
at Grand Oaks and Sumac Court, and the listing now shows it sold at $429,000.

The other new numbers are 32 Alberta Street and 86 Main Street West in Lambton Shores, 1708
and 1709 Bickford Line in St. Clair, 3172 Churchill Line in Plympton-Wyoming and 7024
Wisbeach Road in Warwick. Nothing public explains any of them.

## What the file itself did

Five addresses moved, and the tracker recognizes an address partly by its position, so
each one looks like a departure and an arrival. 34 Alberta Street in Lambton Shores moved
30 metres on 2 October. On 15 October, 10026, 10030 and 10038 McCordic Lane moved 32, 48
and 71 metres, and 7429 Mud Creek Trail about a metre. Four of the five went from a point type of
*Unknown* to *Driveway Entrance*. The file does not say why any of them moved.

The month's one retirement came the same day. 7433 Mud Creek Trail left the file on
15 October, and 10034 McCordic Lane, a number never on file before, arrived 55 metres
away. Every other new number this month carries a fresh *approved* date. 10034
carries 17 February, the date on the older points around it. It may be the same point
under a new address. The file does not link them, and nothing public says.

## Next month

The file held still for the last two days of October. It had gained three more rows by
6 November, which belong to November's piece.
