# Two new six-plexes, one in Merrickville and one in Brockville, get their unit numbers

On 6 October, 114 Lewis Street West in Merrickville gained Units 1 through 6 in the
United Counties of Leeds and Grenville's address file. A rental listing calls the building
a "[brand-new, purpose-built 6-plex in the heart of Merrickville](https://teamrealty.ca/listing/114-lewis-street-w-merrickville-wolford-ontario-x13577856/)".
The same day, 12 Scace Avenue in Brockville became six units too.

The file grew by 74 addresses, about a seventh of one percent. As in September, all of
it came in one republication by the counties, on 6 October. The other twenty-nine reads
in the month brought back the same file.

## Six units on Lewis Street, six on Scace Avenue

The six Lewis Street units share the roll number of the plain 114, which is still on
file beside them. An earlier listing
[offered the lot as vacant land](https://listo.ca/house-for-sale/leeds-and-grenville-real-estate/X11951185/114-lewis-st-w-804-merrickville-merrickville-wolford-ontario-K0G1N0),
with 60 feet of frontage. It sits a block from St John Street, 35 metres
from 411 St John.

12 Scace Avenue took a different route into the file. The address was not joined by six
units. It *became* Unit 1, keeping its record, and Units 2 to 6 arrived beside it on the
same roll number. A sale listing describes a
"[brand-new, purpose-built 6-unit multiplex](https://teamrealty.ca/listing/12-scace-avenue-brockville-ontario-x13698000/)"
in Brockville's east end. The same thing happened at 787 Colonel Curry Drive, also in
Brockville: the plain 787 became Unit 1 and a Unit 2 joined it.

In Prescott, **191 King Street West** arrived as Units 1 to 4, near Centre Street. No plain
191 King Street West has been on the Prescott file since the tracker began in June. A
current rental listing for Unit 3 describes a
"[fully renovated 1-bedroom apartment](https://www.rew.ca/rentals/7470410/3-191-king-street-w-prescott-on)"
in "a landmark building". It does not say how many units the building has, or when they
were made.

## Four numbers 54 metres from Lockett Place

Also in Prescott, **371, 373, 375 and 377 James Street West** are new, none with a roll
number yet. They are 54 metres from 360 Dibble Street West. That is the address
Habitat for Humanity Thousand Islands gave for
[Lockett Place](https://www.brockvilledaily.ca/news/top-3-stories-in-brockville/prescott-just-broke-ground-on-a-new-affordable-housing-site-and-its-named-after-a-beloved-local-leader/),
four semi-detached homes behind Town Hall, named for former councillor Ruth Lockett.
Ground was broken there on 5 July 2025. Four homes there, four numbers here. But the
article does not mention James Street, and nothing public says these are the same homes.

## 503 James becomes 495 Jones in Kemptville

In Kemptville, the record for 503 James Street was renamed and renumbered **495 Jones
Street**, and its point moved about 50 metres. A new **485 Jones Street** appeared about 15
metres from where the old point had been, on the same roll number. A 503 Jones Street was
already on file, so the old number could not come along. The record's 911 address still
reads "James Street". The public record is silent on why.

Up County Road 43, the range 2960-2968 became plain 2960, and 2958, 2962, 2964 and 2966
arrived beside it on the same roll number. Nothing public explains those either.

## The rest of the paperwork

- Four more addresses gained a letter while a new sibling arrived beside them. 151 Berry
  Road became 151A next to a new 151B, and 854 Olajos Lane became 854A next to 854B. 108
  Main Street in Leeds and the Thousand Islands and 45 King Street in Rideau Lakes went
  the same way.
- The four Water Street West numbers in Prescott that arrived blank in September had
  their number fields filled in. Those are four of the month's five renumberings.
- The one retirement was **106 Grenville Way** in Merrickville, on file since June. A
  search found nothing on it. A retired address is a record leaving the file, not a
  building coming down.
- Twelve points moved 10 to 111 metres, mostly on Rideau Lakes waterfront lanes.

*How this is measured*: The tracker reads the counties' published civic-address layer
every day and keeps a snapshot whenever the contents differ from the last one kept.
October 2026 produced one snapshot, on 6 October. The month's figures compare it with
the last snapshot before the month (1 September), so changes made between 1 September
and 6 October are counted here. The source gives every record a stable ID, so an address
renumbered in place counts as a modification and not as a retirement plus an addition.
With only one change day, the daily sum of changes equals the month's net. The tracker
made thirty daily reads and none failed. Each day's report is on the site linked below.
