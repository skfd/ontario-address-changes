# A Skeleton Lake property gets a house, a marina building and five units, and fourteen Huntsville lots get their own roll numbers

On 16 September, seven new records appeared at **1291 Skeleton Lake 2 Road** in Muskoka
Lakes, all on one property. Five are numbered units. The other two are labelled, in the
District's own words, "HOUSE" and "Marina Bldg". It was the first change to Muskoka's
address file in September. Until that day the file had not moved since 29 August.

The file grew by 27 addresses, about one in 2,500 of the 66,471 it held at the end of
August. Of the 30 real arrivals, 23 are in the Township of Muskoka Lakes.

## 1291 Skeleton Lake 2 Road

No plain 1291 has ever been on the file. The property has always appeared as numbered
units: Unit 1 and a run of even units from 4 to 48. The higher units each carry a roll
number of their own. The seven new records all share Unit 1's roll number and are
numbered under it: "1 01" to "1 05", "1 HOUSE" and "1 Marina Bldg". They are spread over
about 110 metres. Unit 1 itself was nudged about 19 metres the same day.

The file names a marina building but not the business. A search for the address found
nothing. Listings and the
[lake association's newsletter](https://skeletonlake.ca/news/7587477) mention a Skeleton
Lake Marina, but nothing found gives it this address. The public record is silent on what
was built or registered here.

## 1180 Woodroffe Road

The month's other split is on Woodroffe Road in Muskoka Lakes. The nearest address on
another street is 8126 Lake Joseph Road, 253 metres away. **1180 Woodroffe Road** gained
Units A to F on the 16th. They sit in a row about 75 metres long, some 55 metres north of
the original point, and all six share its roll number. 1180 is still on file beside them.
A search turned up nothing for the address.

## Mackenzie Drive: same addresses, new roll numbers

Fourteen of the "new" addresses are not new. Until 29 August, 30 to 48 Mackenzie Drive in
Huntsville, near Greaves Avenue and Yonge Street South, shared one assessment roll number.
On 16 September each of the fourteen got a roll number of its own, in an unbroken run.
The numbers and the points stayed where they were.

The tracker identifies a Muskoka address by its number, street, unit and roll number,
because the District's coordinates wobble by up to a metre and a half between
publications. So a new roll number looks like one address leaving and another arriving.
That is where 14 of the month's 17 retirements come from. Nothing public turned up about
the street, so the record is silent on why the roll was divided.

## The rest of the month

Two more retirements are the same kind of bookkeeping. **1047 Mahoney Lane** in
Bracebridge became **1049** on the same roll number, four metres away. At **1633 Walkers
Point Road** in Muskoka Lakes, the old record's roll number now belongs to "1633 Unit 1",
about 80 metres south. A new plain 1633 on a different roll number sits beside it. The
third retirement was Unit 0 at 1102 Woodroffe Road; units 2 to 12 there are still on file.

The other new numbers were scattered single addresses: 1608 and 1652 Paint Lake Road and
3704 Muskoka Road 117 in Lake of Bays, 1011 and 1013 Currie Street in Muskoka Lakes, and
1090 Jones Road in Gravenhurst, among others. Nothing public turned up for any of them.

The District also moved 87 existing points by two metres or more, 25 of them by more than
50. The largest moves were at 1032 Redwood Road in Muskoka Lakes, where three units shifted
between 181 and 361 metres. The file records the new positions, not the reason.

---
*How this is measured*: The tracker reads the District Municipality of Muskoka's
published address layer every day and keeps a snapshot whenever the contents differ from
the last one kept. September 2026 produced eight snapshots, from 16 to 27 September. The
month's figures compare the 27 September snapshot with the last one before the month
(29 August), so changes after 27 September belong to October. Because the tracker keys
addresses on number, street, unit and roll number, a renumbering or a new roll number
counts as one retirement plus one addition. Moves under two metres are treated as the
service's republishing wobble and ignored. Added and retired counts are the same summed
day by day as over the month. Location edits summed to 95 against 87 net, because a few
points moved more than once. The tracker made thirty daily reads and none failed. Each
day's report is on the site linked below.
