# 1239 Centennial Drive gets 87 unit numbers, and 3 Montreal Street appears beside 163 Brock

On 3 October, 87 unit numbers arrived at 1239 Centennial Drive in one update, running
from 101 to 615 with five marked LL1. That one address is nearly two-thirds of everything
new in the City of Kingston's address file this month. Nothing public names it.

## By the numbers

| | |
|---|---|
| New addresses | 134 (87 of them at 1239 Centennial Drive) |
| Retired | 12 (only 2 left with nothing on or beside the same spot) |
| Renumbered | 0 |
| Street renames | 0 |
| Address splits | 19 |
| Total on file at month end | 77,705 |

The file grew by 122 addresses, about a sixth of one percent, up from 77 in September. No
new street appeared.

## 1239 Centennial Drive

The plain address has been on file since the tracker began in June. The new units run
101 to 107, then 15 to a floor from 201 to 615, plus LL1-1 to LL1-5. Their record numbers
are older than records that were already public in June, so they appear to have been
waiting some time before reaching the published file.

Next door, 1245 Centennial Drive has carried the same numbering since June: 101 to 107,
then 15 a floor up to 615, which makes 82 units. That address is
[The Grove](https://cityflats.ca/assets/the-grove/), which its owner, CityFlats, describes
as a 5.4-acre site at Centennial and Cataraqui Woods Drive rezoned for five apartment
buildings. CityFlats totals them at 597 units;
[insauga reported 410](https://www.insauga.com/new-rental-community-in-kingston-ontario-selects-property-management-firm/)
in January, and neither explains the other. The first building, The Cedar, was due for
occupancy in February 2027, and
[ForRent lists](https://www.forrent.com/on/kingston/the-grove/r6f99w0) The Grove at 82 units.
CityFlats expected construction of a second building to begin in the third quarter of 2026.

None of those sources mentions 1239. What the file shows is that the two points are about
64 metres apart, share the postal code K7P 0E9, and have consecutive record numbers
(107369 and 107370). It does not say what stands at 1239 today.

## 3 Montreal Street

Units 1, 2 and 3 of 163 Brock Street were last on file on 8 October. On the 9th, a plain
3 Montreal Street and its Units 1 to 4 appeared about 10 metres from where those units had
stood. No 3 Montreal Street had been on file before. The plain 163 Brock is still there.

A [2025 sale listing](https://wahi.com/ca/en/real-estate/on/frontenac/kingston/sydenham/163-brock-st-kingston-k7l1s2-ontario)
describes 163 Brock as a medical clinic and four residential apartments, opposite Hotel
Dieu Hospital, with the seller leasing back until 30 June 2026. The place and the count
match. Nothing public we found says the apartments took a Montreal Street address, or why.

The labels did not hold at first. On 10 October the four units lost them, leaving five
identical "3 Montreal St" records, and on the 17th they came back.

## Twelve houses gain Units 1 and 2

The month's most repeated shape is a house that keeps its address and gains a Unit 1 and a
Unit 2. Twelve did, from 131 Johnson Street, 96 Regent Street and 1394 Thornwood Crescent on
2 October to 353 Frontenac Street on the 30th. The City
[says it assigns a new unit number](https://cityofkingston.ca/building-and-renovating/building-permits/additional-residential-units-arus)
to each additional residential unit, for emergency services, deliveries and mail. The file
has no field saying that is what these are.

Three addresses got more than two:

- **80 Queen Street** gained 101 to 104 and 201 on 21 October, joining a 301 on file since
  June. A [broker's brochure](https://images.loopnet.fr/d2/TpxCQ7MMbKm1zWnOFU5HDxNtcc1M3uv09Y8yylXPeyg/80%20Queen%20St%20Suite%20300%20Sublease%20Brochure.pdf)
  offers Suite 300 there for sublease. We found nothing on the new numbers.
- **676 King Street West** gained Units 1 to 4 on 8 October. A
  [sale listing](https://property.ca/addresses/676-king-street-w-kingston) describes
  street-level commercial space with two apartments in a building behind, which leaves the
  four unexplained.
- **21 Grange Street** gained Units 1 to 3 on 14 October. We found no public record.

## Twelve retirements, two gone

The count of 12 retirements overstates what left. Three are the Brock Street units above.
Four are relabels on the same points: Units 3 and 4 of 427 Victoria Street became 1 and 2, Unit
2 of 429 Victoria became a plain 429, and Unit B of 443 Johnson Street became Units 1 and 2.

One is a move. On 2 October, 295 Ontario Street's point shifted 162 metres, landing about 3
metres from 291 Ontario Street, which left the file. Nothing public says what either
number is.

Two are an undoing. Units 1 and 2 of 2818 Highway 15, which arrived on 23 September and
appeared in last month's piece, were gone by 1 October after eight days. The plain address
remains.

That leaves **1173 and 1177 Montreal Street**, side by side between 1163 and 1179, which
went on 23 October. Nothing took their place, and they had not come back as of 6 November.
A retirement is not a demolition, and the public record we could find is silent on both.

The only brand-new single number that isn't one of those moves is **1375 Monarch Drive**,
which arrived on 30 October.
