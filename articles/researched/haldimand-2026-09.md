# Haldimand's September: three rural properties get a second address

Three properties in Haldimand County that already had an address were given a second one
in September: 126 Concession 7 Walpole West, on the same assessment roll as 122; 151 Haldimand
Road 49, on the roll of 191; and 3522 Haldimand Road 20, on the roll of 3478. Two more
addresses arrived in Caledonia and Hagersville, and the file of 26,597 addresses ended the
month at 26,602.

This is the first full month the tracker has watched Haldimand's file, so there is no
earlier month to compare it to. Five addresses is about two in every ten thousand.

## A second address on the same land

The County's layer marks each address *Primary* or *Secondary* and carries the roll number
of the property it sits on. Of 26,602 addresses at the end of the month, 414 are Secondary.
Three of September's were:

- **126 Concession 7 Walpole West**, in Walpole, 61 metres from 122 (12 September). On the same
  day 122 lost the printed label the file keeps alongside the number; its number, street
  and position did not change.
- **151 Haldimand Road 49**, in Dunn, 247 metres from 191, whose roll it shares
  (12 September).
- **3522 Haldimand Road 20**, in South Cayuga, on the roll of 3478 (22 September).

The file does not define Secondary or say what is at any of these points. The County's
[civic addressing by-law](https://www.haldimandcounty.ca/government-administration/by-laws-and-policies/by-law-directory/civic-addressing-by-law/)
says construction of a building may not start, and no building permit may be issued, until
the property has a civic number; that numbers can also go to vacant lots on application;
and that the County is not obliged to give a property additional numbers "except in the
case of a secondary suite". So a new number need not mean a new building. Searches for
each new address and its neighbour on the roll found no permit, severance or listing. The
public record is silent on why these numbers were assigned.

## Caledonia and Hagersville

**84 Walk Road** in Caledonia arrived on 1 September, on the even side of the road,
where the file's numbers had jumped from 66 to 88; 88 is 22 metres away. It has its own roll number.

**17 Victoria Street** in Hagersville arrived on 9 September, 20 metres from 19. It has a roll
number of its own that differs from 19's in only two digits. No consent to sever either lot turned up on the
County's Committee of Adjustment pages, so the file is the only record here.

## The one retirement that wasn't

On the same day, 19 Victoria Street's point moved about ten metres. Haldimand's file has no
permanent id for an address, so the tracker recognises an address by its number, street
and position, and a moved point reads as one address retired and another added. That is
the month's only retirement, and one of its six additions: 19 Victoria Street is still on
file, with the same number and roll.
