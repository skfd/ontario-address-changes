# Hastings County moves 284 more dots, and 303 Hastings Street North comes out with 32 apartments

Since the tracker's first read in June, the County of Hastings' address file had held
**303 Hastings Street North** in Bancroft as one point with 32 labels on it, including an Apartment 303 in a run that
otherwise stops at 220. On 19 October that became two points and 32 apartments, numbered
101 to 112 and 201 to 220. It was part of a second month of the County moving its points
street by street: 284 this time, up from 199 in September.

## By the numbers

| | |
|---|---|
| New addresses | 315, of which 284 are existing addresses whose point moved |
| Retired | 294, the same 284 included |
| Renumbered | 0 by the tracker's count; 5 addresses relabelled on the same roll number (below) |
| Street renames | 0 |
| Address splits | 22 reported; 17 are moved points, and one is an address becoming two |
| **Total on file, 31 October** | **31,357** (+21 over the month) |

Twenty-one more addresses on a file of thirty-one thousand, against twenty in September.
Leave out the moved points and the month is 31 new labels and 10 gone.

## York River Heights

303 Hastings Street is [York River Heights](https://www.hastings.ca/communities/services/housing-services/bancroft-properties-0),
a non-profit seniors' complex. The County's housing page gives it 24 one-bedroom and 8
two-bedroom units, which makes 32.

The file reached 32 before and after 19 October, but not the same 32. Until then it listed a
plain 303, an Apartment 303, and thirty others, with no 103 and no 107, all on one spot. Now
the plain 303 and Apartment 303 are gone, 103 and 107 are there, and the 100s and the 200s
each have their own point, 36 metres apart. Only those two rows are new to the file. The
other thirty moved. The file does not say what the two points stand for.

**16 Alice Street** had the same treatment on 9 October: one point and one roll number
became eight of each, Units 101 to 104 and 201 to 204. Unit 202 is new to the file, three
months after [a sale page for it](https://www.rew.ca/properties/202-16-alice-street-bancroft-on)
was last updated. Nine stacks of labels were pulled apart this way, 60 addresses in all.
12A to 12F Hastings Street North now run in a line about 15 metres long.

## Two hundred and eighty-four points

The County's layer has no permanent identifier for a point, so a point moved by more than
about a metre reads here as one address retired and one added. The median move in October
was 34 metres, and 30 went over 100. The longest was 444 metres, at 81 Ruddy Lane. 275 of the
284 kept their roll number.

Just under half the moves, 139, share a municipality code with Hastings Street North and
Alice Street in Bancroft, with 74 on Hastings Street North alone across 9, 10 and 19 October. Shannonville Road
had 22 on the 7th, Highway 28 had 13 on the 19th. None of the 284 had moved in September.

This also accounts for most of the 22 "splits". Seventeen are labels that were already on
file, moving. The tracker counts several as having retired the original address, and it hadn't: plain 12, 20, 24
and 61 Hastings Street North are all still there, at new positions.

## What the County has said

The public record is silent on why the points moved, with no notice from the County or the
Town. The nearest thing is from January. [Reports to the County's 9-1-1 Sub-Committee](https://opencouncil.ca/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/HC-9-1-1-Sub-committee-04-Feb-2026-Agenda-Pdf.pdf)
say the move to Next Generation 9-1-1, which routes calls by location, "continues to require
extensive updates to Geographic Information System (GIS) data and processes", that staff held
sessions at each municipal office "specifically for staff working with addressing", and that
the old network goes in March 2027. They name no street and do not mention moving address
points, so this is context and not an explanation.

## Same place, new label

Five addresses changed their label and kept their roll number:

- **73 Crystal Beach Road** became **73A** on the same point on 1 October, with a new **73B**
  43 metres away. Of the 22, it is the only address that became two.
- **11863 Highway 62** became **11864B**, 464 metres away, the same day. **11864A** arrived with
  it on a roll number of its own.
- **574 Airport Road** became **548**, 397 metres away, on 7 October.
- **15 Cleak Avenue** became **15A** on 10 October, beside a 15B already on file.
- **36987 Highway 62** became **36987B** on 22 October, beside a 36987A.

Searches for Crystal Beach Road and Airport Road found nothing on these numbers.

## What actually came and went

Twenty-one single numbers arrived, scattered across the county, among them 496 Pine Point
Lane, 756 St. Ola Road and 1708 Melrose Road. Eight sit on a roll number another address
already holds, and 260 and 430 Robinson Road arrived on 16 October sharing one, 789 metres
apart. The file does not say what any of them are.

Two left without a replacement, both on Ingram Road on 3 October. 592 shared its roll number
with 593, which stays. **697** is the only address of the month that went and took its roll
number with it. A search found nothing on either.

## What to watch

Whether the moves carry on in November. Baker Street, which September suggested watching, did
not change.
