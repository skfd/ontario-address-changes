# Twenty addresses in Ridgeway moved off Whispering Woods Trail and onto Butternut Crescent

Until early September, twenty homes in Fort Erie were filed as units of one address: 3411
Whispering Woods Trail, Units 3 to 22. On the Region's 3 September read they became 3 to 22
Butternut Crescent, each on the same map point and under the same record id. The street
name matches a Blythwood Homes condominium townhouse community in Ridgeway. Across the
Niagara Region file, September added 75 addresses and retired none.

Seventy-five addresses on a file of 208,000, about one in 2,800. August added 46. Of
September's 75, 44 are in the City of Niagara Falls, 20 in Fort Erie, 7 in Port Colborne,
and one each in Welland, Pelham, St. Catharines and West Lincoln.

## 3411 Whispering Woods Trail becomes Butternut Crescent

The twenty records carry consecutive ids. Before September each read *3411 Whispering
Woods Trail* with a unit number from 3 to 22. Now each reads a plain house number from 3 to
22 on *Butternut Crescent*, with no unit. None of the points moved. The plain 3411
Whispering Woods Trail is still on the file, and there is no 1 or 2 Butternut Crescent.

Listings for 6 Butternut Crescent describe "The Oaks at Six Mile Creek, an exclusive
adult-oriented community by Blythwood Homes in the charming town of Ridgeway", with the
house offered as a "to-be-built interior townhome" and "a condominium townhome"
([listing](https://filipesells.ca/property/6-butternut-crescent-fort-erie-x13440220/)).
A development listing on REW gives The Oaks as a Blythwood Homes community of 124 townhome
units in Ridgeway, "Now Selling"
([REW](https://www.rew.ca/developments/6694/the-oaks-at-six-mile-creek-fort-erie-on)).

What the file records is the address moving, from a unit on a trail to a house number on
its own street. A search found no Town of Fort Erie by-law or council item naming
Butternut Crescent, so when and how the name was approved is not on the public record. The
Region's file has other Butternuts (a Butternut Court in Lincoln, a Butternut Boulevard in
Niagara Falls), but this is the first Butternut Crescent in Fort Erie on the file.

## A Unit 1 and a Unit 2 on eighteen Niagara Falls lots

In the City of Niagara Falls, eighteen addresses gained numbered units while keeping the
plain number: Units 1 and 2 at fifteen of them (6304 Ker Street, 7728 Watson Street, 7745
Secretariat Court, three houses on Harlyn Common, among others) and Units 1 to 3 at 5311
Bridge Street, 6736 McMicking Street and 6915 Harovics Lane. The City allows up to two
additional dwelling units on a residential lot, three homes in all, which "cannot be
severed or sold separately from the main dwelling, but may be rented"
([City of Niagara Falls](https://www.niagarafalls.ca/building-planning-and-business/building-and-renovating/additional-dwelling-units-secondary-units/)).
The file says only *Unit*. It does not say whether these are second suites, and a search
for the three Harlyn Common numbers found nothing.

## 745 Crescent Road gets eighteen units

In Fort Erie, 745 Crescent Road gained eighteen units at the same point as the plain 745,
which has been on file since the tracker's first read: 101, 102, 104 and 105, then 201 to
207 and 301 to 307. There is no 103. A search turned up no listing, site plan or
application for the address. What the eighteen units are, the public record does not
say.

## Four numbers on Killaly Street East

In Port Colborne, 2487A, 2487B, 2487C and 2487D Killaly Street East appeared as four points
about 60 metres apart along the road, east of 2477. No plain 2487 was ever on the file.
Nothing public turned up for the number. On the next kept read, 1 October, the same four
records read 2487, 2489, 2491 and 2493, on the same points. That belongs to October's
piece. Why the letters came first is not on record.

## 7411 becomes 7407 Marvel Drive

One house in Niagara Falls was renumbered in place. The point between 7401 and 7413 Marvel
Drive went from 7411 to 7407. It kept the same id and did not move, and there is no other
7407 or 7411 on the street. The file does not say why.

Next month: whether the plain 3411 Whispering Woods Trail stays on file beside the twenty
addresses that used to be its units.

changing rather than twenty retired and twenty added. With a single snapshot, gross
activity equals the net: 75 additions, 1 renumbering and 20 other edits. Point moves under
