# 591 First Street gets eight units, and two Commissioners Road addresses leave the file

On 23 September, 591 First Street, between Mardell Place and Rushland Avenue, gained eight unit numbers,
2 through 16 in even steps, in the City of London's address file. The original
591 is still on file beside them. Eleven months earlier, a planning report filed with
the City proposed an eight-unit townhouse building at the back of the lot.

The file grew by 12 addresses, less than one in ten thousand of the 143,126 it held at
the end of August. Every addition and retirement arrived in a single update, on
23 September.

## 591 First Street

591 First Street sits on the west side of the street. In October 2025 a
[planning report for Ivy Homes Ltd.](https://london.ca/sites/default/files/2025-10/LTR%20-%20Planning%20Justification%20Report%20-%20591%20First%20Street,%20London%20-%20AODA.pdf)
asked the City to rezone the lot, which held a single-storey house and a detached
garage. The plan was a semi-detached house facing First Street and "an 8-unit townhouse
building internal to the site."

The file now gives 591 eight units: 2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12, 14 and 16. They carry eight
consecutive record numbers, so they were entered together. The base address stayed and
its point moved 14 metres. The count matches the report's eight townhouses. The file
does not say whether the units are those townhouses. No council decision on the
application turned up, so whether the rezoning was approved is not on the public record.

## Commissioners Road West

**559 and 567 Commissioners Road West** came off the file on the same day. They are two
of the three lots in a
[Tricar Properties rezoning](https://london.ca/sites/default/files/2025-07/FINAL%20Z-25058%20-%20555-569%20&%20567%20Commissioners%20Rd%20W%20(AR).pdf)
(file Z-25058) that City staff recommended in July 2025. It would allow a six-storey,
74-unit apartment building. The staff report describes the lands as "currently
developed with three existing single detached dwellings which are proposed to be
demolished."

The third lot, **555 Commissioners Road West**, is still on file, 32 metres from where
559 stood. The file records that two addresses left. It does not record a demolition,
and it does not say why 555 stayed.

## A new street near Bostwick Road

**Scollard Street** appears in the file for the first time, with four numbers: 5975,
5982, 6010 and 6064. The nearest address that was already on file is 3080 Bostwick
Road, 186 metres away. A new **3078 Yorkville Street** arrived the same day, 220 metres
away. 3080 Bostwick Road is
[York Developments' 17-storey tower](https://vbands.com/properties-1/3080-bostwick-road-london),
and its point moved 33 metres in the same update. York has
[proposed a 12-storey third phase](https://urbantoronto.ca/forum/threads/london-3010-3050-yorkville-street-york-12s-zedd.38154)
at Yorkville Street and Southdale Road West. Nothing public turned up for Scollard Street
itself. Every search for the name returns the Scollard Street in Toronto. So the record
is silent on whose street it is and what the four numbers are for.

## 27 and 29 Noel Avenue trade places

On 2 September, the two records for 27 and 29 Noel Avenue exchanged numbers. The point
that was 27 now reads 29, and the point that was 29 now reads 27. The two points are
about ten metres apart, and neither moved. That is the month's two renumberings, and it
changed no address on the street, only which point carries which number. The file does
not say why.

## The rest of the month

Four single additions: **675 Bradley Avenue**, near Jalna Boulevard and Wellington Road;
**523 Southdale Road West**; **3078 Yorkville Street**; and **20585 Nissouri Road**, the
first Nissouri Road address in London's file, 345 metres from the nearest other one.
Two more retirements: **934 Oxford Street West**, between 928 (still on file) and 940,
and **197 Richmond Street**. Nothing public turned up on any of them.
