# Scott Court arrives in Point Edward, while the county rewrites how its whole address file is spelled

On 15 September 2026 the County of Lambton's address file gained **Scott Court** in the
Village of Point Edward: 25 numbers, 300 to 329, on a street the file had not carried before.
The same day the county republished the entire file under new field names, and that
change, not any change on the ground, is why the tracker counted hundreds of addresses
coming and going. Underneath it, September was 68 new addresses on a file of nearly
fifty-seven thousand.

## By the numbers

| **Total on file, 30 September** | **56,984** (+87 since 25 June) |

The tracker's raw count for the month is 826 in and 739 out. 739 of those 826 are the
same addresses, re-issued under a new spelling (explained below); the other 87 are the
file's real growth, and 19 of those are placeholder rows. The 87 is about 0.15% of the
file.

## Scott Court

The 25 new points carry 300, 301, 303, 305 to 316, 318 to 325, 327 and 329 Scott Court,
Village of Point Edward. The nearest addresses on other streets are 1600 Venetian
Boulevard, 235 metres away, and 92 Alexandra Avenue, 280 metres. All 25 show
9 September 2026 in the field the file labels *approved*.

The file does not say what Scott Court is. The closest thing the public record offers:
in May, [Sarnia News Today reported](https://sarnianewstoday.ca/sarnia/news/2026/05/11/work-to-begin-on-new-point-edward-subdivision)
that developer Alan Merrington had signed a subdivision agreement with the Village for
**21 one-level villas at 1600 Venetian Blvd**, with site servicing to start that week.
The story names no street, and 25 numbers is not 21 homes, so the two may be the same
project or may not. Nothing found ties them together by name.

## Five lots on Sante Drive

**Sante Drive** is the month's other new street: 3603, 3607, 3611, 3615 and 3619, in the
Town of Plympton-Wyoming, 58 metres from 6871 Shoreside Drive and 60 from 6884 Elizabeth
Way. This one the record does explain. On 23 March 2026 Plympton-Wyoming's planning
council [passed By-law 22 of 2026](https://www.plympton-wyoming.com/news/posts/2026-03-23-2026-03-25-planning-council-regular-council-meeting-highlights/),
lifting the holding symbol "for 5 lots on Sante Drive". Five lots in March, five
addresses in September. Their *approved* field reads 22 June; the tracker first saw them on
15 September.

## Turnberry Lane fills in its even side

Also in Plympton-Wyoming, **Turnberry Lane** gained thirteen even numbers, 3808 to 3844,
in the gap between 3806 and 3848, which were already on file along with odd numbers
between 3821 and 3843. The nearest other streets are St. Andrew's Circle (38 m) and Sybil
Court (66 m). All thirteen read 10 June in the *approved* field. No planning item, permit notice or
news story about new lots on Turnberry Lane turned up; the public record is silent on
what they are.

The other 23 numbers of 15 September are scattered singles across the county, from
1400 Wellington Street in Sarnia to 7145 Aughrim Line in Dawn-Euphemia. The month's last
two came on 30 September: **5674 Lakeshore Road** in Plympton-Wyoming and **4544
McMillan Street** in Enniskillen, both marked *approved* on 29 September.

## What the file itself did

In the 25 June file, Lambton wrote a street the way a sign does: *West Parkway Drive*, *Port
Franks Estate Drive*, *Highway 40*, and a lettered address as *347 A Kathleen Avenue*.
The [republished layer](https://www.lambtongis.ca/arcgis_adaptor/rest/services/OpenData/AddressPoints/MapServer/0)
has 26 fields instead and splits those strings up: the direction (*West*), the street
type in front (*Highway*), the bare name (*Parkway*, *40*), the number, and a separate
unit (*A*). No notice of the change from the county turned up.

The tracker recognizes an address by its number, street and position, so every address whose
street had a direction or a type in front, or whose number had a letter, looked new, and
so did every point the county moved: 739 in all, from 6514 West Parkway Drive and 10300 Beach O Pines Road in Lambton Shores to
the lettered units on Kathleen Avenue in Sarnia. All 739 came back the same day under the
new spelling. 686 are in exactly the same spot, four within a few metres, and 49, mostly along Confederation Line
and Petrolia Line, were moved by 10 to 194 metres. Nothing left the file. The same
re-spelling is why the tracker's lists show "Parkway" and "Franks Estate" as new streets
and sixteen lettered addresses as "splits": they are names and units that were already
there.

One caution for anyone reading the new field called *approved*: every row in the file now
has a date from 2026 in it, so it cannot be the date an address was first given.

*How this is measured* — The tracker reads the County of Lambton's published address
layer daily and keeps a snapshot whenever the contents differ from the last one kept. The
last snapshot kept before the month is 25 June, so September's figures are one comparison
between it and the last one in the month (30 September), with snapshots kept on 15 and 30
September. Thirty daily reads in September, none failed. Net
counts compare the two ends of that window; the day-by-day edits (gross) come to the 2
additions of 30 September and 173 field changes on 15 September that belong to the republish. The republish
itself is held off the public daily reports as a technical change to the feed. The 739
re-issued addresses were matched to their old records by place and address in the
