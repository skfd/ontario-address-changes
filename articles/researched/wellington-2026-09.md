# Sideroad 18 in Fergus fills in, and 6734 Wellington Road 16 gets five units

On 16 September the County of Wellington's address file filled a short stretch of
**Sideroad 18** in Fergus. **145, 151, 153 and 155** arrived between two numbers that were
already there, so the run from 145 to 157 now has every odd number, seven addresses in
about 46 metres. Two days later, **6734 Wellington Road 16** in rural Centre Wellington
gained Units 1 to 5, and the plain 6734 is still on the file. The public record is silent
on both.

Thirty on a file of almost forty-three thousand is under a tenth of a percent. Twenty-four
of the 35 new addresses are in Centre Wellington. Erin and Wellington North have three
each, Minto and Puslinch two each, Mapleton one, and Guelph-Eramosa none. The changes did
not all come at once. The County's file changed on eleven days in September, between the
9th and the 30th.

## Sideroad 18, Fergus

Where Sideroad 18 runs through Fergus, the file already had 149 and 157. On 16 September
it gained 145, 151, 153 and 155 between and beside them, and on the same day the points for
149 and 157 moved 19 and 25 metres. The nearest address on another street is 146
Stafford Street, 37 metres from 145.

Searches for the four new numbers, and through the County Land Division Committee's 2026
notices, found nothing about this stretch. The development the Wellington Advertiser
[reported](https://www.wellingtonadvertiser.com/council-approves-another-development-on-hwy-6-just-north-of-fergus)
council approving between Sideroads 18 and 19, at 961 St. David Street North, is about
800 metres away and is not this site. What is going up on Sideroad 18, if anything, the public record does not say.

## Five units at 6734 Wellington Road 16

On 18 September, **6734 Wellington Road 16** gained five unit addresses, numbered 1 to 5.
They are not stacked on one building. Their points are between 200 and 530 metres from
the original 6734 and spread across about 390 metres of land. The nearest address on any
other road is 716 metres away. No listing, notice or planning item for 6734 turned up, and
the file does not say what the five units are.

## Two numbers changed, one address went to another road

Five addresses left the file. Each was replaced on or next to the same spot, so none of
them reads as anything going away.

- **7438 Middlebrook Road**, near Elora, became 7438A on the same point and 7438B 24
  metres away (9 September).
- **135 Kertland Street** in Elora became 131 on the same point, with a new 137 11 metres
  away (9 September). **19 Gilkison Street** became 17 on the same point (16 September).
- In Wellington North, **7385 Concession 6 North** came off the file on 16 September. A
  **9392 Concession 6 North** appeared five metres from it, and **7385 Sideroad 3 East**
  appeared 242 metres away. The file does not say whether they are the same property.
- In Fergus, **415 Maiden Lane** left on 30 September, and **420 St George Street West**
  (21 metres away) and **415 St Patrick Street West** (60 metres) arrived the same day.

Searches for each came back empty.

## What the file did to itself

This layer has no record ids, so the tracker tells Wellington's addresses apart by number,
street, unit and position. A point that moves leaves the file and comes back as "new".
That accounts for 11 of the 16 retirements. Most moved four to nine metres, among them
5113 and 5127 Trafalgar Road in Erin on the day 5125 arrived between them. 8563 Concession
6 in Mapleton moved 82 metres. Separately, 8450 Eramosa-Garafraxa Townline read 8452 from
16 to 28 September and went back to 8450 the next day, on the same point. That is why
September's edit count is three higher each way than its outcome.

## What to watch

Whether Sideroad 18 fills any further, and whether anything public turns up about the five
units on Wellington Road 16.
