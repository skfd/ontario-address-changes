# Three townhouse numbers squeeze in beside 3192 New Street, and 884 Glenwood arrives four months after the lot next door went to the Committee to be split

On 15 September 2026 the City of Burlington's address file gained **3186, 3188 and 3190
New Street**, three numbers a few metres apart in the gap between 3180 and 3192, every one
of them marked *Pending*. A July listing describes 3188 as a new freehold townhome with
construction "well underway". Two weeks later **884 Glenwood Avenue** appeared between
880 and 886 in Aldershot, four months after an application to sever one new residential
lot from 880 went before the Committee of Adjustment.

## By the numbers

| | |
|---|---|
| New addresses | 11 |
| Retired | 3 |
| Renumbered | 0 |
| Street renames | 0 |
| Address splits | 1 (541 Chamberlain Road, Units 1 and 2) |
| Status changes | 1 (500 Plains Road East, *Pending* to *Active*) |
| **Total on file, 28 September** | **60,356** (+8 over the month) |

Three of the eleven "new" addresses are also the three retired ones, for a reason
explained below. Take them out and September is eight addresses new to a file of sixty
thousand, which changed on four days out of thirty. A quiet month.

## Three numbers between 3180 and 3192 New Street

The three New Street points arrived together on 15 September with consecutive City record
numbers, six or seven metres apart, and all three sit within eight metres of the dot for
3192, which is still on the file unchanged.

A [listing for 3188 New Street](https://homli.ca/burlington-real-estate/3188-new-street/W13550254)
dated 10 July 2026 offers a new freehold townhome by Coral Gable Homes, about 2,250 square
feet above grade, with construction "well underway" and a chance to choose interior
finishes. A freehold townhome at 3188 has attached neighbours, and 3186 and 3190 are the
obvious candidates, but no source names them, and nothing public turned up for 3192
itself: no consent, no part-lot-control item, no demolition. Burlington's Committee of
Adjustment publishes its agendas but [releases decisions only on request](https://www.burlington.ca/en/planning-and-development/committee-of-adjustment.aspx),
so the paper that turned one lot into three, if that is what happened, is not online.

All three numbers carry the status *Pending*. So does 884 Glenwood, and so do 187 other
addresses on the file. The City's layer
[publishes no definition](https://mapping.burlington.ca/arcgisweb/rest/services/COB/AddressPoints/MapServer/0?f=pjson)
of *Pending* or *Active*; they are plain text in two columns with no code list behind
them. What *Pending* means is whatever the file does next, and 500 Plains Road East,
further down, is the month's one example of a next.

## 884 Glenwood Avenue, and the lot next door

884 Glenwood Avenue appeared on 28 September, *Pending*, on the even side between 880 and
886. On the Committee of Adjustment agendas of
[8 April](https://events.burlington.ca/meetings/Detail/2026-04-08-1730-Committee-of-Adjustment/49c0617e-5c8c-4abf-a912-b41d01251c9b)
and [20 May 2026](https://events.burlington.ca/meetings/Detail/2026-05-20-1300-Committee-of-Adjustment/f6ceb750-4ba6-4815-a356-b44a014d3b1f),
consent application B-004-2024 asks to sever a parcel from 880 Glenwood "for the creation
of one new residential lot", a little under 800 square metres, leaving about 4,000 with
the existing house, with a companion variance for that house's side yard. The decision is not
published. A new even number between 880 and 886, four months after the hearing, is what
an approved severance would look like in this file. The file does not say that 884 is that
lot, and nothing online does either.

## 541 Chamberlain Road gets Units 1 and 2

On 15 September **541 Chamberlain Road**, a house in south-east Burlington near Longmoor
Drive, gained two points: *Unit 1* and *Unit 2*, both typed *Tenant*, the file's word for
a unit inside a property. The original 541 is still there. No other address on Chamberlain
Road has units.

Rental listings for the same house advertise two separate
[lower-level suites, A and B](https://www.zolo.ca/burlington-real-estate/541-chamberlain-road/a),
each "brand-new, never-lived-in" with its own entrance, and a
[renovated main floor](https://www.squareyards.ca/lease/on/halton/burlington/shoreacres/unit-upper-541-chamberlain-road)
let on its own, with a price change on 14 October 2026. The City's
[handout on additional residential units](https://www.burlington.ca/en/planning-and-development/resources/Zoning-Clearance-Certificates/Zoning-FAQ-for-ARU.pdf)
allows up to three such units on a lot with a detached house, by building permit and
without a planning hearing, which is why no committee item exists. The listings say A
and B; the file says 1 and 2. Neither says which is which.

## Three Guelph Line addresses that left and came back

The retired list has three entries, **3350, 6671 and 6701 Guelph Line**, and all three are
also on the new list. Each left and returned in a single update: 3350 on 16 September,
the other two on 25 September. What changed was the dot and the record behind it. 3350's
point moved 236 metres north, from between 3324 and 3353 to beyond 3383. 6701's moved 362
metres south, from north of 6783 to between 6724 and 6774. 6671's moved four metres.

The tracker tells Burlington's addresses apart by number, street, unit and position, so a
moved dot reads as one address gone and one arrived; that part is bookkeeping. But each of
the three also came back with a new City property-record number, and so did the brand new
**3340 Guelph Line** that appeared the same day as 3350 moved, 82 metres from 3324. The
month's twelve new rows carry consecutive record numbers 106775 to 106786, in the order
Enfield, the Chamberlain units, New Street, 3340, 3350, then 6701, 6671 and Glenwood. One
number in that run, 106783, has never appeared on the file. A dot moved four metres came
back with a new record number all the same, and the file does not say why.

Listings describe 3350 as a rural estate and 6701 as vacant farmland. No consent, variance
or permit for any of the four addresses turned up. The public record is silent.

## 500 Plains Road East stops being Pending

The month's one status change is **500 Plains Road East** going from *Pending* to
*Active* on 28 September. That is the
[Northshore](https://condos.ca/burlington/498-plains-road-e-townhouses-498-plains-rd-e-500-plains-rd-e)
condominium by National Homes, eight storeys and 188 units by the listing site's count,
with its condo corporation registered on 24 November 2025 and resale units on the market
since May 2026. The point had been *Pending* since the tracker's first read on 11 June. A
second point for the same number, typed *Property* rather than *Address*, had appeared
about fifty metres away on 20 August. The building was ten months old as a corporation
when its address stopped being pending; the file records the flip and not the trigger.

The remaining addition is **0000 Enfield Road**, an unnumbered point with no name and no
ward value, 33 metres from 619 Wickens Avenue and 1.2 kilometres from the nearest numbered
Enfield address. It is not misplaced: OpenStreetMap shows Enfield Road as two separate
pieces, and the point sits on the
[short eastern stub off King Road](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/10157191) beside the
rail yard, which has no numbers. The file holds 141 points numbered 0000, thirty of them
named (one on Guelph Line is the Centennial Bikeway). This one carries no name, and nothing
public says what it is.

## What to watch

Whether 3186, 3188, 3190 New Street and 884 Glenwood turn *Active*, and how long that
takes; whether 3192 New Street survives the townhomes; and whether record 106783 ever
shows up.
