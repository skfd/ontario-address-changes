# Three James Street numbers give way to the Martha James tower, and two Burlington Avenue lots become 1389 Caroline

On 16 October 2026 the City of Burlington's address file dropped **2082, 2086 and 2090
James Street** and, the same day, switched **2088 James Street** from *Pending* to
*Active*. That corner of James and Martha is Mattamy's 13-storey Martha James
Condominiums, where ground was broken in January 2023. The file carried the three old
numbers for nearly four years after that. Three days later, a few blocks west, **548 and
550 Burlington Avenue** had already gone and **1389 Caroline Street** became active on
the same spot.

One of the six additions is also one of the seven retirements: 675 Wickens Avenue moved

going live.

## 2088 James Street, and the three numbers it replaced

2082, 2086 and 2090 James Street had been on the file, *Active*, since the tracker's
first read in June. They came off together on 16 October. 2088, which sits between them,
had been on the file the whole time as *Pending*, and it went *Active* the same day. On
20 October a second 2088 point appeared about nine metres from the first, typed
*Address* rather than *Property*. It carries an older City record number than 2088
itself, and nothing on the file says why it showed up only now. 2088 is now the only
number on that stretch of James Street. The nearest other points are on Martha and Pearl.

The outside record names the site. Mattamy applied to build a condominium of
[18 storeys at 2082, 2086 and 2090 James Street](https://www.mariannemeedward.ca/posts-to-be-added/six-new-development-applications-ward-2-public-meetings-march-7-27)
on the corner of Martha. By 2022 the building was
[13 storeys and 150 units](https://urbantoronto.ca/news/2022/03/martha-james-condominiums-rise-burlington.47329),
and Mattamy [broke ground in late January 2023](https://renx.ca/mattamy-breaks-ground-on-three-gta-condos).
Units at 2088 James were [on the resale market by July 2026](https://condos.ca/burlington/martha-james-condominiums-2088-james-st/unit-413-W13598160),
in a building listed as 0–1 years old. So the file lagged the street by years. It kept
three numbers for houses on a site that had been a construction site since early 2023.
The tower's units were on the resale market while its own number was still *Pending*. The file does
not say what triggered the switch, and no public record of the condominium's
registration date turned up.

## 548 and 550 Burlington Avenue become 1389 Caroline Street

548 and 550 Burlington Avenue left the file on 16 October. On 19 October **1389 Caroline
Street** went from *Pending* to *Active*. That point had been on the file since 9 July,
and it sits about five metres from where 548 used to be, at the corner of Caroline and
Burlington Avenue.

The [Committee of Adjustment agenda for 23 September 2026](https://events.burlington.ca/meetings/Detail/2026-09-23-1300-Committee-of-Adjustment/8dadfc82-38f7-46aa-b6d2-b4bf00f27c54)
explains most of the change. Application A-041/26 is for a new one-storey house with an
attached garage on "548/550 Burlington Avenue", a vacant lot of 725 square metres. The
staff report says the two lots have been "formally consolidated with the Land Registry
Office". The variances asked for treat **Caroline Street as the front yard**. Heritage
staff noted that the lot faces the designated Skelton-Giddings House at 534 Burlington
Avenue, and said they "would have expected the front façade to front Burlington Avenue
(not Caroline Street)". The same report lists a decade of earlier applications on the
two lots, including two that went to the Ontario Land Tribunal and were refused.

The agenda never mentions 1389. Two Burlington Avenue numbers merging into one lot that
fronts Caroline matches what the file shows. Burlington does not publish Committee
decisions online, so the record does not say whether the variances were granted.

## Northshore's other two numbers

In September 500 Plains Road East, the [Northshore](https://condos.ca/burlington/498-plains-road-e-townhouses-498-plains-rd-e-500-plains-rd-e)
condominium, stopped being *Pending*. Its neighbours followed in October: **498** on
5 October and **496** on 15 October. The listing site gives 498 as the address of the
building's townhouse units and 500 as the address of its apartments. Nothing public was
found about 496. On 5 October the file also dropped the second, *Property*-typed 500
Plains Road East point that had appeared in August, and only the original 500 is left.

## Five new numbers, mostly without a paper trail

- **2298 Queensway Drive** appeared *Pending* on 9 October, between 2294 and 2300, both
  still on file. Council approved a
  [24-unit townhouse plan for 2294–2300 Queensway Drive](https://www.mariannemeedward.ca/posts-to-be-added/burlington-council-unanimously-approves-townhouse-complex-at-2294-2300-queensway-drive),
  with a common-element road, and the project is
  [listed as coming soon](https://www.rew.ca/developments/12081/2294-2300-queensway-drive-townhomes-burlington-on).
  No source names 2298 itself.
- **541 Drury Lane** appeared on 15 October, *Active*, named *Central Park* and typed
  *Secondary Building*. It sits next to the YMCA at 500 and the arena at 519. The public
  record is silent on what the building is.
- **1321 and 1323 Fisher Avenue**, a consecutive pair, appeared *Pending* on 28 October
  between 1320 and 1325, near Augustine Drive. No consent, permit or listing turned up.
- **677 Wickens Avenue**, *Pending* since August, went *Active* on 21 October. The same
  day 675's point moved about four metres and came back with a new City record number.
  The tracker counts that as one retirement and one addition. Nothing public was found
  for either.

The City's record numbers for the month's new points run 106787 (Queensway), 106788
(Drury), then 106794 (Wickens) and 106795–96 (Fisher). The five numbers in between have
never appeared on the file, and neither has 106783, the gap from September.

## What to watch

Whether 2298 Queensway turns out to be the first of the 24 townhouse numbers, whether
1321 and 1323 Fisher go *Active*, and whether the second 2088 James Street point is kept.
At month end 185 addresses were still *Pending*, two fewer than at the start.
