# 153 McDonough Lane becomes 350 Gauleys Bay Road, and 1279 2nd Avenue South becomes four

Between the snapshots of 16 and 22 October 2026, **153 McDonough Lane** in Northern Bruce
Peninsula left the County of Bruce's address file and **350 Gauleys Bay Road** arrived on
the same assessment roll number, about 740 metres to the north. A listing posted last
November describes the property as 56 acres with "over 5600ft. of shoreline on both
Gauley Bay and Lake Huron". It now has an address on the road that carries the bay's name.

Three weeks earlier, on 1 October, the single point for **1279 2nd Avenue South** on
Saugeen First Nation land became Units 1 to 4.

Forty-three more records on a file of nearly fifty-two thousand is under a tenth of a
percent, smaller than September's 58. Of the 45 addresses genuinely new, 12 are in
Northern Bruce Peninsula, 8 in Saugeen Shores, 7 in Brockton and 6 on Saugeen First
Nation land. Thirty daily reads, none failed.

## McDonough Lane to Gauleys Bay Road

153 McDonough Lane had been on the file since the tracker's first read in June; it was
last there on 16 October. On the 22nd, 350 Gauleys Bay Road stood about 740 metres
further north with the same roll number. The nearest other address in the file is
186 McDonough Lane, nearly a kilometre off; the only other number on Gauleys Bay Road
is 99.

A [listing for 153 McDonough
Lane](https://dwelly.ca/app/properties/153-Mcdonough-Lane-Northern-Bruce-Peninsula-ON-N-CA-for-sale-X12504974),
dated 4 November 2025 at $1,550,000 through RE/MAX Grey Bruce Realty, describes 56 acres
with "over 5600ft. of shoreline on both Gauley Bay and Lake Huron", and says "there are
structures on site and a hand pump well". It does not mention a new address. Nothing
public found says why the property was re-addressed, or whether it has been sold. The
file shows a roll number taking a new address on a different road; it does not say what
the new point marks.

## Saugeen First Nation: Units 1 to 4, and two numbers on French Bay Road

**1279 2nd Avenue South** was one point on 30 September. On the 1 October snapshot it
was four, Units 1 to 4, all on the old roll number and all within about ten metres of
the old point, which came off the file the same day. The public record is silent on the
address.

The same snapshot added **752 and 754 French Bay Road**, on the roll number already
carried by 750 French Bay Road, 48 metres away. Saugeen First Nation's [rent-to-own
housing page](https://saugeenfirstnation.com/rent-to-own-housing-program/) says
"applications are now being accepted for the 2025 build located on French Bay Rd",
through Habitat for Humanity, and in December 2025 [Sarnia News
Today](https://sarnianewstoday.ca/midwestern/news/2025/12/10/bruce-power-invests-1m-to-support-new-housing-for-saugeen-first-nation)
reported Bruce Power funding "four new houses for community members, with the first
nearly ready for occupancy". Neither source gives a house number, so neither says these
two numbers are that build.

## Two addresses in Arran-Elderslie change, and stay put

On 16 October **191 Victoria Street** became **191 Balaklava Street**: same point, same
roll number. It had been the last address in Arran-Elderslie filed under plain
"Victoria Street", and it slots between 187 and 199 Balaklava, which were already there.
On 29 October **325 Albert Street** became **315 Albert Street**, again at the same
coordinates on the same roll number. On the 14th, **91 Bruce Rd17** was respelled
**91 Bruce Road 17**, like its neighbours. The file does not say which of these is a
correction and which a change on the ground.

In Saugeen Shores the slow respelling of McLean Crescent continued: **377 McLean Cr**
became **377 McLean Cres** on 29 October, the seventh address on the street to carry the
long form while the rest keep the short one. **443 North Rankin Street**, in Saugeen Shores too,
left the file on 14 October; the public record is silent on why.

## What the file did to itself

Thirty-nine of the month's additions and thirty-nine of its retirements are the same
address at a different point. This tracker tells Bruce's addresses apart by number,
street, unit and position, so a point moved by more than about a metre reads as one gone
and one new. That is the tracker's bookkeeping, not the County's.

The largest is **Sunset Drive** in South Bruce Peninsula, which the brief lists as both
the street gaining the most addresses and the one losing the most: fifteen points from
892 to 968, each keeping its roll number, all shifted 25 to 54 metres between
the 14 and 16 October snapshots. No address there is new. The same goes for **240 South
Rankin Street**, which the brief counts as a split: its two units moved three to four
metres on the 29th. Four points on Hawes Road moved on 2 October. And on 30 October
**463 The Bury Road** moved 225 metres while **214 Pike Bay Road** appeared 122 metres
from it on the same roll number: one property, now two addresses on two roads.

In Huron-Kinloss, the two new addresses **905 and 915 Parkplace** are filed under the
one-word spelling the file uses for 801 to 904, while 895, 914 and 918 nearby are filed
as *Park Pl*.

## What to watch

Whether anything more appears on the 750 French Bay Road roll; whether McLean Crescent
finishes its respelling; whether 214 Pike Bay Road and 463 The Bury Road keep sharing a
roll number; and whether the two unnumbered points on Nayaushing Road, still unnumbered,
get numbers.

*How this is measured* — The tracker reads the County of Bruce's published address layer
daily and keeps a snapshot whenever the contents differ from the last one kept. October
2026 produced eight snapshots (1, 2, 13, 14, 16, 22, 29 and 30 October); the month's
figures are one comparison between the last snapshot before the month (30 September) and
the last one in it (30 October), so anything after the 30th belongs to November. Gross
and net are identical this month: nothing was added and then removed inside it. Bruce
publishes no stable row id, so a moved point counts as one retirement and one addition,
and so does a renamed or renumbered address; the counts above separate them using the
assessment roll number the file carries. Thirty daily reads, none failed. Each day's
