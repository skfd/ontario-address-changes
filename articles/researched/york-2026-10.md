# Two Unionville streets now hold 119 house numbers, and Stouffville gained four streets in a day

On 14 October 2026 York Region's address file redrew Anna Russell Way in Unionville. Forty-five
of its 93 house numbers came off the file. The other 48 moved, one of them by 163 metres.
The street gained 36 new numbers, and a new street beside it, John Canning Way, arrived with
35 more. Afterwards the two streets held 119 house numbers between them, not counting 100 Anna Russell
Way, a building with its own unit numbers. That is the number of townhomes in Minto Communities' Park &
Main, near Main Street Unionville.

Ten times September's gain of 14, on a file of almost 432,000. Nearly all of it came on two
days. On 14 October Unionville had 71 additions and 45 departures. On 28 October 106
addresses arrived on four streets in Whitchurch-Stouffville. The rest of the region added a
dozen numbers between them.

## Anna Russell Way and John Canning Way

Until October, Anna Russell Way carried 93 house numbers, 1 to 93. All 93 shared one postal
code, L3R 3X3, and they had been issued together: their point ids run unbroken from 333202 to
333294. The street also holds 100 Anna Russell Way, a building with its own unit numbers,
which the October update did not touch.

On 14 October the update removed 1, 39, 41, 43 and 45, and 40 of the 48 numbers above 45. The 48 that
stayed were moved. 44 Anna Russell Way moved 163 metres, and 61 moved 149. New numbers ran
from 95 up to 168, which now sits 34 metres from 60 Fred Varley Drive. John Canning Way got
numbers 3 to 38. Number 3 is 8 metres from 75 Anna Russell Way. Two points on Eureka
Street nearby were nudged by 6 and 11 metres the same day.

In 2022 a City of Markham memo placed "multi-unit residential properties to the south and east
along Highway 7 and Anna Russell Way, a privately-owned right-of-way". The memo was written for a
Committee of Adjustment hearing on 36 and 38 Eureka Street, and the applicant was Minto
Communities. Minto was to convey land for a new park, joining 34 Eureka Street to the existing
Anna Russell Parkette, and the memo said that parkland dedication was tied to a site plan application "to facilitate a townhouse
development at 17 Anna Russell Way"
([Markham Committee of Adjustment memo, 31 October 2022](https://markhammuseum.ca/sites/default/files/2025-01/Staff%20Report%20-%20B.021.122%20and%20B.020.22%20-%2036%20and%2038%20Eureka%20Street.pdf)).
Two years later, a sponsored article on UrbanToronto described Minto's Park & Main as "119
townhomes—81 traditional and 38 back-to-back units", "a stone's throw from the historic
Main Street Unionville", with construction starting in November 2024
([UrbanToronto, 4 October 2024](https://urbantoronto.ca/news/2024/10/minto-communities-breaks-ground-park-main-ushering-new-era-markham.57074)).

The file now holds 84 numbers on Anna Russell Way besides number 100, and 35 on John Canning
Way, which makes 119. The UrbanToronto piece names no street, and the Markham memo names no
project, so the matching count is suggestive, not proof. The record does not say what happened to the homes behind
the 45 retired numbers. Nor does it say whom the two streets are named after.

## A new street off Highway 7

Eight days earlier, on 6 October, 3912 and 3928 Highway 7 in Unionville came off the file.
The same day three numbers appeared on a street the file had never carried, Mayor Roman Street:
35, 37 and 39. Number 35 sits about 36 metres from where 3912 Highway 7 was.

Those two Highway 7 addresses are a known site. In 2022 yorkregion.com reported Markham's
approval of a "1,081-unit independent living retirement home complex" by Hildebrand Gardens,
with the address "3912 and 3928 Hwy. 7 E." and a height limit raised "from eight storeys to 14
storeys"
([yorkregion.com, 9 May 2022, via the City of Vaughan's clippings](https://c3.vaughan.ca/news/2022/05-May/clip05-09-11.html)).
The file does not say whether the three new numbers belong to that complex. A search turned up
nothing public on the street's name.

## Four streets in Stouffville

On 28 October, 106 addresses arrived in Whitchurch-Stouffville. Three streets were new:
Jadestone Drive with 43 numbers, Sandpiper Crescent with 28 and Bethridge Way with 18.
Harjones Avenue, which had carried four numbers since tracking began, got 17 more. The new
streets sit beside Elmer Avenue and Baker Hill Boulevard. 3 Bethridge Way is 169 metres from 5551
Bethesda Road. Sandpiper Crescent is not Sandpiper Key Way, a
different street that was already on the file.

In May 2024 Whitchurch-Stouffville council voted to draft-approve a plan of subdivision at
"5481 & 5551 Bethesda Sideroad" by Treasure Hill, file 19T(W)-22.001
([council minutes, 15 May 2024](https://whitchurch.civicweb.net/document/185067/)). The
location fits, but neither the minutes nor anything else found names the four streets, so the
record does not confirm that these are that plan's lots. The Town says its street names come
from an "Approved Street Name Inventory", and that memorial requests are not considered
([Town of Whitchurch-Stouffville](https://www.townofws.ca/work/development-and-land-use/planning-applications/street-naming-and-municipal-addressing)).
Nothing public explains Jadestone, Sandpiper, Bethridge or Harjones.

## Bethune Way reads in order now

The month's 16 renumberings were all on Bethune Way in Markham, near Kennedy Road and 14th
Avenue, and none of the points moved. The street has two short legs and a row across the top,
with 28 odd numbers. Until 14 October, one leg ran 1 to 15 going north. The top row then
started again at the far end, 17 to 39. The other leg ran 41 to 55 back down. On 14 October
the two legs swapped numbers: what had been 55 became 1, 53 became 3, and so on up to 41 and
15. Now 1 to 55 runs in one continuous order: up one leg, across the top and down the other.
The file does not say why, and a search found nothing.

Also on 14 October in Thornhill, the corner points 2 Harris Way and 2 Harper Way, 26 metres
apart, swapped street names without moving.

## The rest of the region

Newmarket added three more second suites: B-278 Herridge Circle, B-140 Meadowbank Road and
B-306 Salisbury Lane. Newmarket gives every registered additional unit the letter B
([Town of Newmarket](https://www.newmarket.ca/node/1233)). In East Gwillimbury 3440 Davis
Drive became 3440A, and a new 3440B appeared about 50 metres away. Richmond Hill gained 295
Highway 7 near Bayview Avenue, and 59 and 61 Lund Street. Aurora gained 109 Spruce Street,
and Georgina gained 35 Polva Promenade. Newmarket lost 480 Harry Walker Parkway with nothing
in its place. Apart from the Newmarket suites, a search found nothing public on any of these.

Next month: whether more numbers follow on John Canning Way and Mayor Roman Street.
