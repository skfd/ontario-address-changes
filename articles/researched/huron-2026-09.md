# Thirty-four units on Simcoe Street in Exeter, and eight lots on Blyth Road

On the 5 September snapshot, three new numbers appeared on **Simcoe Street** in Exeter,
between 41 and 49: **43 Simcoe Street** with Units 1 to 12, **45** with Units 1 to 12 and
**47** with Units 1 to 10. That is thirty-four addresses at once, two-thirds of everything
the County of Huron's file gained in September. In January a planning letter to South
Huron had described a vacant lot on Simcoe Street zoned for at most thirty-four units, and a
concept plan for thirty-four stacked townhouses in three buildings.

Fifty-two on a file of thirty-eight thousand is about a seventh of a percent. Thirty-four
of them are in South Huron, ten in Ashfield-Colborne-Wawanosh, four in Huron East, two in
Central Huron, and one each in Howick and Morris-Turnberry. Goderich, Bluewater and North
Huron added none. Thirty daily reads, none failed.

## 43, 45 and 47 Simcoe Street

No plain 43, 45 or 47 Simcoe Street was ever on the file: the units arrived without a
building number before them. They sit in three short rows. 43's twelve run north to south
along one side, 45's twelve run east to west along the north and 47's ten parallel to them
to the south, none of the rows more than about forty metres long. The nearest addresses on
other streets are 551 Albert Street, 85 metres away, then 520 Andrew Street and 82 Rowe
Avenue. The County numbered all thirty-four in one unbroken run of its internal record IDs.

The outside record has one document. On 28 January 2026 Baker Planning Group sent South
Huron a [minor-variance
application](https://www.southhuron.ca/media/lxlbdzhr/minor-variance-letter-simcoe-street-january-2026_cp_17592405312682.pdf)
on behalf of 1343877 Ontario Ltd. and Pol Quality Homes. It concerns a site with 15.1 metres
of frontage on Simcoe Street, "a remanent block from the adjacent Plan of Subdivision for
Pinnacle Quality Homes", and "currently vacant, with no buildings or structures". The
site-specific zoning caps it at thirty-four units, and the letter's concept plan "shows 34
stacked townhouse units and 62 off-street parking spaces" in "three (3) new residential
buildings, each 2 storeys to 2.5 storeys in height", mostly two-bedroom units with a
balcony. It also proposes a footpath from Albert Street to Simcoe Street. The variances it
asked for were smaller setbacks on three sides.

The letter gives no civic numbers, so the match is the count: thirty-four units in three
buildings, and thirty-four addresses under three numbers. Nothing public that this search
found records a decision on the variance, a site plan approval or a building permit. The
file shows that the addresses exist. It does not say whether anything has been built.

## Eight numbers on Blyth Road

On 26 September, eight new numbers appeared on **Blyth Road** in Ashfield-Colborne-Wawanosh:
34736, 34742, 34820, 34828, 34928, 34934, 35012 and 35020. They lie in a straight line along
the road in four close pairs, spread over about 260 metres. The same day, the point that had
been **34696 Blyth Road** since the tracker's first read in June came back as **35076**,
about 280 metres further east, under the same County record ID.

Real-estate listings since May have been selling "one of eight newly released country estate
lots" at 34696 Blyth Road, "less than 5km from Goderich". Each lot is "just shy of 1 acre",
131 by 246 feet, priced at $249,900, with access and hydro, and buyers put in their own well
and septic ([Lot 7](https://www.squareyards.ca/sale/on/huron/goderich/goderich-town/lot-7-34696-blyth-road),
listed 8 May; [Lot 6](https://www.keyhomes.ca/property/ontario/ashfield-colborne-wawanosh/29721559)).
The listings and the file both count eight. The County's consent decision for the
severance could not be found, so which lot each new number belongs to is the file's
business, not the record's.

## What the file did to itself

None of the month's eight "retirements" is an address leaving. This tracker tells Huron's
addresses apart by number, street, unit and position, so a point that moves or changes its
number counts as one record gone and another arriving. The County's own record ID stayed
the same through every one of these this month, which is how they can be traced:

- **203 Quebec Street** and **53 Wellesley Street** in Goderich moved 8 and 13 metres.
- **42918A Cranbrook Road** in Huron East lost its A and became 42918, on the same spot.
  Along with the Blyth Road move, that makes two renumberings the tracker does not count as such.
- **246 Matilda Street** in Central Huron became 246 Matilda Street Unit 1 on the same
  point, and a new Unit 2 appeared about 23 metres north.
- **42892 Cranbrook Road** and **43653** and **43757 Hydro Line Road** did not move at all.
  Their street-number field had been empty in the County's layer, for 42892 since the
  layer's schema change in late July, and it was filled back in on 5 and 12 September.

The other nine new addresses are single rural numbers, one each on Harper Line, River Line,
Browntown Road, Maple Line, Sawmill Road, Jamestown Road, Porters Hill Line and
Morris-Turnberry Road, plus 3089 Queen Street in Howick. The record is silent on them.

## What to watch

Whether a variance decision or a building permit for the Simcoe Street site turns up in
South Huron's records; whether the Blyth Road lots get anything more than numbers; and
whether the County finishes refilling the street numbers its July schema change left blank.

*How this is measured* — The tracker reads the County of Huron's published 911 address layer
daily and keeps a snapshot whenever the contents differ from the last one kept. September
2026 produced four snapshots (5, 12, 19 and 26 September). The month's figures are one
comparison between the last snapshot before the month (22 August) and the last one in it
(26 September). So anything the County changed between 22 August and the 5th is counted
here, and anything after the 26th belongs to October. Gross and net are equal this month: no
address arrived and left again inside it. Huron's identity for an address is number, street,
unit and rounded position, so a moved or renumbered point counts as one retirement and one
addition; all eight retirements above are that. Thirty daily reads, none failed. Each day's
report is on the site linked below.
