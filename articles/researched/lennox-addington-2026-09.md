# Two Creighton Drive lots gain an A and a B, and a Thorpe Road number moves down the road

On 2 September, 126 and 128 Creighton Drive in Loyalist Township each gained an A and a
B in the County of Lennox and Addington's address file. The four new records sit within
a few metres of the original addresses, and 126 and 128 are both still on file beside
them.

## By the numbers

The file grew by 12 addresses, about one in two thousand of the 26,160 it held at the
end of August. Seven of the new numbers are in Stone Mills and six in Loyalist. Nothing
was added in the other municipalities.

## Creighton Drive

Creighton Drive runs next to Dusenbury Drive and Sweetgrass Drive. Real-estate listings
place the street in
[Golden Haven, a Golden Falcon Homes community in Odessa](https://homli.ca/loyalist-real-estate/75-creighton-drive/X13653034),
and the builder's homes are
[listed on Dusenbury Drive](https://spotlight.century21.ca/odessa-real-estate/46-dusenbury-drive/unbranded)
too. Lettered numbers are not new to the street: 127A and 140A have been on file since
the tracker began in June.

What 126 and 128 now hold, the file does not say. No listing, permit or planning item
for either address turned up, so the public record is silent on why each lot has gained
an A and a B.

The same update gave **256 Dr Richard James Crescent** in Amherstview a 256A, a few
metres from 256, which is also still on file. Listings put the street in the
[Lakeside Ponds subdivision](https://www.rew.ca/properties/184-dr-richard-james-crescent-loyalist-on).
Nothing public turned up for the address.

## 1725 Thorpe Road becomes 1707

The month's one retirement was **1725 Thorpe Road** in Loyalist. It first appeared on
11 August, was last seen on 9 September, and by 17 September had been replaced by
**1707 Thorpe Road**. The two records have the same coordinates, to five decimal places.
The new number sits between 1700 and 1713, while the old one sat between 1713 and 1815.

In the world that is a renumbering. In this file it can only show as a retirement and an
addition. The county's layer carries no stable record ID, so the tracker identifies each
point by its address, and a new number means a new record. A search turned up no notice
about Thorpe Road, so the public record is silent on why the point was renumbered after
five weeks.

## The rest of the month

The other six were single new numbers on rural roads in Stone Mills. They were 999 and
1001 County Road 14, 1663 Centreville Road and 306 Main Street on 1 September, then
324 Moscow Road on the 9th, 218 Richmond Point Lane on the 23rd and 4766 County Road 4
on the 25th. The new 306 Main Street is in Stone Mills, not to be confused with
306 Main Street in Odessa, which has been on file since June. Nothing public turned up
for any of them.

*How this is measured*: The tracker reads the County of Lennox and Addington's published
civic-address layer every day and keeps a snapshot whenever the contents differ from the
last one kept. September 2026 produced six snapshots, from 1 to 25 September. The month's
figures compare the 25 September snapshot with the last one before the month (27 August),
so changes after 25 September belong to October. The source has no stable record ID, so
the tracker identifies each address by its full text. A renumbered address therefore
counts as one retirement plus one addition, and the tracker records no renumberings for
this county. The daily sum of changes equals the month's net: nothing was added and then
retired inside the month. The tracker made thirty daily reads and none failed. Each day's
