# Seventy-two numbers in Midhurst in one morning

On 2 September the County of Simcoe's address file gained 72 numbers on three streets in
Springwater: all of **Twinleaf Avenue**, a street the file had never carried, 2 to 38;
**117 to 147 Bearberry Road**; and the odd numbers **51 to 75 Periwinkle Road**. Nearly half
of the county's whole month arrived in that one update.

## By the numbers

| | |
|---|---|
| New addresses | 148 |
| Retired | 0 |
| New streets | 2 (Twinleaf Avenue, Rudolph Court) |
| Houses relabelled Unit A as new units joined them | 10, all in Orillia |
| Renumbered in place | 2 |
| Points moved more than 100 m | 2 |
| Total on file at month end | 190,417 |

That is fewer than one address in a thousand across the eighteen municipalities whose
addressing the County keeps, and not one left the file.

## Twinleaf Avenue

The 72 came in as one batch: their record ids run unbroken from 248548 to 248619. They
also share one roll number, which the file had already put on 75 addresses on Sweet
Cicely Street, 11 on Periwinkle and 7 on Honeysuckle Court. That makes it a block of
land the assessment roll has not yet divided into lots. The County splits a parent roll
into per-lot numbers later; it did that on 21 September for a different Springwater
block, around Switchgrass Avenue, but not yet for this one.

The streets are named for plants, as their neighbours are: Wild Ginger Lane, Sassafras
Road, Calypso Avenue. In the file, 2 Twinleaf Avenue is 11 metres from 65 Sweet Cicely
Street, and a listing for 34 Sweet Cicely
[describes a Brookfield Residential home in Midhurst Valley](https://www.greatertorontohomepros.com/property-search/detail/211/S13744490/34-sweet-cicely-st-springwater-on-l9x-2c7/).
Midhurst Valley is
[a master-planned community built by a partnership of Brookfield, CountryWide Homes, Geranium and Sundance Homes](https://businessviewmagazine.com/civil-municipal/dec-2021/95/),
whose first phase launched in 2021. No naming by-law or subdivision item for Twinleaf
Avenue turned up, and no web page mentions it at all yet.

## Rudolph Court, Marchmont

On 16 September a second new street arrived in Severn: **Rudolph Court**, 22 numbers from
1822 to 2100. Those are rural-style numbers, about 13 apart, so the lots are large. In the
file the street sits between Millwood Road and Town Line, near Highway 12. A listing for
1914 Rudolph Court calls it
[Lot 9 in Glen Oak Estates, "a new estate subdivision in Marchmont"](https://www.keyhomes.ca/property/ontario/severn/29789838),
with a drilled well, a septic system and possession at the end of 2026. The listing went
up around June, so the lots were for sale about three months before they had addresses.

All 22 came in under the roll number of 3879 Town Line, the property next door. On
28 September, ten of them got roll numbers of their own.

## Orillia's lettered houses

Ten Orillia houses went from a plain number to *Unit A* during the month, and each got
new lettered neighbours on the same spot. The record id stayed the same each time, so
each house was relabelled, not replaced. The busiest day was 25 September.
**29 Albert Street North** and **57 Dunlop Street** each became Units A to D.
**165 Barrie Road** became A to C. 95 Albert Street North, 3145 Emperor Drive,
264 Diana Drive and 248 Mississaga Street West each became A and B. Earlier in the
month, 437 Peter Street North, 208 Isabella Drive and 3137 Emperor Drive did the same.
That adds up to fifteen new units.

The City's own guide says
[Orillia allows up to four dwelling units on a lot](https://www.orillia.ca/media/ltcnybfy/additional-dwelling-unit-information-guide-quick-guide.pdf),
and two of these lots now carry four. The file does not say whether any of the units has
been built. Elsewhere the County did it differently: five Bradford West Gwillimbury
houses, on Trailside Drive, Rowe Street, Vasey Road, Montrose Boulevard and Empire Drive,
each gained a *Unit B* while keeping their plain numbers.

In Coldwater, John Street gained **17**, then **19, 21 and 23**, each as an A and a B, on
12 September. That is seven rows on seven consecutive roll numbers. No plain 19, 21 or 23
was ever on file. A listing for 16 John Street, a door along, places the street
["in picturesque Coldwater"](https://www.wahi.com/ca/en/real-estate/on/simcoe/severn/16-john-st-severn-l0k1e0-ontario-canada).
Nothing public was found on what is being built there.

## What the file itself did

In Adjala-Tosorontio, 23 Sandhill Crescent became **62 Bayberry Drive** on 9 September.
The point and roll number stayed the same, and 62 carries on Bayberry's run of even
numbers from 42 to 60. In Ramara, 3379 County Road 47 became **2126 Concession Road 3**
on the 15th, with its point shifted 21 metres. On 28 September 5577 Upper Big Chute Road
in Severn moved 170 metres, and **1808 Thickson Road** appeared exactly where it used to
be, under the same roll number. In Ramara, 3986 Melrose Drive moved 211 metres on the
12th. The public record is silent on all four.
