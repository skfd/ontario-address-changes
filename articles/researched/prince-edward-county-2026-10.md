# Twelve new addresses in the County, and six French Street houses get their street

Prince Edward County's address file gained twelve addresses in October, four of them on
**Big Island Road**. On 17 October it also filled a gap in Picton: **50, 52, 54, 56, 58
and 60 French Street** had been on the file since August with a house number and no
street name, and that day they gained "French Street". Sale listings place the street in
West Meadows, a Picton subdivision.

## By the numbers

| | |
|---|---|
| New address points | 26 (12 addresses new to the file, two points each; 2062 Big Island Road re-pointed) |
| Retired points | 4 (281 Arthur Road the one address gone) |
| Renumbered | 2 |
| Street renames | 0 |
| Address splits | 0 |
| Total points on file at month end | 27,597 (+22) |

The County's file usually keeps two points per address, one on the building and one at
the lot or the road, so 27,597 points stand for about 16,400 addresses. Twelve new
addresses in a month is a small month, all of it published between 2 and 28 October
after a September in which the file never changed.

## Six numbers on French Street find their street

The six even numbers from 50 to 60 French Street sit in a row about seven metres apart,
across the street from 49 to 61. Until 17 October each one carried a number and an empty
street field. Now they read French Street, and only one point in the whole file is still
without a street name. Their nearest neighbours on the file are 78 Griffin Street, 20
metres away, and 75 George Wright Boulevard.

Listings for [49 French Street](https://www.zoocasa.com/picton-on-real-estate/49-french-st)
and [15 French Street](https://ned-burgess.c21.ca/listing/ON/Prince-Edward-County-Picton/15-French-Street-K0K-2T0/194143481)
call it West Meadows, by Port Picton Homes. The County's file for the
[West Meadows subdivision](https://www.thecounty.ca/13-t-18-501-west-meadows/) (13-T-18-501)
describes land south of Talbot Street, Hallowell ward, with 245 homes planned in total. It
names no streets, so the link runs through the listings. Nothing public says why these six
points went two months without a street.

## Big Island Road and the county roads

On 17 October 2002, 2026 and 2028 **Big Island Road** arrived within about 300 metres of
one another. **2062 Big Island Road**, already on file, had its two points replaced by two
new ones within 20 metres. The nearest other street on the file is County Road 15, almost
900 metres off. The rest of the new numbers are scattered across the county: 2650 County
Road 13, 3098 County Road 3, 1611 County Road 15, 128 County Road 20, 2220 County Road 4
and 55 Mowbray Road. A search turned up sale listings on Big Island Road and nothing else.
The public record does not say what is being built at any of them.

## Walmsley gets an Extension

On 17 October the two points of **130 Walmsley Road** became **130 Walmsley Extension**,
and a new **91 Walmsley Extension** appeared about 130 metres away. It is the first time
the file has used *Extension* on Walmsley. **186 Walmsley Road** had arrived on the 2nd.
No naming by-law or notice for Walmsley Extension turned up. The file does not say whether
this is a new stretch of road or a new label for an old one.

## Lake Avenue Lane

On 20 October **37 and 38 Lake Avenue Lane** moved 159 metres. A new **59 Lake Avenue
Lane** arrived the same day, one of its two points a few metres from where 37 and 38 now
sit. The County has an approved site-plan file, [SP-06-21, for the Lake Avenue RV Resort &
Campground](https://www.thecounty.ca/sp-06-21-lake-avenue-rv-resort-campground/). It amends
a 2016 agreement whose works were not finished on time, its latest submission is dated
24 April 2026, and its drawing set is filed as "37 Lake Avenue". Nothing in it explains
the move or the new number.

## The small edits

**80 Talbot Street** in Picton became **92 Talbot Street** at the same spot. On Arthur
Road, 281 left the file: one of its points was renumbered 286 and replaced 286's own
point, and the other was retired. 121 Harbard *Lane* became Harbard *Road*. At 60 and 62
Bowery Street the "B" moved from the number to the unit field, so each now reads Unit A
and Unit B. A retired or renumbered address is a record changing, not a building coming
down, and nothing public on any of them says why.
