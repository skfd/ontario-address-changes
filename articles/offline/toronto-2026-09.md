# Science Centre Station is Don Valley Station now, and Toronto's file shrank for the first time

On 15 September 2026 the point at **1175 Eglinton Avenue East** stopped being labelled
*Science Centre Station* in Toronto's address file and started being labelled **Don Valley
Station**. In the same snapshot two neighbouring points, **817 Don Mills Road** and **6 Gervais
Drive**, carried the name *Don Valley Station* for the first time. Nothing else about the three
points changed.

It was also the first month since these articles began in which the file ended smaller than it
started: 105 fewer records on 30 September than on 27 August. Most of that is the file tidying
which record it keeps for an address. About fifty addresses actually left.

The run so far: +28 in March, +29 in April, +19 in May, +15 in June, +12 in July, +7 in August,
and now −105 records, or about −50 addresses once the swaps are netted out.

## A station's new name

*Science Centre Station* had been on 1175 Eglinton Avenue East since our first snapshot in April
2025, and on no other point. On the 15th that label was replaced, and by the end of the month no
point in the file carried it. The point itself is still classed as *reserved*, the file's word for
a number that has been assigned but is not yet in service.

The two points that gained the name sit to the north: 817 Don Mills Road is 67 metres from 1180
Eglinton Avenue East, and 6 Gervais Drive 131 metres from it. On the same day 6 Gervais Drive was
reclassed from a *structure* to a *structure entrance*. The file records the new name in three
places; it does not say why the old one went.

## Thirteen doors on Carlaw Avenue

Between the snapshots of 11 and 14 September, **439 to 469 Carlaw Avenue** came off the file:
thirteen odd numbers, 439, 445, 447 and every odd number from 449 to 463, then 467 and 469. All
thirteen were classed as *structure entrances* rather than as lots, and nine of them carried
consecutive identifiers, issued as one batch. 471 Carlaw Avenue, 48 metres from 469 and classed
as the structure itself, is still on file, and so is the even side of the street opposite.

A run of entrance points issued together and removed together has the shape of an administrative
act, not of a block coming down; a demolition takes whatever points happen to stand on it. The file
says only that the thirteen doors are no longer recorded. In the same snapshot, the label *Gerrard
- Carlaw Parkette* came off 855 Gerrard Street East, 121 metres from 439 Carlaw. The file does not
connect the two.

Carlaw was the largest of 19 entrance points retired in September. The others were 194A, 196
and 198 Neville Park Boulevard (off on 24 September), 920 and 924A St Clair Avenue West (15
September) and 166 Alberta Avenue (15 September).

## 194 numbers went into service

The busiest days of the month, 15, 25, 28 and 29 September, were dominated by one kind of
edit: a reserved number becoming *regular*. 194 did so over the month. They were spread thin:
no street had more than seven (Florence Avenue), and a quarter were in Etobicoke-Lakeshore. Every
one had been on the file as reserved since at least September 2025, and 178 of them since our
first snapshot.

In 55 cases an older regular point for the same address came off in the same edit, the swap
August's article described at 355 Church Street. The label travels when it does: 372 Briar Hill
Avenue's old point, labelled *Briar Hill Parkette*, came off on 28 September, and the point that
went regular in its place picked the name up. At **954–970 Scarlett Road**, the one row that
described the whole range came off on 15 September as 960 Scarlett Road went regular.

Not every reserved number made it. Five that never went into service came off together on 16
September: **50, 52, 54 and 56 Lavender Road** and **29 Lowell Avenue**. With them went reserved
second points at 48 Lavender Road and 46 and 48 Cree Avenue, whose regular points remain. Lavender
Road is in York South-Weston and Lowell and Cree avenues in Scarborough Southwest, but the eight
carried identifiers from one unbroken run, 30026322 to 30026334. Caribou Road (188 and 190),
Holloway Road (37 and 39) and 190 Neville Park Boulevard lost reserved numbers the same way.

## Three new lane names, one new point

Two of September's three new street names arrived on points that were already there. On 3
September, **363R Euclid Avenue**, a rear address, became **168 Palmira Lane**, 39 metres from
507 College Street. The same day **189R Logan Avenue** became **32 Sunday Mews**, 25 metres from
185 Logan Avenue. Same identifiers, same coordinates; only the address changed.

The third is new outright: **51 Ciamaga Lane** appeared on 14 September, 17 metres from 89 Barton
Avenue.

## Where the new numbers are

**2 Northampton Drive** became 2A, 2B and 2C on 14 September, five metres from 102 Shaver Avenue
North. No plain 2 was ever on the file, and the three arrived regular. The other three splits
added reserved A and B suffixes beside an original that is still there: **349 Melrose Street**,
**55 Bywood Drive** and **917 Victoria Park Avenue**.

On 3 September **784 and 786 Spadina Road** arrived reserved, with a second point at 782, all
within a few metres of one another. Also on the 3rd, **890 Dundas Street West** became **888**, its point moving
about seventeen metres, and **188 Claremont Street** appeared four metres from it. 134 Magnolia
Avenue became 132 Magnolia Avenue, the month's one plain renumbering.

## Labels

Besides the station, *Morningside Library* came off 255 Morningside Avenue on 23 September; the
same label is still on file at 4279 Lawrence Avenue East. 401 College Street was labelled
*Kensington Community School*, 77 Gough Avenue *Carpark 137*, and 7 Ashtonbee Road gained the
longest label of the month, *Enbridge meter/regulator station and electrical/generator building*.

## What a shrinking month means

A smaller file is not a smaller city. Of the 129 records that left, nearly half were second
records for addresses still there, and ten more were numbers that had only ever been reserved.
What September mostly shows is the file putting a long backlog of reserved numbers into
service and, in the same pass, clearing out entrance points and duplicates it no longer keeps.
Whether October continues the clear-out, the file will say in a month.

### How this is measured

Every day a snapshot of Toronto's Address Points dataset is pulled and diffed against the one
before it. This piece covers the stretch between the snapshots of **27 August 2026** and
**30 September 2026**, seventeen snapshots in all.

Counts are **net**: an address added and retired within the month nets to nothing. The daily
edits add up to almost the same figures this month (184 status changes against 181 net), so
little was done and undone. A "retired" record is a row leaving the file, which in 63 cases meant
another record for the same address remained, and we have said so where it did. Coordinate changes
under 50 metres are dropped as noise because the City's export oscillates between two geocode
sets; twelve moves cleared that floor in September, the largest 251 metres at 20 Four Winds
Drive. No events were held back for review this month.
