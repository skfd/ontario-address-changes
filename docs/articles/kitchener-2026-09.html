Three Rosenberg streets fill in, and 265 units at 20 Cameron Street South go Active

On the 21 September snapshot, 122 numbered addresses appeared at once on three streets in southwest Kitchener: 59 on Eskerwood Way, 47 on Taverners Crescent and 16 on Mihai Eminescu Way. Until then the City's file had held only a handful of unnumbered placeholder points on those streets. All three are drawn on a plan of subdivision that a provincial tribunal approved in 2020. Downtown, the 265 apartments at 20 Cameron Street South went from Pending to Active in the first week of the month.

189 on a file of 132,000 is about a seventh of a percent. Ward 5 took 146 of the 191 additions. Daily edits added up to almost exactly the same as the month's outcome, so nothing was added and then taken away again. Thirty daily reads, none failed.

Eskerwood, Taverners and Mihai Eminescu

All 122 arrived on the same day, all already Active, all in Ward 5 and all on a single City parcel. Eskerwood Way runs 200 to 317, Taverners Crescent 5 to 80 and Mihai Eminescu Way 6 to 36. From 201 Eskerwood Way, the nearest addresses on other streets are 17 Taverners Crescent (8 metres), 835 Amand Drive, Sandymount Street and Gehl Place, under 100 metres away. Rosenberg Way is just under 300 metres away.

The outside record names the project. In February 2020 the Local Planning Appeal Tribunal issued its decision on Stamm Investments Limited's appeal over roughly 43 hectares at 186 Gehl Place, "within the Rosenburg Community". Stamm had appealed after the City refused or failed to decide on the zoning and the draft plan of subdivision, 30T-14201. The parties settled at the January hearing and the Tribunal granted draft approval. The attached plan labels Eskerwood Way, Taverners Crescent and Mihai Eminescu Way. Its revision table records "addition of Mihai Eminescu Way" in September 2016, and the conditions require the plan to be registered in stages. The decision does not say when any stage would be registered, and nothing this search found says which stage these 122 numbers belong to.

No Kitchener record that this search found says why the street carries the name.

Cosens Crescent, new to the file

A day later, on 22 September, a new street name entered the file: Cosens Crescent, with 22 numbers from 101 to 130. These are still Pending. They sit about 360 metres east of the Eskerwood cluster, 17 metres from 243 Rosenberg Way and 61 metres from 154 Forestwalk Street. The name does not appear in the Stamm plan, and no search turned up a by-law, a plan or a listing for it. The public record is silent on Cosens Crescent for now.

265 apartments at 20 Cameron Street South

Since the tracker's first read in June, the file had carried 265 unit records at 20 Cameron Street South, floors 1 to 24 with no floor numbered 13, every one Pending. On 4 September, 103 of them became Active, and the other 162 followed on the 7th. Those 265 make up nearly all of the month's 282 Pending-to-Active changes.

The file puts 20 Cameron Street South on the same City parcel as 475 King Street East, whose 218 units have been Active since June. 475 King Street East is Vertikal, Drewlo Holdings' two-tower rental project: towers of 19 and 22 storeys on a four-level podium, 488 units in all. On a SkyriseCities forum thread that follows the project, posters labelled photos "Cameron Tower" and "Madison Tower" in August 2025. They reported "Tower 1 has been completed" in November 2025 and said the Cameron Tower "still needs all of its cladding" that December. On 21 September 2026 they wrote that its exterior is "mostly complete now". The file does not say what Active means for a unit, and the forum is people watching from the street, not the developer. Still, the two records point the same way: the second tower's addresses went Active this month.

One of the month's two retirements is on the same parcel. 481 King Street East was on the file as Active until the 20 August snapshot and was gone by 4 September. Nothing public says why. The other retirement was an unnumbered placeholder point on Nelson Avenue.

A plaza changes streets

On 4 September a new address, 1225 Homer Watson Boulevard, appeared on the same parcel and under the same tax roll number as 535 Manitou Drive. On the 4th and the 7th, 19 unit records that had been filed under 535 Manitou Drive moved across to it, keeping their City record IDs. Units 101 to 117 became 101 to 117, 1225 Homer Watson Boulevard, and two lettered units became A and B there. 535 Manitou Drive itself stays on the file. Lease listings describe 535 Manitou as a newly developed retail plaza at the corner of Manitou Drive and Homer Watson Boulevard. Nothing public says why its units now take the Homer Watson address.

Smaller items

  • 114 Madison Avenue South gained Units 1 to 10 on 4 September. The plain 114 is still on file. Listings describe two-bedroom townhouse units there, built in 2026. Eleven other addresses also gained units, among them 1265 Ottawa Street South and 95 Franklin Street North (four each) and 31 Albion Street (three). The rest gained a Unit 1 and Unit 2.
  • 24 Charles Street West spent the second half of August numbered 26 on the file, while a separate record already held 26 Charles Street West. On 4 September it went back to 24. That accounts for one of the month's two renumberings.
  • Bleams Road: a point that was an unnumbered "Bleams Rd" in June and 2200 from August became 2201 on 4 September, in the same spot. The file does not say why.

How this is measured — The tracker compares the City's address file between snapshots. This month's figures are one comparison, from the last snapshot before September (20 August) to the last one in it (23 September). Changes the City published after 23 September will appear in October's piece. A "retired" address is a record leaving the file, not necessarily a building coming down, and Pending and Active are the City's own status words, which the file does not define.
