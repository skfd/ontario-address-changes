126 numbers in one day on five Paris streets at Losani's Riverbank Estates

On 31 October 2026 the County of Brant's address file gained 126 numbers in a single update, all in Paris and all on five streets that were already there: Gort Avenue, Herriot Street, Blackman Crescent, Summerwind Drive and Gladys Stephens Court. Herriot Street went from four numbers to 48. The rest of October was a handful of rural additions and three new Unit 2s.

Two entries appear in both lists, explained below, so October was 136 numbers new to the file and two real removals. Nine in ten of the new numbers arrived on the month's last day.

Five streets, one update

None of the five streets is new to the file; all of them had addresses when this tracker first read the file in June. What October did was fill them in. Gort Avenue gained 46 numbers (20 to 50, and the odd side from 81 to 131), Herriot Street 44, Blackman Crescent 18, Summerwind Drive 12 and Gladys Stephens Court six. Between them the five streets went from 142 numbers to 268. The new points cover about half a kilometre from south to north, from 1 Gladys Stephens Court up to 131 Gort Avenue, 67 metres from 120 Summerwind Drive. At the eastern edge, 5 Herriot Street is 51 metres from 18 Barker Street.

Sales listings put three of the streets in a Losani Homes community. A realtor's page for Losani's Parkview Towns puts them at "88 Gort Avenue (Corner of Gort Ave & Summerwind Dr)", "part of the Parkview Collection at Riverbank Estates", 37 units; a second release is also on Gort Avenue near Summerwind Drive. On Herriot Street, number 2 was listed in 2024 as a "brand new Bordeaux Farmhouse Riverbank Losani Home". The file had 2 Herriot from the start; its neighbours from 5 to 54 only arrived on 31 October.

The County's civic addressing by-law says addresses in a new subdivision are assigned before development and "confirmed at the time when the draft plan of the development is registered". Nothing in the County's agendas or notices that turned up names these streets, so the public record does not say what happened on the 31st. Nor does it say who Gladys Stephens was.

Three letters on Markel Drive

On 24 October 60 Markel Drive in Paris gained Units A, A2 and B, placed 30 to 40 metres from the main address, which stays on the file. This is not the "Unit 2" numbering the by-law gives a second dwelling, and the file does not say what the three units are. The nearest other addresses are 75 Hutchings Road, 200 metres away, and 950 Rest Acres Road.

The rest of the month

Three houses gained a Unit 2: 18 Whitton Drive and 84 Hitchman Street in Paris, and 57 Howell Road in the former Township of South Dumfries. The by-law gives that number to an "ARU or similar secondary unit". One went the other way: 180 Scott Avenue's Unit 2 left the file on 23 October, and 180 itself remains. New rural numbers went to 80 McBay Road, 315 West Quarter Townline Road, 296 Second Concession Road and 72 Fourth Concession Road.

The two entries in both lists are bookkeeping. 82 McBay Road's point moved 26 metres on 13 October, the day 80 McBay appeared beside it, and its place name changed from Former Township of Brantford to Former Township of Brantford Township. This tracker tells Brant's points apart partly by position, so the move shows up as one address leaving and one arriving. 356 East Quarter Townline Road appeared on 22 October at exactly the spot where an unnumbered point had been: a street-only marker given a number. The other removal is also an unnumbered point, near Second Concession Road. It left a day after 296 Second Concession appeared 195 metres away. The file does not say they are the same.

What to watch

Whether Gort Avenue's remaining gaps fill in the same way, and whether October's Unit 2s, and September's, appear in the County's monthly building permit reports.

How this is measured: the tracker reads the County of Brant's published address layer daily and keeps a snapshot whenever its contents change. October 2026 produced seven snapshots (13, 17, 22, 23, 24, 30 and 31 October). The month's figures are one comparison between the last snapshot before the month (23 September) and the last one in it (31 October). Gross and net are equal: nothing was added and then removed within the month. Brant publishes no stable row id, so a point that moves counts as one retirement and one addition.
