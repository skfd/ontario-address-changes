Two finished condo buildings on Charlton Avenue East finally get their apartments on file

On 10 September 2026 the City of Hamilton's address file gave 467 Charlton Avenue East and 479 Charlton Avenue East fifty-four units each, numbered 101 to 606 across six floors. These are not new buildings. They belong to Vista 3 Condos on Charlton, which a listing site gives as built in 2020, and units in them were being sold under these unit addresses before the file had them.

The table counts what the file did, and the file overstates both columns by 46: that many addresses left and came straight back, moved or respelled (see below). Strip those out and the month is 158 genuinely new addresses and one genuine retirement, in a file of about 274,000.

108 apartments on Charlton Avenue East

Vista 3 has three street numbers. The third, 455 Charlton Avenue East, already had its 54 units on file when the tracker started reading Hamilton in June. Its two sisters had only their building addresses until 10 September, when all 108 units arrived together. The building page lists it as 6 floors and 54 units, which is exactly what each address received.

The paperwork for the site goes back further. The City's By-law 17-203, passed on 27 September 2017, lifted the holding provision from the multiple-dwelling zoning on 467 Charlton Avenue East. And a listing for unit 203 at 467 went up on 15 July 2026, eight weeks before 203 – 467 Charlton Avenue East existed in the City's address file. The file does not say why two of the three buildings waited. It is a reminder that the file records paperwork reaching the City's address layer, which can trail the world by years.

Thirty-one more units at Rocky Ridge

On 14 September 1085 Concession 10 West, in rural Flamborough near Highway 6, gained 31 units: 40 to 45, 80, 96, and 108 to 131. Seventy-one other units were already on file. Real-estate listings describe the address as Rocky Ridge Estates, a "year round land lease community" selling new bungalows on leased lots. The listings give the postal code L0R 1K0; the City's file gives N0B 2J0. Neither says which is current.

Small buildings, and one address gone

Seven more addresses gained units: four at 567 Scenic Drive, beside Upper Paradise Road, three each at 27 Millen Avenue and 1565 Rymal Road East, and two each at 113 Erin Avenue, 211 Cochrane Road, 1569 Rymal Road East and 31 Brenlyn Court. Every original address is still there. 35 Frid Street, near 45 Frid and Main Street West, arrived on 7 September. Searches found nothing public on any of them.

The month's one real retirement is 552 Barton Street in Stoney Creek, which left on 7 September. 26 Hewitson Road sits seven metres from where its point was, with the same postal code. The file does not say whether the two were one property, and the public record is silent.

What the file itself did

The other 46 "retirements" came back in the same update:

  • 85 Bonaventure Drive had units written B 1 to C 8, with a space. On 23 September they became B1 to C8.
  • 75 Berkindale Drive had units 1 to 13. On 29 September they became A to M.
  • 393 Rymal Road West, its eleven units and 391 next door all moved about 220 to 240 metres west on 7 September. Numbers, units and postal code stayed the same.
  • Four single points moved 24 to 71 metres: 2255 Barton Street East, 279 Nash Road North, 561 Concession 5 West and 3 Nisbet Boulevard.

The tracker treats a respelled unit or a moved point as one address leaving and another arriving. None of them is a building coming or going.

How this is measured — The tracker reads the City of Hamilton's published address layer daily and keeps a snapshot whenever the contents differ. September 2026 produced seven snapshots, from 7 to 29 September. The month's figures compare the last snapshot before the month (31 August) with the last one in it (29 September), so the 30th belongs to October. Gross activity matched net exactly: 204 additions and 47 removals, with nothing added and undone inside the month. There were thirty daily reads and none failed.
