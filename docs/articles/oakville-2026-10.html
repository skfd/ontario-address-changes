Eclipse Avenue arrives with 52 new numbers off Post Road, and six Bronte numbers become one

On 28 October 2026 Oakville's address file gained a street it had never held: Eclipse Avenue, with 29 numbers. The same update added 23 numbers to Post Road, just around the corner. Earlier in the month six neighbouring numbers on Lakeshore Road West in Bronte, 2365 to 2377, came off the file, and one new number, 2375, took their place.

The tracker's raw count is bigger than the month was. 44 of the 113 "new" addresses are also among the 53 "retired" ones, for a reason explained below. Take them out and October is 69 additions and 9 removals in a file of seventy-one thousand. Most of the additions came in one update.

Eclipse Avenue and the end of Post Road

All 52 additions on 28 October sit in one block. Eclipse Avenue's numbers run 52 to 114, even side only. Post Road's new numbers run 3529 to 3579, odd side only, and pick up where the file's existing run stopped at 3525. 114 Eclipse Avenue is 50 metres from 3529 Post Road, and 52 Eclipse is about 110 metres from 40 Burnhamthorpe Road East.

What is on those lots, the file does not say. Listing sites already have pages for 3561 and 3573 Post Road, both describing three-storey row townhouses. Neither page gives a date or names a builder.

The street name has no public record either. Oakville does not name new streets by by-law. Under its street-naming procedure the Town and the developer agree on a name and a Town director makes the final call, so there is no council vote to find. A web search turns up no other Eclipse Avenue in Oakville. The record is silent on where the name comes from and which plan of subdivision the street belongs to.

2365 to 2377 Lakeshore Road West becomes 2375

2365, 2367, 2369, 2371, 2373 and 2377 Lakeshore Road West were on the file on 25 September. By the 7 October update all six were gone, and 2375, a number the file had never carried, appeared between where they had been. It sits about 18 metres from the old 2377. The neighbours on either side, 2361 and 2381, did not change.

The Town has a site plan application on file for that block: Graywood Bronte Village, 2365 to 2377 Lakeshore Road West (file 1729.075/01), described as a "seven storey mid-rise building containing 618 m2 of retail space and 157 residential condominium units". Listing sites carry the project as Claystone, at 2375 Lakeshore Rd W. The Town's page gives no status or decision date. Nothing public found says when the buildings on the old numbers came down, or whether they have. The file records only six numbers ending and one beginning.

Three numbers that changed on the spot

Three points stayed where they were and got a new number:

  • 234 Cardinal Drive became 236 on 9 October. The street's other numbers there are 230, 231, 235, 238 and 239.
  • 2485 Plymouth Drive became 2845 on 21 October, at exactly the same coordinates. Its neighbours are 2830, 2860, 2871 and 2880, so the new number sits in its block's range and the old one did not. 2485 had been on the file since August. The file does not say why the number changed.
  • 208 Tilford Road became 210 on 30 October. The street's other numbers near it are 205 and 216.

The tracker counts each of these as one address retired and one added, because the number is part of how it tells addresses apart. They are the only October retirements apart from the six on Lakeshore Road West.

44 dots that moved a few metres

On 9 October 43 addresses left the file and came straight back with the same number, street and unit, each dot moved by less than seven metres. A 44th, 133 East Street, did the same on the 7th. Most are on John McKay Boulevard (3014 to 3079) and Dartmouth Crescent (1162 to 1174), with a few on Wheat Boom Drive, Milland Drive, Robert Lamb Boulevard and Meadowridge Drive. Oakville's layer has no stable record number, so the tracker tells addresses apart by number, street, unit and position, and a moved dot reads as one address gone and one arrived. It happened to seven addresses in September as well.

Twelve of them are units at 3070 John McKay Boulevard and 1220 Dartmouth Crescent. Their labels changed from "UNIT 12" to "Unit 12" when they moved, and that is why the tracker counted two address splits. Both buildings have had those units on file since tracking began in June. One small typo went with the moves: 1082 Wheat Boom Drive had been on file as "1082WHEAT BOOM DR", with no space, and now has one. Nothing public says why the points were moved.

The rest of the month

The other genuine additions were single numbers. Three filled gaps on Maverick Way (1179, 1187, 1195), and there were new numbers on Wolverine Path, Wrybill Path, Dove Drive, William Cutmore Boulevard, Littlefield Crescent, Badger Crescent and Randall Street. Last month's new streets got one more number each: 3240 John McKay Boulevard and 1166 Bronwyn Road, both on 30 October. 500 Burnhamthorpe Road East arrived on the 24th on its own, with no other address on file near it, and nothing public says what it is.

3413, the number that arrived in September in the William Cutmore run with no street name, still had none on the 30 October update.

What to watch

Whether Eclipse Avenue's odd side follows; whether anything turns up at 2375 Lakeshore Road West beyond the single number; and whether 3413 gets its street.

How this is measured — The tracker reads the Town of Oakville's published address layer daily and keeps a snapshot whenever the contents differ from the last one kept. October 2026 produced seven snapshots (7, 8, 9, 21, 24, 28 and 30 October). The month's figures are one comparison between the last snapshot before the month (25 September) and the last one in it (30 October), so edits after the 30th belong to November. Gross and net are equal this month: nothing was added and then removed inside it. The "genuinely new" and "genuinely gone" counts pair each addition with a retirement carrying the same number, street and unit. 30 daily reads, none failed.
