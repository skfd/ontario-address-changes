Brantford in October: one new number at Northland Gardens, three gone from Grand River Avenue

On 24 October, Brantford's address file added unit 63 at 50 Hayhurst Road. Until then the units there ran 62, then 64 to 71. On the same day the points for 37 other units in that complex moved slightly on the map. Five days later 238 Grand River Avenue appeared and 220, 256 and 260 Grand River Avenue came off the file. Two addresses arrived and three left, and those five are all of October's real change. Everything else in the numbers is points being moved.

Why 55 is really 2

Brantford's address layer gives its rows no permanent ID. So this tracker identifies an address by its number, street, unit and position. Move a point and the old record appears to leave while a new one arrives, even when the address itself has not changed. In October, 53 of the 55 "new" addresses had the same number, street, unit, postal code and street type as an address that left the same day. Only the location changed, by 2 to 46 metres.

The tracker's daily reports also count four "splits" this month: 332 North Park Street, 50 Hayhurst Road, 56 Memorial Drive and 68 Memorial Drive, each with nine or ten units. These are moves too. Every unit except 63 was already on file, and so was each building's plain address. Neither has changed.

Northland Gardens

Those four addresses are one place. The City's community housing options page lists Northland Gardens at 56–68 Memorial Dr., 332 North Park St. and 50 Hayhurst. The province's designated housing projects regulation names the same addresses. Its units are numbered across the whole complex rather than building by building: 1 to 10 at 332 North Park, 11 to 20 at 56 Memorial, 53 to 61 at 68 Memorial and 62 to 71 at 50 Hayhurst.

On 24 October all 37 unit points the file already had there moved. The Memorial Drive and Hayhurst points moved 2 to 8 metres and the North Park points 3 to 46 metres. In the same update, unit 63 filled the gap on Hayhurst. Nothing public says why the file was redrawn that day, or why 63 was missing until then. One gap remains: 56 Memorial Drive's units run 11, 12, then 14 to 20, and there has been no unit 13 in the file at any point since tracking began in June.

Grand River Avenue, 200 to 300

The 29 October update dealt with one stretch of Grand River Avenue, the blocks around Leonard and Holme Streets. Sixteen addresses there, from 200 to 300, moved by between 2 and 34 metres. Three numbers that had been on file since tracking began in June were retired: 220, 256 and 260. One number was added: 238 Grand River Avenue, in postal code N3T 4Y4. Its point is 34 metres from where 220's was.

The public record does not explain this. Searches for each address, and for development, demolition, zoning or site-plan items on this part of Grand River Avenue, found nothing. A retired address is a record leaving the file, which can mean a demolition, a merger of lots, or a correction, and the file does not say which.

What to watch

Whether 238 Grand River Avenue gets a building permit or a listing that explains it, and whether Northland Gardens ever gets a unit 13.

How this is measured: the tracker reads the City of Brantford's published address layer daily and keeps a snapshot whenever its contents change. October 2026 produced two snapshots, on 24 and 29 October. The month's figures compare the last snapshot before the month (18 September) with the last one in it (29 October). Gross and net are equal, because nothing was added and then removed within the month. Brantford publishes no stable row ID, so a point that moves counts as one retirement and one addition.
