Six units at 48 King Street in Bluewater, and a file that stopped on the 10th

On the 10 October snapshot, 48 King Street in Bluewater appeared as six units at once: 101, 102, 201, 202, B01 and B02. That is more than half of everything the County of Huron's file gained in the month, and the month gained ten addresses. After the 10th, it did not change again.

Ten on a file of thirty-eight thousand is about one in 3,800, against fifty-two in September. Eight are in Bluewater and two in Goderich; no other municipality added any.

48 King Street

No plain 48 King Street was ever on the file: the units arrived without a building number before them. All six points fit in a patch about six metres across, in two groups of three. The file does not say what the B stands for, or what the units are.

King Street is on Huron's file in five municipalities, so the neighbours matter here. The nearest other addresses on this one are 56 and 55 King Street, about 70 metres away, and the nearest on other streets are 83 Lorne Avenue, 31 metres off, then 55 Queen Street and 83 London Road. It is 212 metres from 64 Richmond Street South, and a report on Bluewater council in February places King Street, there County Road 84, and Richmond Street South in Hensall.

Searches found no application, permit or news item for the number. Bluewater's October council highlights could not be read, so that part of the record is unknown rather than silent. The file shows that the addresses exist. It does not say whether anything has been built.

57 Wellesley Street

On 3 October 57 Wellesley Street in Goderich arrived as Unit 1 and Unit 2, about six metres apart. Here too no plain 57 was ever on the file. The nearest points are 53 Wellesley Street, about 23 metres north, and 203 Quebec Street, 31 metres off. Those are the two Goderich addresses that moved 13 and 8 metres on 5 September. Nothing in the file connects the moves to the new units, and the search found nothing public on 57.

Two single numbers

101 Tuyll Street in Bluewater appeared on 3 October, between 100 and 102. Those two, with 99 and 103, have been on the file since the tracker's first read in June. 128 Philip Street, also Bluewater, appeared on the 10th, about 76 metres south of 124 and 75 metres from 138 Dashwood Main Street. The record is silent on both.

What the file did not do

All ten are new records. September's eight "retirements" were moves and relabels under the County's own record IDs; this month none of that happened, and no existing address was touched. The County's IDs for the ten run in one unbroken sequence, straight on from the highest one on the file in September.

A quiet stretch like the three weeks after the 10th is a stretch in which the County published nothing new. It says nothing about what was built or sold in those weeks. The file records paperwork completing, and paperwork has slow months.

What to watch

Whether anything public turns up for 48 King Street; and the blanks left by July's schema change, which did not shrink. 136 addresses had no street number in the County's layer at the end of September, and 136 still have none.

How this is measured — The tracker reads the County of Huron's published 911 address layer daily and keeps a snapshot whenever the contents differ from the last one kept. October 2026 produced two snapshots (3 and 10 October), and every read after the 10th matched. The month's figures are one comparison between the last snapshot before the month (26 September) and the last one in it (10 October), so anything the County changed between 26 September and the 3rd is counted here.
