Hastings County moves two hundred dots, and a Mephisto Lake address becomes Island B

In September the County of Hastings' address file moved about two hundred of its points a few metres each, street by street. Fifty-six of them were on St. Lawrence Street East in Madoc. Under that, a smaller month: sixteen addresses changed while their point stayed put, and the point for 262 Mephisto Lake Landing moved half a kilometre to an address the file labels 1 Water-Mephisto Lake-Island B.

By the numbers

Renumbered 0 by the tracker's count; 16 addresses changed on the same point (below)

Total on file, 29 September 31,336 (+20 over the month)

Twenty more addresses on a file of thirty-one thousand. The 251 and 231 are true to the tracker's bookkeeping and misleading on their own, for the reason in the next section.

Two hundred points, a few metres each

The County's layer has no permanent identifier for a point, so this tracker tells addresses apart by number, street, unit and position. A point moved more than about a metre reads as one address retired and one added. In September, 199 addresses did exactly that: same number, same street, same unit, new position. The median move was 20 metres.

It went street by street. St. Lawrence Street East in Madoc, part of Centre Hastings (a listing places it there), had 56 points moved on 12 and 18 September, none by more than 56 metres. Chemaushgon Road had 21 on the 18th, Thrasher Road 11 on the 15th, Mink Lake Road 9 on the 11th, and so on to the end of the month. A few moved much further: 671A to 671E Jarvis Road between 317 and 472 metres, 497B and 497C King Road about 700.

This is also why the brief reports thirteen "splits", such as 297 St. Lawrence Street East becoming six apartments. Those apartments have been on file since the tracker's first read in June; they moved with their building. The file does not say why any of this was done, and no notice from the County turned up.

Same house, new address

Hidden in the same lists are addresses that changed while the point stayed exactly where it was, with the same assessment roll number:

  • 380 Dundas Street in Deseronto became 140 Prince Street on 15 September, near 383 Dundas and 139 Fourth Street.
  • 101 Egan Lane became 9 Old Bessemer Trail the same day, the first address on a street new to the file, 46 metres from 89 Egan Lane. The search for Old Bessemer Trail found nothing.
  • 4 and 6 Chescher Court became 4B and 4A on 14 September. Each house kept its point and roll number. Two houses were renumbered and nothing was divided.
  • Letters came off. 68A and 68B Maple Street became plain 68 on 4 September. 71A, 71B and 71C York Road, three labels on one point, became 71. 396A and 396B Spry Road became 396 and 412.

The point for 262 Mephisto Lake Landing kept its roll number and moved about 518 metres to an address of a different kind, 1 Water-Mephisto Lake-Island B. The nearest other address is 193 metres away. Mephisto Lake is in Tudor and Cashel. The file does not say why.

What actually arrived

Among the additions that are not moves are 231, 233 and 235 Baker Street in Stirling, which arrived on 16 September on consecutive roll numbers, six to eight metres apart, next door to 229 Baker, which a listing describes as an end-unit townhouse. Two highway numbers, 15138 Highway 62 and 6404 Highway 620, followed on the 19th.

Two single-row edits close the month. The file had held two different properties both labelled 39 Prince Albert Street West, about 300 metres apart, and on 19 September one of them became East. And 560 Bronson Rapids Road changed municipality in the file (the code went from RAWD to HUNT), with a new roll number and the point unmoved.

What to watch

Whether the street-by-street moves carry on into October, and whether Baker Street keeps filling in.

How this is measured — The tracker reads the County of Hastings' published address layer daily and keeps a snapshot whenever the contents differ. September 2026 produced 17 snapshots (2 to 29 September). The month's figures are one comparison between the last snapshot before the month (29 August) and the last one in it (29 September), so edits on the 30th belong to October. Gross and net are equal this month. Because the layer has no stable row id, a point moved by more than about a metre counts as one retirement and one addition. The 199 such pairs above were matched by identical address and unit, and the readdresses by identical position and roll number. Thirty daily reads, none failed.
