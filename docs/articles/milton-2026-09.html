Tremaine Road becomes Gaetan Road and Carton Lane, and 66 addresses come back

On 3 September, 130 Tremaine Road addresses in the Town of Milton's address file changed street. 119 became Gaetan Road and 11 became Carton Lane, keeping the same numbers and the same points. Six days later, 66 of the new Gaetan Road addresses, numbers 4029 to 7580, went back to Tremaine Road. The month ended with 53 on Gaetan Road and 11 on Carton Lane.

The file grew by 24 addresses, about one in two thousand. The renames account for far more activity than the additions: counting the 66 that went back, 196 address records changed street during the month, for a net of 64.

Tremaine Road gives up its name

The Town gave notice that it intended to pass a by-law renaming Tremaine Road to Gaetan Road and Carton Lane, with objections due by 15 June 2026 and Council to consider the by-law on 22 June. The April staff report says why. Halton Region proposed giving the Tremaine name to the newly built road, so the existing road north and south of Campbellville Road needed names of its own.

The April report proposed Troop Point for the northern piece. Affected owners polled themselves and preferred Carton, and Council settled on Carton Lane for the northern section and Gaetan Road for the southern. The council record and local press give the reasons for the names. Carton honours M.J. Carton, who sat on the Nassagaweya and Esquesing councils in the early 1900s. Gaetan refers to land that the founding members of the Italian Canadian Club donated to Conservation Halton. No public record of the 22 June vote itself turned up. The file shows the new names arriving on 3 September, ten weeks after that meeting.

In the file, the 11 Carton Lane addresses run from 8690 to 9248 and sit near Campbellville Road; 9046 is 130 metres from 5675 Campbellville Road. Gaetan Road kept 15 to 141 and 8560, near Milton Heights Crescent, Kelso Road and No. 3 Side Road. Both stretches are in Ward 1.

Six days on Gaetan Road

The 3 September change also took in 66 addresses farther south, from 4029 to 7580 Tremaine Road. That stretch runs from near Burnhamthorpe Road up to Old Tremaine Road. For six days, 4029 Gaetan Road sat 92 metres from 3527 Burnhamthorpe Road. On 9 September all 66 went back to Tremaine Road. Old Tremaine Road itself is what was left when Tremaine was re-routed to the 401 around 2000.

The file does not say why the southern stretch was renamed and then restored, and the public record is silent on it. One Tremaine Road point north of 7580 kept its name throughout: 8301, which the file describes as a secondary access to 52 Peru Road.

One detail went missing in the change. Before September, the file had both an 81 and an 81A Tremaine Road, two points about 80 metres apart on the same property record. Both now read plain "81 Gaetan Road".

Nineteen second units

The other news is small and steady. Of the 24 new addresses, 19 are additional dwelling units added to existing houses, such as "858 Luxton Drive - ADU 1" on 3 September and "460 Bell Street - ADU A" on the 30th. Two came on the same street, at 1315 and 1323 Temperance Crescent. The file held 2,159 ADU addresses at month end, up from 2,140. Milton requires a building permit for every ADU. The file does not say at what stage of that permit the address is issued.

The other five were plain new numbers: 5301 Tremaine Road, 6800 Britannia Road and 198 Martin Street on 10 September, 2935 James Snow Parkway on the 17th, and 6751 Eighth Line on the 24th. Nothing came off the file.
