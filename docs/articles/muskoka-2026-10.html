McConnell Crescent doubles in a day, and three new condo buildings appear on Tree Tops Lane

On 17 October, 46 new numbers appeared on McConnell Crescent in Bracebridge, all at once and all on one roll number. Five days later, three buildings' worth of condo units arrived on Tree Tops Lane in Huntsville. Together the two streets account for 83 of the month's 142 new addresses.

The file grew by 137, about one address in 490. Huntsville gained 62, Bracebridge 55, Muskoka Lakes 22, Georgian Bay 2 and Gravenhurst 1.

McConnell Crescent

McConnell Crescent was already on file, with 34 numbers between 1 and 39, each on its own roll number. Listings for those homes describe brand-new freehold townhouses in the Macaulay area of Bracebridge, just off Clearbrook Trail. The nearest addresses on other streets are on Whitehorn Park Road and Manitoba Street.

The 46 new numbers run from 38 to 70 on the even side, then from 74 to 106 on both sides, plus a single "0". Unlike the older numbers, all 46 share one roll number. The file does not say why. Nothing public names a builder or a plan of subdivision for these numbers, so the public record is silent on what is being built.

Tree Tops Lane

Tree Tops Lane is a short dead end across the road from Deerhurst Resort. Condo listings group its first buildings, 1 to 4, as Treetops at Hidden Valley. Those have been on file since the tracker started in June.

On 22 October the file added units 101 to 304 at 11 and at 13 Tree Tops Lane, and 101 to 302 at 9: 30 units, numbered by floor, each with its own roll number in one unbroken run. No plain 9, 11 or 13 has ever been on file. Seven plain numbers came the same day: 5 and 7 on one roll number, and 15, 16, 17, 18 and 20 on another. A rental listing already advertises unit 202 at 13 Tree Tops Lane. Nothing found names who built the three new buildings.

Two buildings that grew

162 Howland Drive in Huntsville gained units 207 to 215 on 17 October. Units 101 to 106 and 201 to 206 were already there, and so is the plain 162. A real-estate site lists the address as Huntsville Lofts by Sandhu Developments, a condominium with six retail units, a drive-thru Starbucks among them.

4 Armstrong Point Road in Port Carling gained 16 units on the 22nd. They are labelled L16 to L18, M10 to M15 and T3 to T9, all at a single point. Units C1 to C6 and BH1 were already on file. Hotel booking sites list Muskoka Lakes Hotel and Resorts at this address. Nothing public explains what the letters stand for.

Four that were already there

Four of the "new" addresses had been on file all along. The tracker identifies a Muskoka address by its number, street, unit and roll number, so a change to any of those counts as one address leaving and another arriving. 20 Spencer Street and 1685 Lone Pine Drive, both in Bracebridge, came back on new roll numbers, and 20 Spencer's point moved about 44 metres. The two bunkies at 93 Potato Island Road in Georgian Bay had been filed since June with no street name, and they gained one on the 17th.

That leaves one real retirement: 36 Ann Street in Bracebridge, last seen on 17 October. Two days later 34 Ann Street appeared about 21 metres away, on a different roll number, along with 170 Hiram Street around the corner. The file does not link them.

Everything else was single numbers scattered across the District: five on Balsam Chutes Road and four on East Elliott Street in Huntsville, and three units at 1553 Brackenrig Road in Muskoka Lakes, among others.

How this is measured: The tracker reads the District Municipality of Muskoka's published address layer every day and keeps a snapshot whenever the contents differ from the last one kept. October 2026 produced four snapshots, from 17 to 24 October, and nothing changed after the 24th. The month's figures compare the 24 October snapshot with the last one before the month (25 September). A renumbering or a new roll number counts as one retirement plus one addition. Moves under two metres are treated as the service's republishing wobble and ignored. Additions, retirements and moves were the same summed day by day as over the month, so nothing came and went within the month. The tracker made thirty daily reads and none failed.
