Vanguard Drive arrives with nine numbers, and 68 Hemlock Street becomes four townhouse records

On 4 September a street the City of Greater Sudbury's address file had never carried appeared in it: Vanguard Drive, with nine numbers from 183 to 396, all on the same day. Eleven days later, in Capreol, the single point for 68 Hemlock Street became four, each marked as a townhouse.

The file grew from 70,097 to 70,126 records, about four hundredths of one percent. A third of the growth is one street. No address left the file: in Sudbury's file a retired address keeps its record and changes its status field instead.

Vanguard Drive

The nine Vanguard Drive points (183, 190, 232, 245, 274, 297, 322, 359 and 396) run north for roughly 400 metres. The nearest addresses on other streets to 183, the southern end, are 2626 Kingsway, 191 metres away, and 264 Levesque Street, 202 metres away. None of the nine carries a unit, a business name or a building type; they are plain civic numbers.

The spacing is wide, 213 numbers across nine points, and the file does not say whether more are to come. Nothing public says what Vanguard Drive is for. A search for the street name in Sudbury finds no plan of subdivision, naming report, development notice or listing.

68 Hemlock Street, Capreol

68 Hemlock Street has been on file since June as one point. On the 15 September snapshot that point was relabelled a townhouse unit, and three more points appeared beside it with the same address and the same label, spread across about 28 metres. None of the four has a unit number: the file now holds four records that read, word for word, 68 Hemlock Street.

The file does not say when the townhouses were built or by whom, and a search for the address turned up nothing.

Two addresses replaced on the spot

Twice this month one point was marked retired and a new address appeared on the same spot:

  • 4589 Municipal Road 80 in Val Therese appeared on 10 September. On the 15th it was marked Retired, and 4589 Fergus Avenue appeared at exactly the same coordinates. The retired record stays in the file, which is why it counts among the 29 new addresses. A day after the first point, 4670 Municipal Road 80 appeared about 45 metres west.
  • Sunnyside Road has had two points both labelled 2095 since the tracker began in June. On the 22 September snapshot one of them was retired, and 2105 Sunnyside Road appeared about six metres from it. The other 2095 is still active.

The file records the swap, not the reason for it.

What else moved

Two more addresses were marked retired, 233 Elgin Street (25 September) and 1250 South Bay Road (30 September), with no reason given. 2745 Henri Street went the other way: it was marked retired on 29 August and back to active on 1 September, so it counts here as a retired address coming back.

The file also names businesses on some of its points, and those names moved. On one day, 24 September, three Kathleen Street points changed: 533 from Donovan Freshmart to Donovan Variety, 552 from Rogers Photo Studio to Healing Touch, and 624 from Vacant to eCloudz. On the 15th, 42 Lorne Street went from Salon Beejou to Covalin Electrical Supply. On the 30th, an unnamed restaurant point at 4764 Regional Road 15 in Chelmsford, the number that also carries the Chelmsford Mall, was named Mary Brown's Chicken, and a new unnamed retail point appeared at the same number. These are labels being updated, not buildings changing; the file does not say when any of the businesses opened or closed.

A quiet month, then: one new street, one row of townhouses, a few corrections. The file is a record of addresses being issued, and issuing them is paperwork that keeps its own pace.

How this is measured — "New" and "retired" compare the last snapshot before September (31 August) with the last one in it (30 September); the tracker read the file on all 30 days of the month without a failure and kept 24 distinct snapshots. Day by day the month added up to 30 additions and 54 location edits, but no point ends the month moved 10 metres or more, the tracker's noise floor for this file. Three location-only edits on 25 September were held as a feed artefact and are excluded from every number above.
