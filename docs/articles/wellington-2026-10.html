Six numbers leave Wellington Street beside Everton

On 13 October the County of Wellington's address file dropped 338, 346, 362, 370, 378 and 394 Wellington Street, six even numbers in a row across the road from the hamlet of Everton in Guelph-Eramosa. Nothing took their place. An old planning record describes lots on that side of the street being given up, but nothing public says why the numbers went this month.

Five on a file of almost forty-three thousand is about a hundredth of a percent. The file changed on two days, the 13th and the 20th. September had eleven.

The even side of Wellington Street

The six numbers sat in a line about 190 metres long on the south-west side of the street. All six had been on the file since tracking began in June. The odd numbers facing them, 339 to 395, are about 40 metres away, are labelled Everton in the file, and are all still there. The six that left carried no place label at all. After 13 October, Wellington Street here has no even number above 300.

There is a paper trail for that side of the road, though an old one. In July 2013 the Wellington Advertiser reported that a plan of subdivision, first approved by the County in 2008, "removed five vacant lots west of Wellington Road 49 (Wellington Street)" and created five new ones on the north side of Market Street, and that Guelph-Eramosa council supported extending it for three years. A second Advertiser story on the township's approval lists a condition that the land west of Wellington Street be designated agricultural, to rule out more lots there.

That matches the place and does not match the count. The articles speak of five lots and the file dropped six numbers. Neither article gives a civic number, and nothing dated 2026 turned up. Market Street's addresses, 110 to 160, have been on the file since June and did not change. The second story's page is dated 1 January 2025, which does not fit a plan the 2013 report says was approved in 2008, so its real date is not known. The file shows six numbers gone from the side of the street those stories describe, and it does not say why they went now.

Two numbers changed, one arrived

  • 121 Main Street North in Mount Forest became 129 on the same point (13 October).
  • 9490 Concession 6 North in Wellington North became 9491 on the same point (13 October). Its nearest neighbour is 9496, 74 metres away.
  • 4018 Concession 7 in Puslinch is the month's one new address (20 October). It sits between 4010 and 4024.

Searches for all three came back empty. A number changing on a point that does not move can be a correction or a property being re-described, and the file does not say which.

What to watch

Whether anything public turns up about the Wellington Street numbers, and whether the County's layer gets its full street names back (below).

How this is measured — The tracker reads the County of Wellington's base-data address layer daily and keeps a snapshot whenever its contents differ from the last one kept. The layer covers Centre Wellington, Erin, Guelph-Eramosa, Mapleton, Minto, Puslinch and Wellington North. Guelph is a separate city and is not in it. October 2026 produced two snapshots, on 13 and 20 October, and the month's figures compare the second with the last snapshot of September, taken on the 30th. One thing is held out of every count here. Since 13 October the layer has cut every text field at 20 characters, so St Andrew Street West reads St Andrew Street Wes; the layer's own definition now gives each field that length. That relabelled 3,253 records on 124 street names and shortened one place label on 26 more. No address changed by it, and the names were still cut at the last read. Because an address is identified by its number, street, unit and rounded position, the tracker saw the relabelling as thousands of removals and additions and held that day's additions and removals off the site. The six Wellington Street retirements and the two number changes arrived in the same update. They were separated from it for this piece by comparing the snapshots directly, so the site's report for 13 October shows only the 26 shortened place labels and none of them. The report for 20 October shows 4018 Concession 7, beside a point at 33 Scott Street in Fergus that shifted by a rounding step on the 13th and back on the 20th, which is not counted here.
