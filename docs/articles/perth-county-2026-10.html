Twelve lettered addresses on six numbers, from Atwood to Perth South

On 7 October the County of Perth's address file gained Units A and B at both 170 and 172 Main Street in Atwood, and 3972A, 3972B, 3974A and 3974B on Byron Street in Perth East. Only 172 had been on the file before, as a plain number; there had never been a plain 170, 3972 or 3974. In Perth South, 1720 Perth Road 123 and 3186 Road 122 each split into an A and a B. Twelve of the month's 21 new addresses are those letters and units. The file does not say why for any of them.

Sixteen on a file of about 18,700 is under a tenth of a percent, and a sixth of September's 96. North Perth gained 7, Perth East 6, Perth South 2 and West Perth 1. All of it came in one update: the file was the same on every read until 6 October, changed on the 7th, and did not change again in October.

Two doors on Main Street, two on Byron

In Atwood, 170 Main Street arrived as Unit A and Unit B. There had never been a plain 170 on the file. Next door, the plain 172 came off the file the same day its Units A and B arrived. All four units share one assessment roll number, the one 172 had before. 168 Main Street, next along, did not change.

On Byron Street in Perth East, 3972 and 3974 arrived already split, as 3972A and 3972B and 3974A and 3974B. No plain 3972 or 3974 was ever on the file. The four share one roll number, and the nearest older addresses are 2173 Andrew Street and 3970 Tannahill Street.

The two Perth South splits were more ordinary. 1720 Perth Road 123 and 3186 Road 122 each came off the file and returned as an A and a B, on the same roll and within about 15 metres of the old point.

No severance, consent or listing turned up for any of the six. The public record is silent on what was built, divided or planned.

154 Boyne Avenue becomes 514

The point for 154 Boyne Avenue in North Perth was retired, and 514 Boyne Avenue appeared at exactly the same coordinates under the same roll number. Its nearest neighbours on the file are 525 Riverview Drive and 485 Victoria Avenue South. The file does not say whether the old number was a slip. In West Perth, 2891 Road 164 left as 5996 Perth Line 20 arrived 61 metres away on the same roll.

What the file did to itself

This tracker tells Perth's addresses apart by the full address and its position, so a point that moves leaves the file and comes back as a "new" address. Thirty-eight of the 59 additions are that. Thirty-five of them are in one place: 25 of McNally Drive's 46 numbers and 10 of Featherstone Street East's 25, all on a single roll number, each nudged 3 to 9 metres. Sale listings for lots on McNally Drive and Featherstone Street East both describe Countryside Meadows, "a thoughtfully designed new subdivision by PK Homes Inc" at the north end of Listowel. Every one of those numbers has been on the file since the tracker's first read in August, so none of them is new to the file. The file does not say why they moved. The other three moves were 2917 Forest Road (57 metres), 1712 Perth Road 123 and 5995 Perth Line 20.

Another 15 records changed only their roll number. Twelve are 33 Murray Court, Units 1 to 12, in Perth East: until October they shared one roll, and now each has its own.

What to watch

Whether the Countryside Meadows roll splits into one per lot, as 33 Murray Court's did this month, and whether anything public turns up on the Atwood and Byron Street splits.

How this is measured — The tracker reads the County of Perth's published NG911 address layer daily and keeps a snapshot whenever the contents differ from the last one kept. The layer covers North Perth, West Perth, Perth East and Perth South; Stratford and St. Marys are separate cities and are not in it. October 2026 produced one snapshot, on 7 October. The month's figures compare it with the last snapshot before the month, 8 September, so everything counted here was published between the reads of 6 and 7 October. Gross and net are equal: no address arrived and left again inside the month. An address is identified by its full text and rounded position, so a moved point counts as one retirement and one addition. Thirty daily reads in October, none failed.
