Arend Street and Rose Lane arrive in Mitchell, 28 numbers each

On 8 September the County of Perth's address file gained two streets it had never carried, both in Mitchell. Arend Street arrived with 28 numbers, and alongside it 7 to 29 Napier Street arrived as twelve pairs, each a Unit A and a Unit B. Three years earlier, a plan for this block had called for 37 detached homes, 12 lots for 24 semi-detached homes and a new street. About two kilometres east, Rose Lane arrived with 28 more numbers behind 230 Ontario Road, and the public record says nothing about it.

Ninety-six on a file of under nineteen thousand is about half a percent. Eighty-six of the new addresses are in West Perth, ten in North Perth, three in Perth East and none in Perth South. All of it came in one update: the County's file was the same every day from 20 August to 7 September, changed on the 8th, and did not change again in September.

The Frank, Napier, Clayton and Nelson block

The new numbers fill one Mitchell block. Arend Street's numbers stretch over about 220 metres. The nearest addresses that were already on the file are 20 Nelson Street, 92 metres from 20 Arend, then 6077 Frank Street and 36 Upper Thames Lane. The file numbers Arend from 6 to 32, plus 34. Napier Street gained Units A and B at each odd number from 7 to 29, and there was never a plain 7 to 29 Napier on the file. Clayton Street gained 36, 38 and 40, and Nelson Street 28 and 30. Each pair of Napier units shares one assessment roll number, and the twelve rolls run consecutively.

On 1 June 2023 StratfordToday reported that Perth County council had received a recommended plan of subdivision for "a currently-empty lot between the streets of Frank, Napier, Clayton and Nelson", a 9.4-acre field then in cash crops. The plan called for "37 lots for single detached dwellings, 12 lots for 24 semi-detached dwellings and a new municipal street". The 37 singles were to be phase one and the semis phase two. The staff report put the developer's estimated prices at $650,000 and $550,000 and said that "these units will not be attainable for either a moderate- or low-income household".

The twenty-four Napier units match the plan's 24 semi-detached homes exactly. The plan's 37 detached lots have no equally neat counterpart: the file has 33 new numbers on Arend, Clayton and Nelson. Arend is the only new street name on the block, but nothing found says it is the plan's new street, or where its name comes from. The file also does not follow the plan's order. Both phases got their numbers on the same day, and nothing in the file says what has been built.

Rose Lane

Rose Lane's 28 numbers run from 10 to 33, then the odd numbers 35 to 41, over about 140 metres just off Ontario Road. Their 28 assessment roll numbers run consecutively, starting at the number directly after the roll of 230 Ontario Road, and on the same day the point for 230 Ontario Road moved 105 metres to sit beside 10 Rose Lane. The nearest older address is 238 Ontario Road.

The search found no plan, notice or listing for Rose Lane or 230 Ontario Road. The public record is silent on what is there.

Three addresses, each split in two

Three addresses split into an A and a B on the same spot, and each original came off the file the same day: 135 Pugh Street in Perth East, next door to a 137A and 137B that were already on the file, and 6675 Perth Line 86 and 7568 Road 154 in North Perth. In North Perth, 8358C and 8358D Fairlane Road also arrived. The record is silent on all of them.

What the file did to itself

This tracker tells Perth's addresses apart by the full address and its position, so a point that moves leaves the file and comes back as a "new" address. That accounts for 12 of the 16 records reported as retired. Along with 230 Ontario Road, 34 Clayton Street moved 23 metres, 6077 Frank Street 21 metres and 7003 Raymond Drive 43 metres. 91 and 93 Kenton Street had shared one point and are now two, as are 101 and 103 Clayton Street. 693 and 695 Conners Drive, Units A and B, moved under 10 metres. A thirteenth, 6604 Line 66, became 6604 Road 154 on the same spot under the same roll number. The file does not say why. Another 18 records changed only their roll number, twelve of them on William Shakespeare Street in Perth East.

What to watch

Whether the rest of the Mitchell block gets its numbers; whether the roll number shared by 29 and 31 Arend, 34 to 40 Clayton and 28 and 30 Nelson splits into one per lot; and whether anything public turns up about Rose Lane.
