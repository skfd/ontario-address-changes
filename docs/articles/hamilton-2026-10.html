McMaster's largest residence reaches the address file five weeks after its students

On 1 October 2026 the City of Hamilton's address file gave 1190 Main Street West 573 unit numbers in one update, from 100 to 1510. That is Lincoln Alexander Hall, McMaster University's new residence, and students began moving in at the end of August. The file caught up about five weeks later.

By the numbers
New addresses 601
Retired 7
Renumbered 0
Street renames 0
Address splits 7 (units added under an existing address)
Total on file, 30 October 274,585 (+594 over the month)

The hall alone is 95% of the additions, and September's net gain was 157. Both columns are slightly overstated: none of the seven "retirements" was an address leaving (see below). Take those out and the month is 596 new addresses and two placeholder records gone at 220 Hempstead Drive, in a file of about 274,000.

573 numbers at Lincoln Alexander Hall

The hall is named for a 1949 McMaster graduate who became Canada's first Black MP and Ontario's first Black lieutenant-governor. Ground was broken in November 2023. The building is listed at 15 and 10 storeys and sits beside 99 Traymore Avenue, between Forsyth and Dalewood. Its developer gives it 1,366 beds and an August 2026 opening.

The plain address had been on file since the tracker began reading Hamilton in June, and it is still there. What arrived in October is the inside of the building. The City's addressing policy says unit numbers in a multi-floor building typically carry a floor indicator, and read that way the file shows a shape: 25 numbers on the first floor, 12 on the second, 58 or 59 on each of the third to ninth, 45 on the tenth and eleventh, and nine on each of the top four.

The file does not say what one of these numbers is. McMaster offers pods and pod suites of one to five beds and publishes no count of them, so 573 cannot be matched to anything, and it is not the number of students. Every floor from the third to the ninth also skips the same endings: 04, 11, 23, 37, 46 and 60. The policy leaves unit numbers in a multiple dwelling to the owner, and nothing public says what those gaps are. One smaller mismatch: the file gives the postal code L8S 0A1, and McMaster gives L8S 1C2.

Three numbers with an R

3334 Homestead Drive and 3338 Homestead Drive, in Glanbrook, each gained three records on 19 October: Units 1 and 2 at the plain number, and a 3334 R or 3338 R about 20 metres west of it. 182 R Erindale Avenue followed on the 26th, beside 182.

The R has a stated meaning. Under the same policy, a detached additional dwelling unit gets the letter R as a suffix, and the City requires it to have its own address so that emergency crews, mail and utilities can find it. The file now holds 64 numbers carrying an R, up from 61.

On Homestead there is one more trace. A listing that has since ended marketed 3334 with a permit-ready plan for a two-storey home and a two-storey additional unit, "attached by garage only", and said 3338 was for sale separately. That is a seller's description of a plan. The file does not say what was built.

Two streets with one number each

8 Talon Road arrived on 12 October, about 180 metres from 30 Times Square Boulevard and 575 Highland Road West. 20 Senior Drive arrived on the 19th in Ancaster, beside 284 Nakoma Road and 143 Cornwallis Road. Neither name had been on the file since tracking began in June. Searches found no by-law, subdivision or listing for either street, so the public record is silent for now.

Elsewhere, 2548 and 2574 Highway No. 5 West arrived in Flamborough on the 26th, with nothing public on either. 15 Wexford Avenue North and 93 Welbourn Drive each gained Units 1 and 2 with the original address kept.

What the file itself did

None of the seven "retirements" was an address leaving:

  • 100 Parkside Drive became 100 Clappison Avenue at exactly the same point, with the same postal code. It fits a run already on Clappison, between 85 and 105. Council added land to Clappison Avenue in December 2021, but nothing found explains why this address moved streets now.
  • 220 Hempstead Drive moved about 33 metres east, and its two records labelled PT 1 and Unit gave way to Units 1 to 4. That is three of the seven. Nothing public explains it.
  • 4 Portside Street had units written G 1 and G 2, with a space. They became G1 and G2.
  • 293 Highway No. 8 moved about five metres on 9 October.

The tracker treats a moved point or a respelled unit as one address leaving and another arriving. None of them is a building coming or going.

How this is measured — The tracker reads the City of Hamilton's published address layer daily and keeps a snapshot whenever the contents differ. October 2026 produced six snapshots, from 1 to 30 October. The month's figures compare the last snapshot before the month (29 September) with the last one in it (30 October). The read on the 31st matched the 30th, so nothing is left over for November. Gross activity matched net exactly: 601 additions and 7 removals, with nothing added and undone inside the month.
