Four new numbers on Birch Street, and 36 addresses change ward

On 7 October 2026 the City of Quinte West's address file added 7, 9, 11 and 13 Birch Street in Trenton, four points a few metres apart, right next to 5 Birch Street. In August 2025 the City's planning committee approved a rezoning at 5 Birch for two townhouse blocks, one of five units and one of four. Two days earlier, 36 existing addresses had their ward changed, 20 of them on one street in Frankford.

Ten more on a file of about twenty thousand. September came to +17. Twenty-eight of the additions and twenty-eight of the retirements are the same 28 Edith Lane addresses leaving the file and coming back on the 27th; see the last section.

Birch Street

Before October, Birch Street in the Trenton ward ran 2 to 25 and 41 to 49, every number on file since the tracker's first read in June. On 7 October four odd numbers arrived together: 7, 9, 11 and 13, spaced about six metres apart in a row starting some 30 metres east of 5 Birch and ending short of 15. The nearest addresses on other streets are 13 Gilmour Court and 195 Sidney Street.

In August 2025 Quinte West's Planning Advisory Committee approved a rezoning that allows "two townhouse structures to be built on Birch Street": "the development at 5 Birch Street would see the construction of one five-unit building and another containing four units" (QuinteNews, 22 August 2025). The staff report behind it (25-066PD) is a scanned document that could not be read here, so the record found does not say who applied, how the lots are laid out or what the units will be numbered.

So four numbers have appeared beside a lot approved for a four-unit block and a five-unit block. The file does not link them to 5 Birch, and no public source found does either. If they do belong to that project, five more numbers have yet to arrive.

Thirty-six addresses change ward

On 5 October nothing was added or removed, but 36 addresses that were already on the file had their ward field changed. Their points did not move.

  • Rosewood Drive, Frankford. Before the change, the odd numbers from 5 to 21 were in Frankford and the even numbers from 4 to 26 were in Sidney. Everything from 23 up was Sidney. On the 5th, twenty of those, 23 through 48, moved to Frankford. The even 4 to 26 are still Sidney.
  • Hamilton Road. Eight numbers between 2323 and 2393 moved from Trenton to Sidney, and 2223 moved the other way, from Sidney to Trenton.
  • North Murray Street. 57, 61 and 63 moved from Sidney to Trenton.
  • River Drive. 122, 124 and 126 moved from Frankford to Sidney.
  • Crowe Road. 432 moved from Murray to Sidney.

Quinte West's municipal election was three weeks later, on 26 October (Quinte West Votes). No ward boundary review or by-law was found, and nothing public ties these 36 changes to the election. The file records the new ward without saying whether a boundary moved or a label was corrected.

The rest of the month

707 Foxboro-Stirling Road, in Sidney, became units 1 and 2 on 14 October. Both carry the unit type "House". Unit 1 sits exactly on the old point, unit 2 about eleven metres east, and the plain 707 left the file the same day. Nothing public was found on the property.

On the 14th 300 Eggleton Road appeared, and on the 17th it was replaced by 378 Eggleton Road at exactly the same spot. It is one address whose number was changed after three days, and the only reason the month's edit total runs one ahead of its outcome. Four single numbers arrived in the Murray ward on 7 October: 3 Carr Road, 379 Stoney Point Road South, 991 Powerline Road and 102 McMaster Road.

On 27 October, 28 points on Edith Lane, from 2 to 45, moved between 1 and 13 metres and nothing else about them changed. Quinte West's file has no permanent record number, so the tracker identifies each row by its number, street, unit and position. A point that moves more than about a metre therefore counts as one address retired and one added. Those 28 account for the gap between the table's totals and what actually changed. Edith Lane has been on the file since the tracker's first read in June. A listing for 8 Edith Lane, posted on 29 August, describes a "brand-new" end-unit townhome in "Batawa Landing by Wooler Homes" (condos.ca). Nothing public says why the points were moved.

How this is measured: The tracker reads the City's published file daily and keeps a snapshot whenever it changes. October's figures are one diff from the last snapshot before the month (25 September) to the last one in it (27 October), across 30 reads with no failures. Changes published after the 27th fall into November. Gross activity (40 in, 30 out) is one higher than the net figures on each side, because 300 Eggleton Road came and went inside the window.
