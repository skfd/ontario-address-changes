Two Frontenac addresses took a letter, and only one address really left

On 8 October, 1038 Pranger Court in Central Frontenac became 1038A, and a new 1038B appeared 89 metres away. Eleven days later the same thing happened in South Frontenac: 184 Burridge Lake Road became 184B, with a new 184A 24 metres from it.

Retired 4 (the same three, plus one)
Renumbered 0

Total on file at month end 22,408

The file grew by 11 addresses, about one in every two thousand, after growing by 16 in September.

One number, two letters

In both cases the original point stayed exactly where it was and only its label changed. The letters went opposite ways. On Pranger Court the old address became the A and the new point the B; on Burridge Lake Road the old address became the B and the new point the A.

Pranger Court already had 1026A through 1026D, so every number on the court now carries a letter. The nearest address on another street to 1038B is 1038C Pleasant Hill Lane, 42 metres off.

The file does not say what stands at any of these points, and a search found no listing, notice or news item for either address. South Frontenac's public notices from 21 August to 2 November include about 20 planning items, none of them on Burridge Lake Road.

The one that left

57 Oak Shores Crescent in South Frontenac was last seen on 13 October, with no replacement in the file. 55 and 59 are still there on either side. A retired address is a record leaving the file, which is not the same as a building coming down, and the public record is silent on which this was.

The other three retirements were not addresses leaving:

  • 1033 Primrose Lane is the correction September's piece was waiting for. Its full address has said Primrose Lane since 28 September while its street field said Price Road; by 19 October the street field agreed. The point did not move, so what looks like a new street here is one row being fixed.
  • 8078 Road 509 in North Frontenac moved 90 metres by 7 October.
  • 1585 Leggat Lake Road in Central Frontenac shifted about two metres on 8 October, the day a new 1575 arrived 57 metres away.

The twelve that are new

Besides 1038B, 184A and 1575 Leggat Lake Road, nine single numbers joined established roads. Central Frontenac took seven of the twelve, including 949 Crow Lake Road and 1146 Steiger Road, the last arrival on 31 October. South Frontenac took three, among them 2171 Keeley Road and 1127 Beach Lane. North Frontenac took two, 1060 Turkey Lane and 1170 Hamilton Hills Lane. Frontenac Islands had none.

What the file itself did

On 8 October, 18 labels closed the gap between number and letter, so 1050 A Penyck Lake Lane became 1050A and 5112 A Battersea Road became 5112A. No address of that form remains. Nothing else about those rows changed.

How this is measured — The tracker reads the County of Frontenac's published address layer daily and keeps a snapshot whenever its contents differ from the last one kept. October 2026 produced six snapshots (7, 8, 13, 19, 29 and 31 October); the month's figures are one comparison between the last snapshot before the month (28 September) and the last one in it (31 October). Additions and removals summed day by day match that comparison, 15 and 4. Edits summed day by day come to 20: the two letterings and the 18 closed gaps. A cleanup of doubled spaces on 395 labels on 7 October changed no address and is excluded from every figure.
