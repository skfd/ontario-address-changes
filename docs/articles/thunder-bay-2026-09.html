A second number for the rezoned land at 1091 Central Avenue

On 9 September 2026 the City of Thunder Bay's address file gave 1075 Central Avenue its own point, 28 metres from 1091 Central Avenue and on the same land-registry parcel. That parcel is the one council rezoned in April 2025 for a retail warehouse with a gas bar. In the same update, 1091 stopped being labelled City Owned.

Those figures overstate the month. Four of the nine "new" addresses and four of the five "retired" ones are the same addresses: 4100 25th Side Road, 140 Churchill Drive West and 681 and 683 Mercier Street. Their points moved between 7 and 54 metres. Thunder Bay's layer gives addresses no lasting id, so the tracker recognizes an address by its number, street and position, and a moved point shows up as one address leaving and another arriving. Take those out and September brought five new addresses and lost one.

1075 Central Avenue

Council approved the by-law changes for 1091 Central Avenue in April 2025: a retail warehouse of 15,049.5 square metres with a gas station, on the south side of Central west of Golf Links Road (Your ThunderBay, 14 April 2025). Local reporting has tied the site to Costco, and NetNewsLedger, citing that reporting, wrote in April that Costco bought the 7.7-hectare property in December 2025 (NetNewsLedger, 7 April 2026).

The file records two things on that parcel. It gained a second number, 1075 Central, with its type set to Address Only and no property type. And 1091 lost its City Owned label, leaving that field blank. If the sale closed in December, the label came off about nine months later. The file does not say what 1075 will be the address of. It does not name Costco either.

Two more numbers beside it

1077 Central Avenue arrived the same day. It is also an Address Only number, on the parcel of 1175 Central Avenue, 83 metres away, and 1175 also stopped being City Owned in the same update. 1112 Premier Way, 102 metres from 1306 Capital Way, went onto the parcel of 1245 Central Avenue, an address that first appeared in July. No public record of either turned up.

The other two arrivals stand on parcels of their own: 2288 Government Road, 160 metres from 145 Boyko Road, and 639 Hilldale Road, in the gap between 635 and 640. The one real retirement was 2131 R.S. Piper Avenue, the only address on its parcel, 48 metres from 2111 R.S. Piper. A search for each of them found no permit, severance, sale or demolition. The public record is silent on all of them, and a retired address is a record leaving the file, not necessarily a building leaving the street.

What the file itself did

Eight addresses changed only their labels. Four Oliver Road numbers, 1171, 1175, 1181 and 1191, went from Regular Property to Address Only, and 1165 Oliver Road went the other way. 700 Red River Road became City Owned. The layer publishes no definitions for these values. 5405 Mapleward Road lost the "Thunder Bay" in its city field. That is a label dropping off a point, not an address leaving the city.

How this is measured — The tracker reads the City of Thunder Bay's published address layer daily and keeps a snapshot whenever its contents differ from the last one kept. In September it was read on 30 days with no failures, and the contents changed once, on 9 September. The file had not changed at all in August, so the month's figures compare the 24 July snapshot with the 9 September one. Net and gross agree: 9 added, 5 retired, 8 label changes and 1 city-field change. Because the layer has no lasting address id, any move of a point counts as a retirement plus an addition. A city land-inventory field that flips in batches is not counted. Each day's report is on the site linked below.
