Twelve new streets and 821 addresses in one day off Spratt Road

On 18 September 2026 Ottawa's address file gained 821 addresses in a single block of Riverside South, east of Spratt Road: 150 on Solarium Avenue, 86 on Spumante Street, 77 more on Ed Findlay Street, and a dozen streets the file had never held before, from Ivory Street to Redstart Walk. That one update is seven in ten of all the addresses the City added in September.

About one address in 350 on the file at month's end was new in September, and almost all of them came in three batches: 821 on the 18th, 169 on the 15th and 67 on the 28th.

Riverside South fills in

The 18 September batch covers about 630 by 940 metres. Five of its streets were already on file: Ralph Hennessy Avenue, Solarium Avenue, Ed Findlay Street, Lavender Street and Fuchsia Street each got longer. The other twelve debuted that day: Spumante Street, Ivory Street, Rotunda Crescent, Shallow Cove Place, Platinum Circle, Bodega Crescent, Magenta Crescent, Portside Terrace, Cogswell Grove, Casa Loma Way, Cabana Terrace and Redstart Walk. 600 Solarium Avenue sits 37 metres from 164 Ed Findlay Street and 164 metres from 4875 Spratt Road.

That last address is in the title of a 2020 noise study for "Riverside South Phase 17, 4775 & 4875 Spratt Road", prepared for the Riverside South Development Corporation: about 59 hectares east of Spratt Road, planned as single houses, street townhouses and stacked townhouses in three phases. The draft plan of subdivision, D07-16-20-0018, went to a public meeting in December 2020 and carries a signed draft approval dated 15 October 2021. The documents do not say which of the 821 addresses fall inside Phase 17 and which are on neighbouring land, so this piece does not either.

Of the street names, only one has a public story. Veterans Affairs Canada records that the City named Ralph Hennessy Avenue in November 2015 for Vice-Admiral Ralph Lucien Hennessy of the Royal Canadian Navy, who died in Ottawa in 2014. For Ed Findlay and the twelve new names, the public record is silent: no naming report turned up.

Four streets in Cardinal Creek

Three days earlier, on the 15th, 169 addresses arrived in east Orléans, all at once: 79 more numbers on Cours du Lévrier (3372 to 3598), and three new streets, Hyacinth Way (34), Terrasse de Tadoussac (32) and Rue du Canot d'Écorce (24). 1150 Canot d'Écorce is 171 metres from 1291 Old Montreal Road, and 3450 Lévrier is 215 metres from 792 Antonio Farley Street, a street that listings place in Cardinal Creek Village. Cardinal Creek is Tamarack's community of about 3,000 homes east of Trim Road and north of Old Montreal Road. Which phase these four streets belong to, and who chose their names, nothing public says.

Sixty-seven B's off Brian Coburn

On the 28th, 67 addresses arrived on three streets near Brian Coburn Boulevard and Navan Road: 33 on Rosalie Ridge, 26 on Paleo Heights and 8 on Stompin' Tom Private. Each one is a B: 95 B Rosalie Ridge, 250 B Paleo Heights, 501 B Stompin' Tom. Every one of them sits on a number already on file, about five metres from its plain twin. Listings for 95 and 133 Rosalie Ridge sell them as townhomes with "a legal 1-bedroom secondary suite with private entrance". The file does not say what a B is, so it is an inference, but a fair one, that these 67 are those suites getting their own front-door numbers. B is the City's usual mark for this: 112 of September's new addresses carried one.

Patchwork Circle, for one week

In Richmond, nine numbers, 800 to 816, arrived on 21 September as Patchwork Circle. On the 28th the same nine records, unchanged in every other way, read Patchwork Place. 800 is 30 metres from 432 Meynell Road and 40 from 6327 Ottawa Street West, in Mattamy's Richmond Meadows, where sale listings call it Patchwork Street. The record does not say why it was renamed after a week.

What the file did to itself

338 addresses switched between the layer's two kinds, MAIN and SUBORD. 330 of them went from subordinate to main, nearly all whole streets in newer subdivisions: 52 on Gosling Pond Crescent, 50 on Prosper Circle, 27 on Camargue Private. The layer itself labels the values "Main" and "Subordinate" and does not define them. All but five of the 1,161 new addresses this month came in as subordinate, so the file's pattern is addresses issued one way and promoted later. Why, and on what trigger, the City does not publish.

Fifteen addresses left the file, nearly all singles. The one cluster is a tidy-up: at 1270 Trenton Avenue, units numbered 1 A, 1 B and 2 B gave way to plain letters B to E, while 1268 next door gained A to E and 1256 Dorchester Avenue, 155 metres away, gained A to F. And two points on the Sir-George-Étienne-Cartier Parkway, 501 and 504, were moved 580 and 476 metres west on the 17th, keeping their numbers. Nothing public ties either number to a building.

What to watch

Whether the 821 Riverside South addresses and the 169 in Cardinal Creek get promoted to MAIN the way Gosling Pond and Prosper did; and whether Patchwork stays a Place.

How this is measured — The tracker reads the City of Ottawa's published address layer daily and keeps a snapshot whenever the contents differ from the last one kept. September 2026 produced sixteen snapshots, from 3 to 30 September; the month's figures are one comparison between the last snapshot before the month (31 August) and the last one in it (30 September). Twenty-nine days were read and one read failed. Gross and net additions are equal this month (1,161); the nine Patchwork records appear as renames in the daily edits but not in the month's outcome, since they arrived and were renamed inside it.
