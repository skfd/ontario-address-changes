A 13th floor comes off 25 Wellington Street South, and 67 numbers line up on Blair Valley Street

In the City's one update of the month, seen on 11 October, 13 unit records left 25 Wellington Street South: 1301 to 1312 and 1314, the only 13th-floor numbers the tower had on file. What remains now matches a published count of the building exactly. The same update put 67 new numbers on Blair Valley Street in Doon South, about two thirds of the month's additions, on a road a sales listing still calls incomplete.

75 on a file of 132,000 is well under a tenth of a percent, and less than half of September's 189. Ward 4 took 67 of the 102 additions. Thirty daily reads, none failed, and only one of them found a changed file, so the month's edits and its outcome are the same numbers.

Blair Valley Street

Until this update the street held three unnumbered points. Now it has 67 new numbers, all Pending, all in Ward 4, on three City parcels: evens 10 to 50, odds 31 to 61, and a third run of odd numbers from 825 to 883. That third run reaches east from near 63 Pine Warbler Street to Stauffer Woods Trail. Number 10 sits 29 metres from a point on Blair Creek Drive.

A sales listing posted in early November offers freehold townhouses on the street "in Harvest Park at Doon South", which it calls the newest section of that neighbourhood. "Road is incomplete at this time," it adds, and it gives Jacob Detweiller Drive and Winter Wren Crescent as the closest finished intersection. The listing does not name the builder. This search found no plan of subdivision for the street, and nothing that explains why one run of numbers starts in the 800s.

The 13th floor at 25 Wellington Street South

Since the tracker's first read in June, the file had carried 1301 to 1314 at this address, with no 1313, all Active. All 13 records left together, and their City IDs are one unbroken run. Units 1201 to 1214 below and 1401 to 1414 above stayed.

That leaves 445 numbered units on 36 floors, numbered 1 to 37 with no 13. The condos.ca page for this part of Station Park, built by VanMar Developments, gives "36 floors/ 445 units". Before October the file held 458 units on 37 floors. The 18-floor building at 5 Wellington, on the same City parcel, has no 13xx numbers on file either.

Nothing public that this search found says the building skips a floor, or why the records left, and no listing turned up for a 13xx unit. The file shows only the two counts now agreeing.

Nine new records arrived at the address in the same update, labelled PKG, LKR and seven SRV, all Active. The tracker counts that as an address split, but none of the nine is numbered like an apartment. The file does not spell the labels out.

16 units at 60 Trussler Road

Units 1 to 16 appeared at 60 Trussler Road, all Pending, and the plain 60 moved 60 metres on the map. It now sits 51 metres from 70 Cora Drive. In 2021 the City gave notice of a vacant land condominium here "consisting of 5 residential units". Nothing this search found connects five units to sixteen, or says what has been built.

42 Windom Road leaves, 32 Windom Road shifts

42 Windom Road came off the file, while 32 Windom Road moved 25 metres and its Unit B moved 28. A 2023 planning report prepared for Jackman Construction proposes 58 stacked townhouse units across "32-42 Windom Road", reached by one consolidated driveway. The file does not say the update has anything to do with that proposal, and a retired address is not necessarily a building gone.

210 Heritage Drive starts going Active

Eighteen of the month's 20 Pending-to-Active changes are here: Units 1 to 16, A and B. Units 17 to 26 are still Pending. A rental listing describes Haravale Towns at this address as 26 stacked townhomes, built in 2026.

Twenty points shuffled near Rosenberg Way

The tracker logged 12 street renames this month, and none of them is a street being renamed. All 12 are unnumbered placeholder points re-filed under a different street name: seven went to Pinsent Crescent, two to Rosebush Street, and one each to Rosenberg Way, Sidewinder Street and Muirfield Crescent. Eight neighbours took a number and a new street at once, among them 100 to 115 Bandon Drive and 505 and 535 Rosenberg Way. All 20 lie within about 650 metres. Solitude Street, which gave up three points, keeps its numbered addresses from 133 to 149. Sheephorn and Sawbuck streets have no points left. No by-law or plan turned up for any of it, so the public record is silent.

Placeholders turned into numbers elsewhere too: 23 points on Newcastle Drive, near 43 Castlebay Street, became 186 to 222, all still Pending. They count as edits, not new addresses. Nine other placeholders left Eskerwood Way, Taverners Crescent and Mihai Eminescu Way, the three streets that took 122 numbers in September.

Smaller items

  • 631 Frederick Street gained Units 1 to 4 and 69 Nathalie Street gained A and B, all Pending, with both base addresses still on file. 397 King Street East arrived as a single Active address. The public record is silent on all three.
  • 75 and 111 Otterbein Road left the file. Both were Pending records that appeared in August, when older records with those numbers became 5 and 11. The file does not say why.

Next month, watch whether the Blair Valley numbers go Active and whether the last ten units at 210 Heritage Drive follow.

How this is measured — The tracker compares the City's address file between snapshots. This month's figures are one comparison, from the last snapshot before October (23 September) to the only one in it (11 October). The City's file changed once in that window, between the 10 and 11 October reads, and had not changed again by 6 November. A "retired" address is a record leaving the file, not necessarily a building coming down, and Pending and Active are the City's own status words, which the file does not define. Twenty-nine points also shifted on the map, most by only a few metres. Every address quoted here can be checked in the day-by-day reports.
