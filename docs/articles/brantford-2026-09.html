Eleven numbers fill in on three West Brant streets, and one of them says 26 and 36 at once

On 5 September 2026 the City of Brantford's address file gained eleven numbers on Turnbull Drive, Gillespie Drive and Wilmot Road, three neighbouring streets in West Brant that council dedicated as public highway in a single vote in August 2021. Every one of the eleven slots into a run of numbers already on file. One of them reads 26 in its street-number fields and 36 on its full-address label, and the file does not say which is right. Two more numbers arrived alone: 209 Campbell Street on the 1st and 486 King George Road on the 18th.

Thirteen on a file of thirty-nine thousand. August had looked busier, with 191 in and 186 out across the month, 162 of each on one day, and came to +5. September is the quieter month and the more legible one: thirteen additions, nothing removed, nothing edited.

Turnbull, Gillespie and Wilmot fill in

Before September, Turnbull Drive's even side ran 2 to 22 and then jumped to 34; the odd side ran 7 to 25 and then jumped to 33. On 5 September the file gained 24 and 28 on the even side and 27, 29 and 31 on the odd. Gillespie Drive had 135 and then 143; it gained 137, 139 and 141. Wilmot Road had 4 and then 10; it gained 6 and 8. All eleven points sit within 165 metres of one another, with 143 Gillespie 82 metres from 24 Turnbull and 24 Cheevers Road 99 metres away. These are numbers filling spaces in a sequence; the file does not record what stands on them.

The streets themselves have a paper trail. On 24 August 2021 Brantford City Council received Report 2021-398, "Road Dedication By-law, Gillespie Drive, Turnbull Drive and Wilmot Road", and passed by-laws 156-2021, 157-2021 and 158-2021 declaring Blocks 102, 101 and 103 of Registered Plan 2M-1928 to be public highway under those three names. The by-laws went through third reading with the rest of the night's list, ten votes to none (council minutes, 24 August 2021). The staff report behind the by-laws sits on the City's meeting portal and could not be read for this piece; nothing that could be read says why the streets carry those names.

One of the new numbers is already advertised. A rental listing for 8 Wilmot Road describes a "4 bedroom detached home by Empire Communities on a premium 42' lot backing onto greenspace", about 3,100 square feet, at $3,200 a month; on 22 October the listing had been up for 49 days (REW). Nothing was found for the other ten.

A point that says 26 and 36 at once

The eleventh West Brant row is the odd one. Its full-address label reads 36 TURNBULL DR, and the file already had a 36 Turnbull Drive, on record since the tracker's first read in June. But the new row's separate street-number fields read 26. Its dot on the map sits between 24 and 28 Turnbull, 13 and 15 metres from each, where a 26 would go; the older 36 is 88 metres away, eight metres from 34 and eight from 38, where a 36 would go.

So the City's file now carries two points labelled 36 Turnbull Drive, one of which describes itself as 26 everywhere except on the label. The file does not say which field is wrong, and this piece does not guess. The tracker shows the row as "36 TURNBULL DR" on the daily report, because it displays the label, so a reader checking the 5 September report will see 36 Turnbull Drive listed as new on a street that already had one.

The same eleven rows share a second small tell: their postal codes are written without the space, N3T0K4 where the rest of the file, August's 191 additions included, writes N3T 0K4.

Four acres on King George Road

486 King George Road appeared on 18 September, on the even side between 466 and 504, about 90 metres from 493. In the City's file it is the month's loneliest new address: the nearest address on any other street is 441A Powerline Road, 891 metres away. The file carries only the number and a postal code.

A listing for the parcel describes about four acres, roughly 180 feet of frontage by 900 deep, designated Core Natural and zoned "N - Core Natural", with residential development "not permitted under the current zoning" and the land marketed as a recreational retreat or land investment, "not a residential building lot"; the page showed the listing as sold and last updated on 1 September 2026 (Squareyards). No planning or permit item for the address turned up. Why a parcel its own listing calls "not a residential building lot" gained a civic number this month is not something the file or the record explains.

209 Campbell Street, and what to watch

209 Campbell Street arrived on 1 September, 27 metres past 201 on a stretch where the odd numbers had run 191 to 201 and then stopped; 203, 205 and 207 are not on file, and no even number between 190 and 230 is either. Nothing public was found for the address. The record is silent.

Next month: whether the row labelled 36 Turnbull is corrected to 26, or the older 36 changes instead; whether 26, 30 and 32 Turnbull appear; and whether 486 King George Road stays on file.

How this is measured — The tracker reads the City of Brantford's published address layer daily and keeps a snapshot whenever the contents differ from the last one kept. September 2026 produced three (1, 5 and 18 September); the month's figures are one comparison between the last snapshot before the month (31 August) and the last one in it (18 September), so anything the City published after the 18th belongs to October. Gross and net are equal this month: nothing was added and then removed, and nothing was edited. Brantford publishes no stable row id, so the tracker tells points apart by number, street, unit and position; that is why the two rows labelled 36 Turnbull Drive count as two.
