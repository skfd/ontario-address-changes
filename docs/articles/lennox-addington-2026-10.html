Sixty numbers on two new Napanee streets, Bash Avenue and Bristol Crescent

Between 21 and 24 October, two streets that had never been in the County of Lennox and Addington's address file arrived in Greater Napanee with sixty numbers between them: 39 on Bash Avenue and 21 on Bristol Crescent. They sit side by side, off Old Hamburg Road and Oke Road.

By the numbers
New addresses 82
Retired 5 (three of them still on file, moved a few metres)
Renumbered 0
Street renames 0
Address splits 4
Total on file at month end 26,249

The file grew by 77 addresses, about one in 340 of the 26,172 it held at the end of September, against 12 the month before. Eighty of the 82 new records are in Greater Napanee, and the other two are in Stone Mills.

Bash Avenue and Bristol Crescent

The first number to appear was 1 Bash Avenue, on 21 October. The next day brought 34 more, and the 23rd four more, so the street now has 39 numbers from 1 to 56 on both sides. On 24 October Bristol Crescent followed with 21 odd numbers, 1 to 41, all at once. The two streets are one patch of ground: 1 Bristol Crescent is 26 metres from 56 Bash Avenue. The nearest older addresses are 63 Old Hamburg Road, 80 metres from 1 Bash Avenue, then 29 Champlain Street and 16 Oke Road, about 210 metres away.

Bristol is one of the names on the Town of Greater Napanee's approved street-name inventory of February 2025. The inventory gives the reason: Edmund James Bristol was born in Napanee in 1861 and was elected MP for a Toronto riding in 1905, then re-elected five times. The list first went out for comment that February, and the Town describes it as names approved and waiting to be given to new streets. It names no location for Bristol.

Bash is not on that list, and no by-law or notice naming Bash Avenue turned up. It is also the name of BASH Developments, whose Springside Meadows community opened with a ribbon-cutting in January, described as "a new master-planned residential community in the east end of Napanee". The same inventory holds the name Dickson for the "Springside Meadows Subdivision", and no Dickson street is in the file yet. No public source we found names the roads in Springside Meadows or says whether these two streets are part of it, so the record is silent on which development they belong to.

One quirk of the arrival: on 21 October the file briefly carried a point with only the number 3 and no street, at the spot where 3 Bash Avenue appeared the next day. And the 34 records added on the 22nd came without their address label, which was filled in on the 23rd. The tracker's daily totals count that as 34 renumberings, but no number changed.

Camden Road and Heritage Park Drive

On 27 October, 276 Camden Road arrived together with 276A, 276B and 276C. No plain 276 had been on file before. Next door, 278 Camden Road gained 278A, 278B and 278C the same day. The original 278 is still there: it was moved about four metres. Because this layer has no record IDs, the tracker counts a moved point as one retirement and one addition, which is why 278 appears among the month's retirements. The nearest neighbours are on Church Street, Dairy Avenue and Queen Street.

On 24 October, 164 and 168 Heritage Park Drive each gained an A and a B. Both originals have been on file since the tracker began in June and are still there.

No listing, permit or planning item for any of these six addresses turned up, so the public record is silent on what the lettered numbers are for.

The rest of the month

On Newburgh Road, 270, 272 and 274 appeared on 26 October beside 268, whose point moved about seven metres that day, and 235 followed on the 28th. Single new numbers arrived at 331 Stone Hedge Road (7 October), 1493 Roblin Road (26 October) and 3250 County Road 9 (29 October). In Stone Mills, 66 South Shore Drive appeared on the 29th, while 74 South Shore Drive moved about 43 metres west.

Only two records actually left the file. One was a placeholder point labelled "Unassigned Nugent Road" in Stone Mills, gone after 7 October. The other was a Stone Mills point that carried only the number 1653 and never a street name; it was given an address label on 9 October and was gone by the 14th. The file does not say why either was removed.

How this is measured: The tracker reads the County of Lennox and Addington's published civic-address layer every day and keeps a snapshot whenever the contents differ from the last one kept. October 2026 produced eleven snapshots, from 7 to 29 October. The month's figures compare the 29 October snapshot with the last one before the month (25 September), so changes after 29 October belong to November. The source has no stable record ID, so the tracker identifies each address by its text and point. A renumbered or moved address therefore counts as one retirement plus one addition. Three of this month's five retirements are moved points of that kind. The daily changes add up to 84 additions and 7 retirements, two more of each than the month's net, because two short-lived records (the bare "3" and the labelled 1653) came and went inside the month.
